Earlier fixture assertions, active-Anvil capture assumptions, native scratch
cleanup expectations and build/GameTest warnings remain in original archives.
They are corrected or explicitly delimited in their reports, not erased by a
clean final summary. See [TEST-REPORT](TEST-REPORT.md).

Downgrade requires a complete matched pre-upgrade backup. Older strict schema-2
readers can reject the added optional fields, even though both describe their
definitions as schema 2. Repeated claims cannot repair full/unwritable/future
stores. Refer to [migration procedures](MIGRATION-REPORT.md).

Original-content parity is still incomplete in the [porting matrix](../../PORTING_MATRIX.md).
v1.4 development completion does not trigger the deferred full campaign or
authorize v1.5 production without its separate development-order decision.
