## 2. Architecture and persistence key

`K` = actual v1.2 process/lifecycle framework, [ADR-016](../../decisions/ADR-016-MACHINE-MULTIBLOCK-KERNEL.md)
and [ADR-019](../../decisions/ADR-019-PRECISION-RESOURCE-OWNERSHIP.md).
`KC` = classic family under [ADR-061 §3](../../decisions/ADR-061-CLASSIC-CONTENT-IDENTITY-IMPORT-AND-VALIDATION.md)
and [ADR-064 §1](../../decisions/ADR-064-CLASSIC-MACHINES-FLUIDS-AND-COMPONENTS.md).
It uses [ClassicControllerBlockEntity](../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/machine/classic/runtime/ClassicControllerBlockEntity.java) (bounded lifecycle and `ProcessMachineLogic.tick`),
and [ClassicBatch](../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/machine/classic/runtime/ClassicBatch.java) (`ProcessDefinition`, simulation and
`ProcessTransactionExecutor.commit` on a detached store before publishing one checkpoint).
It is not the unregistered `machine/classic/adapter` controller.
`KR` uses that physical kernel but intentionally starts no recipe:
[ResearchControllerBlockEntity](../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/machine/classic/runtime/ResearchControllerBlockEntity.java) delegates jobs/credits to the registry under
[ADR-066 §6.1](../../decisions/ADR-066-CLASSIC-LIFE-SUPPORT-EQUIPMENT-RESEARCH-AND-PRESENTATION.md).

rather than claiming v1.2 processing. This table records the decision that permits that
design; it is **not** a blanket JEI/test waiver, and the press's piston-protection
exception must not be mistaken for a universal framework exception.

### 7.2 Exact limits of that proof

- It covers all packaged **recipe files/types**, all registered obtainable items and the
  declared 17-goal classic tree in the default enabled survival model. It is not limited
  to a handful of example recipes.
- Recipe input quantities/time, gravity constraints, simultaneous FE throughput and
  probabilistic waiting time are not simulated; reachability is existential, not
  resource-conservation, balance or a demonstration of playability.
- `PackagedRecipeGraph.CLASSIC_MACHINES` enumerates nine formed processing multiblocks.
  It does not enumerate operational nodes for **all 52 devices**. In particular, obtaining
  an observatory/processor item is not proof that the survey → data-unit → paid analysis
  route works; satellite research is modelled as a repeatable `research:lifetime` fact.
  There is no separate exhaustive graph of all ground-survey jobs, all satellite-kind
  research thresholds and endgame/solar/beacon operational preconditions (F13).
  Alternate satellite routes may make items reachable without testing that ground route.
- There is no SCC report over all operations or arbitrary external datapacks. Within the
  declared graph, the fixed-point required-node assertion already catches hard cycles;
  absence of a separate SCC algorithm is not itself a failure. Custom packs, disabled
  device combinations beyond the explicit Tau Ceti case, permissions, restart ordering and
  multiplayer liveness need their dedicated tests/campaign, not a broader interpretation
  of this unit result.
- Test source writes `classic-advancement-reachability.md`,
  `registered-item-acquisition.md` and their without-Tau-Ceti variants beneath
  `build/reports/`. That mutable location is **not durable evidence**. No such reports
  are present in the inspected build-evidence ZIP. A committed/exported C5 witness report
  and index receipt remain unlocated in the consulted evidence (F14).
