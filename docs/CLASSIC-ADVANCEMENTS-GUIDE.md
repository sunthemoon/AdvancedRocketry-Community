# Classic advancements

The **Advanced Rocketry** advancement tab follows the classic mod's tutorial
path. Advancements are feedback only: they give no items, experience or recipes,
and you can earn any of them in any order. Each one is earned on the server when
the thing it describes really happens.

Every advancement below can be earned in a new survival world, starting in the
Overworld with vanilla items. You need no commands and no other mods. Four
advancements cannot be earned in this build yet; they are listed at the end.

## Machines and materials

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Start with a Crafting Table | Have a crafting table in your inventory. |
| Acquire a Small Plate Press | Craft one from a piston above three iron ingots. To use it, place it facing down with obsidian two blocks below it, put an iron block in between and give the press a redstone pulse: the block turns into four iron plates. |
| Acquire a Rolling Machine | Craft a rolling machine. It needs machine casings and circuits; circuits start with silicon wafers made from quartz and redstone. |
| Acquire an Electrolyzer | Craft an electrolyzer. Powered with FE and supplied with water, it splits water into hydrogen and oxygen canisters. |
| Feel the Heat | Craft an electric arc furnace from blast bricks, iron plates, a machine casing, redstone and copper. |
| Spin Doctor | Craft a lathe. Its motor needs steel plates, so you first need a working arc furnace (for steel) and rolling machine (for plates). |
| More Than Its Parts | Be within 16 blocks of a multiblock machine's controller when its structure forms. The machines are the rolling machine, the precision assembler, the lathe, the electric arc furnace and the cutting machine. A structure forms when its last missing block is placed. Rebuilding a broken structure counts again for players who did not have it yet. |
| Collect a Space Suit | Have the space suit helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots in your inventory at the same time. |
| Acquire a Warp Core | Craft a warp core from a machine casing, an advanced circuit and a data storage unit. |

Power for the machines comes from a combustion generator (burns ordinary fuel)
or a solar generator.

## Rockets, the Moon and stations

| Advancement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Hold My Beer | Right-click TNT while holding a rocket seat. Nothing explodes; the seat is placed on the TNT as any block would be. |
| Went to the Moon | Arrive on the surface of the Moon. |
| Moon Landing | Ride a rocket until it touches down on the Moon. Only players in the rocket's seats when the descent ends earn it; watching from the ground does not count. |
| Giving It All She's Got | Be inside a space station you may visit (its owner, a member or an operator) when the station completes a warp. |

**Flying to the Moon.** Build a rocket from a rocket motor, a fuel tank, a
rocket seat and a guidance computer, and assemble it with a rocket assembler.
Fuel it at a fuel loader with rocket fuel cells; fuel cells are made from
hydrogen canisters, which the electrolyzer produces. Sit in the seat and launch
to the Moon from the rocket's flight console.

**Warping a station.** Use a station deployment kit to create a station in
Space, then fly a rocket to it. Place warp cores inside the station and charge them with
FE: an in-system warp costs 2,000,000 FE and an interstellar warp 8,000,000 FE.
While looking at a warp core in your station, the owner runs
`/arce station warp <destination>`, checks the quote, and confirms with
`/arce station warp confirm <station>`. After a ten-second countdown the station
moves; everyone in the station who may visit it earns the advancement.

## Not yet available in this build

| Advancement | Why |
|---|---|
| Crystalline | The crystallizer is not in this build yet. |
| Dilithium | Dilithium crystals come from the crystallizer. Dilithium ore and dust exist, but nothing produces the crystal yet. |
| One Small Step | Goes to the players aboard the first rocket ever to land on the Moon in the world. It needs a permanent world record of that first landing, which is not in this build yet. |
| Flight of the Phoenix | Goes to the players aboard the first station ever to warp in the world. It needs a permanent world record of that first warp, which is not in this build yet. |

These four appear in the tab but cannot be earned until a later build adds them.
When the two world-first advancements arrive, a landing or warp made before that
build will not count retroactively.
