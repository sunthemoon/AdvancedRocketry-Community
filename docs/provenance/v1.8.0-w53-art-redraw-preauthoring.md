## Why the five files are redrawn

The Repository governance run 38556570618 at `fc194a8e` (step 11) ran
`tools/audit/screen_generated_art.py` over `src/generated/v1.8/resources`
with both pinned client JARs. It flagged five NEW textures out of 171. A
local rerun at `6bf14169` over all 187 current PNG files gives the same five
and nothing else:

| Verdict | Texture | Closest vanilla texture | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIT | `textures/block/station_light.png` | 1.20.1 `block/glow_item_frame.png` | rank 0.9402, same size |
| SUSPECT | `textures/block/force_field_projector_side.png` | 1.12.2 `particle/footprint.png` | rank 0.8043, at half scale |
| SUSPECT | `textures/block/solar_array_side.png` | 1.20.1 `block/barrel_bottom.png` | rank 0.7793, rotated |
| SUSPECT | `textures/block/solar_generator_side.png` | 1.20.1 `block/respawn_anchor_top_off.png` | rank 0.7761, same size |
| SUSPECT | `textures/item/carbon_brick.png` | 1.20.1 `item/heart_of_the_sea.png` | rank 0.7674 over an outline IoU of 0.9211 |

No flagged file shares a pixel colour with its match: each verdict comes
from the whole-image luminance rank correlation alone. Each of the five
drawings is a nested frame whose brightness steps evenly from the edge to
the centre, and that layout correlates with many vanilla textures. Under
ADR-066 section 7.3 and ADR-061 section 4.8, a flagged NEW file is not
appealed or reclassified because its author drew it. It is redrawn.

## How the replacements are drawn

- Each texture is redrawn in the provider that already owns it:
  `V180StationLightData`, `V180ForceFieldData`, `V180SolarArrayData`,
  `V180SolarData` and `V180CraftingComponentData`. Each is a new 16 x 16
  palette grid, encoded by the unchanged `V180MaterialArt.png` encoder with
  the texture's existing tint. The result is deterministic and code-drawn.
- No vanilla, upstream, LibVulpes or third-party image is opened, viewed,
  traced, sampled or used as a drawing input.
- A development probe outside the repository calls the unchanged screening
  tool's own functions on candidate grids and prints only the measures. It
  reads the vanilla JARs to compare, exactly as the screen does.
- Each texture keeps its path, size, model bindings and role. The block
  faces stay fully opaque, and the carbon brick keeps its transparent
  margin. The station light keeps a pale lit centre in a metal housing,
  and the other faces keep their machine-casing reading.
- The new layouts replace the evenly nested frames with asymmetric,
  feature-based arrangements.

## Acceptance and records

The whole `src/generated/v1.8/resources` root must screen with zero HIT and
zero SUSPECT, using the unchanged tool, thresholds and inputs and both
pinned clients. If a redraw stays near a vanilla texture, the design changes,
not the screen. The earlier provenance records keep their text, entries and
hashes. The four markdown records of the block faces each gain a dated W53
section. The carbon brick's pre-authoring declaration
(`v1.8.0-c16d-acquisition-preauthoring.md`) stays byte-unchanged, because
`ACQUISITION-ADOPTION-01.md` pins its SHA-256. Its schema-2 record
`v1.8.0-c16d-acquisition.json` keeps the old texture entry and gains the new
one. The new hashes and the screen result are added after authoring.
