## What will be authored

All of the following are NEW, written for this project:

- Code: `FluidFuelLoaderBlock`, `FluidFuelLoaderBlockEntity`, its Fluid port,
  `FluidFuelLoaderMenu`, the client-only screen, `FluidFuelLoaderItem` (the
  nonstackable drop carrier), `LinkerItem` (the rocket-binding half only), the
  empty-pressurized-tank recipe ingredient and the data provider
  `V180FluidFuelLoaderData`.
- Text: English and Chinese names of `fluid_fuel_loader` and `linker`, the
  twenty `FluidFuelLoaderStatus` messages, and the menu and linker feedback.
  The text goes into the shared v1.8 language files as new keys.

`V180FluidFuelLoaderData` will write the following GENERATED output under
`src/generated/v1.8/resources`, deterministically from its own source:

- 16 x 16 textures drawn in code with the existing `V180MaterialArt` encoder:
  the loader's tank side, its port top and its base, and the linker icon;
- the loader's blockstate, block model and item model, and the linker's item
  model, which reference vanilla parent models by resource ID only;
- the loader's loot table, which drops one loader item;
- two new community recipes and their recipe-unlock advancements: the loader
  from an empty `pressurized_tank`, a `liquid_io_board`, a `control_board` and
  a `machine_casing`; the linker from a `tracking_circuit`,
  `minecraft:iron_ingot` and `minecraft:redstone`.

## What is not used

No upstream Advanced Rocketry file, no LibVulpes file, and no Minecraft, Mojang,
Microsoft, Forge or third-party image, model or text is copied, traced, sampled
or opened as an authoring input. The ledger rows `block:fuelingStation` and
`libvulpes:java/itemLinker` name the behaviour to deliver; they are not
permission to import. The two recipes are new community recipes, as section 4
of the design proposes, and claim no legacy recipe parity.

## After authoring

The JSON record `v1.8.0-c17a-fluid-fuel-loader.json` will list each generated
target with its SHA-256, the generator source and its SHA-256, and the
authoring commit. The new textures will be screened with
`tools/audit/screen_generated_art.py` over `src/generated/v1.8/resources`
against both pinned vanilla clients, as W53 did; every new texture must be
CLEAR, and a flagged texture is redrawn, not appealed.
