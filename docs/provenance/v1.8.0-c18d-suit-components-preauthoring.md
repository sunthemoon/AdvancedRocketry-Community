## Legacy files stay where they are

The legacy `models/jetpack.obj` and the other OBJ/MTL models are planned imports
of their owning units (`docs/work/v1.8.0-asset-plan.csv` rows 266 to 271) and
have not been imported. This task does not open, copy, trace or transform them,
nor any legacy, LibVulpes, Minecraft, Mojang, Microsoft, Forge or third-party
texture or model.

## New community-authored work

The worn jetpack, pressure tanks and visor plate are drawn by NEW MIT code: a
few axis-aligned boxes defined in the renderer source (no model file), textured
by one NEW original 32 x 32 RGBA texture drawn pixel by pixel in a DataGen
provider with the project's existing deterministic PNG encoding. No bitmap is
read as input. Colour per gas or visor is applied as a vertex tint at render
time, so the texture itself is neutral metal and glass.

Planned generated target:
`src/generated/v1.8/resources/assets/advancedrocketrycommunity/textures/entity/suit_components.png`.

Planned checks before delivery: the existing generated-art screen
(`tools/audit/screen_generated_art.py`) against the vanilla client textures, a
unit test that the committed PNG equals its generator, and real-GPU V1 and
two-client V2 visual evidence, which this task cannot produce.
