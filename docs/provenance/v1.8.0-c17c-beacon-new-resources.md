## Independent origin

The stable content IDs are `advancedrocketrycommunity:planetary_beacon` (block, item and
block entity) and `advancedrocketrycommunity:beacon_finder` (item) under ADR-065 section 2.
Their four 16 x 16 RGBA images are drawn by code in `datagen/V180BeaconData.java`:

- the controller front, a framed screen with a target ring and centre;
- the controller side, a panel with vertical ribs;
- the controller top, a plate with a central emitter;
- the finder item, a hand-held unit with a screen and an arrow, on a transparent background.

The grids are new. They reuse only the repository's existing `V180MaterialArt.png` encoder.
No upstream, LibVulpes, Minecraft/Mojang/Microsoft/Forge or third-party image is read,
traced, sampled, cropped, recoloured or imported. The legacy `beaconFinder` texture is
missing upstream and stays excluded.

## Models, data and language

- **Models.** The controller model references `minecraft:block/orientable` by identifier
  only and uses the new community textures; its blockstate rotates it for the four
  horizontal facings. The finder item model references `minecraft:item/generated` by
  identifier only.
- **Pattern.** `machine_patterns/planetary_beacon.json` is new modern JSON for the v1.2
  pattern kernel. It records the legacy layout fact pinned in ADR-065 section 1 (a
  5-by-3-by-3 structure with a redstone-block cap and a structure column and base) with
  the project's own `machine_casing` in place of the LibVulpes structure block, and
  references `minecraft:redstone_block` by identifier only. No legacy source text is
  copied.
- **Data.** The blockstate, the self-drop loot table, the two shaped recipes and their
  recipe-unlock advancements are new modern JSON. The recipes use steel or aluminium
  plates, `user_interface`, `control_board` and one circuit part each. They make no claim
  of legacy parity.
- **Language.** The English and Chinese names, the tooltip, the HUD strings and the
  beacon status messages are new.

Paths are written by `runData` into `src/generated/v1.8/resources`.
