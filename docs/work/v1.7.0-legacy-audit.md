## 6. Legacy safety defects that the redesign must not reproduce

1. Client packets set laser targets and the running state, packet 13 indexes
   `MODE.values()` with an unchecked client integer (a server-side exception),
   and elevator buttons dereference a null capsule on the server (§1, §5).
2. Railguns and active laser drills force-load chunks permanently or at
   client-supplied coordinates; laser and elevator code initialises whole
   dimensions on demand (§1, §2, §5).
3. Cross-dimension cargo moves in one tick between two independently saved
   chunks, so a crash between the two saves duplicates or loses the stack (§2).
4. The laser drill keeps only the last block's drops per layer and discards
   overflow when jammed (§1).
5. Laser cleanup mutates the world from a new thread (§1).
6. The area gravity controller scans and pushes every entity in a 65-block cube
   every tick, and resets fall distance (§4).
7. No device checks who is operating it (§1–§5).
8. Rewards and drops depend on an unseeded `Random` (§1).

## 7. Current-repository facts relevant to the contracts

- No endgame block, item, placeholder, registry entry or research unlock exists
  in v1.6, so there is nothing to map or migrate.
- Station gravity is per station, set by `/arce station gravity` through a
  checked commit with a per-station cooldown and the server-wide write spacing
  (ADR-041). `CelestialGravityController` applies a position override to players
  through Forge's synchronized `ENTITY_GRAVITY` attribute.
- The celestial schema (2) has no star or black-hole flag; star systems are root
  trees (ADR-043) and the Tau Ceti star is not orbitable.
- Energy is Forge Energy; machines keep their own buffers. The kernel's process
  journal covers items and fluids only.
- Cross-store item delivery already exists for resource missions: a SavedData
  ledger with a save epoch, block-entity receipts with the chunk-save persistence
  signal, and a total reconciliation table (ADR-050 §2, ADR-051 §5–§11).
- ADR-045 provides the elevator endpoint validator and the constraints the v1.7
  elevator must satisfy or supersede.
- Network channels: life support 1, celestial 3, rocket flight 8, rocket visual
  1, satellite 1. The public API is at version 1.7.

## 8. Assets

The legacy laser, railgun, black-hole, gravity and elevator models, textures and
sounds (for example the orbital-laser and elevator OBJ models, the
`laserDrill` and `gravityOhhh` sounds) are MIT upstream assets listed in
`legacy-manifest/assets.csv`. **None is imported by this preparation.** Any later
import must first enter the provenance ledger (`docs/08`, `docs/provenance/`);
otherwise v1.7 content uses community-authored assets.

`tile/TileForceFieldProjector` was fetched to confirm its scope: it extends
force-field blocks up to 32 blocks when powered. It is classic content, not an
endgame system, and stays with the v1.8 matrix audit.
