## Root's design choices (under the owner's delegation of design points)

- **Machine fronts** show what the machine does, in the project's casing palette: the rolling machine a pair of
  rollers with a sheet between them, the precision assembler a fine assembly grid or arm. Not vanilla blocks.
- **Ports**: one frame per port, with its kind shown by an icon and colour consistent with the v1.8 hatches already
  in use (`textures/block/item_input_hatch.png` and its siblings: a dark panel with an arrow). Input and output differ
  by arrow direction; energy shows a plug or bolt, not a redstone block; fluid a droplet. Reuse the hatches' style
  rather than inventing a second one.
- **Sides**: the v1.2 machines and their ports get a NEW plain panelled side texture with no drawer handles.
  `machine_casing_side.png` itself belongs to v0.1.0's approved baseline
  (`docs/provenance/v0.1.0-minimal-content.json`, pinned by `scripts/validate_v010_asset_baseline.py`): do not change
  that file. List in your record every other block that shows it, so Root can decide on a baseline correction later.
- **Gas containers**: the three canisters and the four pressure tanks become metal gas cylinders with a valve on
  top. Oxygen and hydrogen get distinct colour bands and the empty canister none; the pressure tiers differ by band
  count or shade. Vanilla Minecraft has no oxygen tank, and AR 1.12's `pressuretank0` to `pressuretank3` are excluded
  (G2, authorship not cleared): draw NEW art, copying neither.
