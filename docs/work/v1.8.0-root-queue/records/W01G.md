## Choices where the task or ADR left room

- **Clearance tag contents.**
  - The tag holds `minecraft:air`, `cave_air` and `void_air`, listed explicitly because
    1.20.1 has no vanilla air tag.
  - It also holds the snow layer, the six torch and wall-torch variants (normal, soul and
    redstone) and redstone wire, plus the vanilla tags `#minecraft:rails`,
    `#minecraft:wool_carpets`, `#minecraft:buttons` and `#minecraft:pressure_plates`.
  - The remaining entries are the moss carpet, vine, glow lichen, ladder, lever, tripwire
    and tripwire hook.
  - It has `replace: false`, so data packs can extend it.
  - It leaves out anything with a block entity, solid blocks, fire, scaffolding,
    repeaters and comparators.
  - Root can trim the "similar vanilla blocks" part: buttons, pressure plates, lever,
    tripwire, tripwire hook and ladder.
- **ADR-064 section 8.1 note.** No section 8 implementation note existed. A labelled
  one-line note was added under item 1. No decision text changed.
- **N3, direct successor.** A part exactly one generation ahead is not reconciled: the
  formation transaction already adopts it as the candidate.
  - This keeps the W01F one-generation results, and their exact-generation assertions,
    unchanged.
  - Parts two or more generations ahead return to the restored controller's generation,
    and the machine forms there. Later re-formations reuse the lost generation numbers.
    That is harmless, because formation needs every candidate part loaded and parts own
    no Item or Fluid.
- **N1, quarantine documentation.** AGENTS.md section 3.3 asks for the impact of a
  mechanism that isolates player data. The R-021 extension and the ADR-064 note now state:
  - the scope: that controller only, with no ticking, no hatch capabilities and no
    ordinary removal of it or its hatches;
  - the duration: until an offline repair, so restarts do not clear it;
  - the log volume: none, while the controller reports REPAIR;
  - the recovery: the existing R-021 procedure.

  The text also says that the fresh path discards nothing.

## Known risks

- A quarantined partial controller writes no log line. An operator finds it only through
  the REPAIR reason in its menu, the same as the existing future-schema quarantine.
- A controller snapshot that never formed (generation 0) whose parts are bound at
  generation 2 or later still conflicts. A part binding cannot have generation 0, so
  there is nothing to reconcile to. The parts must be rebuilt.
- If a data pack adds a classic hatch to `machine_clearance`, a hatch in a clearance cell
  would match and could be claimed as an optional part. The generated tag contains no
  block-entity block.
