## Design, changed files, and sibling review

All changed Java files are in `src/adapterTest/java/io/github/sunthemoon/arceadaptertest/`.

- `AdapterWorldFixture.java` adds a bounded seven-block-cube glass shell inside
  the existing 16-cube template. It completes the shell before clearing its
  five-block-cube interior, with an air gap around every rocket member. A per-test
  terminal listener restores outside block states and any captured BlockEntity NBT
  on pass or failure, including timeout. Setup and restore suppress neighbor/shape
  updates, following W301F's containment approach. No global fixture collection is used.
- `AdapterDropFixture.java` prepares that enclosure and retires the verified
  command-block-backed start button without a drop, remembering its original state.
  The control-water channel now has roofs over both source and target. Its source,
  nine surrounding stone cells, and original button are restored at termination.
  Tick-8 checks additionally verify the enclosure and channel walls. The existing
  water-arrival and zero-drop assertions are unchanged.
- `RocketComponentGameTests.java`, `AdapterRegistrationGameTests.java`, and
  `AdapterFaultGameTests.java` use the common preparation. All three have source
  cells that remain air during asynchronous disassembly or recovery, so all share
  the ambient-fluid exposure. The fault class's W273 water regression also used
  the same uncapped, unrestored channel; it receives the containment fix too.
- `AdapterAsyncFixture.java` latches the first unexpected admission result rather
  than resubmitting after its assertion. A `target_occupied` diagnosis names every
  absolute restore coordinate, block state, and fluid state, without loading an
  unloaded chunk. `target_occupied` remains a failure, never an accepted refusal.

The other command-based adapter assembly fixture, `RocketFuelGameTests`, does not
disassemble or retry publication into emptied cells; its short fuel check has no
equivalent restore-target interval. Registration-event-only tests likewise do not
exercise that interval. They were not changed. All original drop regions, item
coverage, emptiness requirements, operation budgets, and test timeouts are retained.
No tests were removed and no new test identity was needed; existing water regressions
gain containment assertions, and teardown checks verify restored outside cells.
