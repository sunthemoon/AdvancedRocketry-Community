player, moving that player within the normal interaction distance before
each request. It no longer manufactures a flight plan,
debit, destination snapshot or permissive preparation callback. The product
performs request/replay registration, planning, landing selection and the

chunks, and the destination observations cover the eight product Moon pads.

ticks continue. Setup and completion additionally require sixteen active
atmosphere providers, not just sixteen ACTIVE enums. Refills still use only
normal inventory and energy interfaces. No Level clock, ticker or product

The runner requires all eight such observations, instead of requiring eight
optional callback notifications from a two-slot callback queue. The original
callback checkpoint counters remain in the evidence. Tests reject seven
