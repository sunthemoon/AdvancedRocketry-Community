# W319: Deleted-station landing pads

## Scope and design (written before implementation)

This task owns `StationManager`, `LandingPadIndex`, their focused tests, the two named escrow GameTests,
the corresponding expected-log counts, and this record. It does not change pad or landing selection rules,
station creation, the ledger, status files, build configuration, or QUEUE. No upstream files or assets are imported.

Removal will run on the server thread in the existing confirmed station-deletion operation, after all deletion
guards and the registry deletion succeed, but before the registry's existing flush. It removes only that station
UUID's index bucket (at most eight entries), marks the index dirty, and forgets transient choices targeting that
UUID (at most 128 choices). Other stations, claims, choices, and the monotonic epoch counter remain unchanged.
The existing flush saves dirty overworld SavedData, including the index. This is not a multi-file crash transaction;
a crash or disk failure can still leave the old station/index files out of step. No new durability promise is made.
Rejected deletion must leave the index and choices unchanged. Physical pad blocks outside the station's removable
template are not scanned or removed; their obsolete binding cannot identify a new station that reuses the cell.

Old schema-1 saves retain their existing entries on load. The index loader has no authoritative station registry,
and independent SavedData loading, unavailable registries, or torn saves do not establish safe deletion authority.
There is therefore no load-time pruning or registry/world scan. Existing orphan entries may still require operator
repair; this change prevents new ones during successful deletion. An unavailable/malformed index continues to
preserve its original root without normalization, including during station deletion. No save-refusal mechanism
or new data-discard policy is introduced. The index schema stays at 1; fields, limits, IDs, and epoch semantics
do not change. Root arranges the required independent read-only review before merge.

## Escrow cleanup finding

The 20 entries in W318 are a cleanup gap, not expected durable data. The ten live-return scenarios each create two
synthetic entries under their transfer UUID, not a station UUID. They are needed until the F8 assertions prove that
only the exact transfer's claim was released, but should then be removed by fixture cleanup. Their own `finish`
now removes both entries and asserts the bucket is empty, after the existing claim assertions.

`EscrowGovernedSourceFlightGameTests.finish` already removed both its synthetic entries in this checkout. It now
also asserts that its bucket is empty; no source-flight cleanup behavior was changed. The final full run saved an
empty pad index, zero stations, and zero reservations. Its index is still schema 1, with `next_epoch=71`.
