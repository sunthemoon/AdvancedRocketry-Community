- For each step I read its `original` text and the row in `CAMPAIGN-CHECKLISTS-*.md`. The text after the
  separator is the oracle. For J18 step 8 I also read item 6 of the D1 outcome freeze, which says that ordinary
  compasses use the Level's native shared spawn "including border fallback".
- I read the machine readbacks of the step from the session `results.jsonl`, and the request file where the
  order of actions mattered, and say in each judgment whether they agree with the frames. No readback was rescored.
- I viewed 10 of the round's sheet pages for an overview and cite 91 distinct native frames. A cited frame was
  opened as the native file, with two exceptions that were read as unscaled or enlarged crops of the native file:
  four of I09's six heap frames (the top strip, unscaled) and five of J18's compass frames (the hotbar and the
  held item, enlarged). The crops were made by `J/tools/cropzoom.ps1` and `J/tools/montage.ps1`.

  frames made by `J/tools/montage_list.ps1`, and sampled natively. The tiles are reduced copies, at 0.35 to 0.62
  of native size, so they served for the overview and the cited frames for text. The 43 viewing aids are kept in
  `J/tools/aids/`, with the frame lists they were built from.
- There is no `VISUAL_FAIL` verdict. No verdict rests on a file name, a caption or a log line alone; two frames
  whose names promise something the frame does not show are recorded as such (J18 step 8, I04 step 5).
- I kept W482's rule for oracles with a part that cannot appear on screen (it is an interpretation, not part of
  the TASK): the verdict is `VISUAL_PASS` when the visible part is shown and an agreeing readback covers the
  rest, and `STILL_PENDING` when the oracle names something visible that no frame shows. Each such judgment says
  "Visual half only".
- None of the 20 steps asks for sound in its text, so no verdict has a sound half.
- For each step with an earlier verdict, the judgment says whether round 07 changes it. The earlier verdict is
  the latest of [W482](W482.md) (rounds 04 and 05) and [W490](W490.md) (round 06); [W485](W485.md) kept every
  W482 pass among these rows. For A14 step 1 it is Root's `STILL_PENDING` of R704, which replaced W482's proposed
