### Exact leak and index audit

The defect was not the live allocator failing to delete a station. The new F8 tests replaced
the `DimensionDataStorage` entry with a freshly decoded `StationRegistrySavedData`, then deleted
their fixture only from that replacement. The original `Fixture.data` still held its committed
station and cell claim. Three startup-bound consumers retained that original object:

- [AtmosphereAnalyzerLifecycle](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/atmosphere/instrument/AtmosphereAnalyzerLifecycle.java)
  captures `stations::findAt` in its registered analyzer's `StationRegionBodyContextResolver`.
- [ServerEnvironmentQueries](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/compat/environment/ServerEnvironmentQueries.java)
  retains both the registry and a resolver using its `findAt` method.
- [SolarExposure](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/machine/solar/SolarExposure.java)
  retains the registry in its server-lifetime binding.

The analyzer test builds its local reader against the current data, but `nativeUse` also asserts
the result of the registered `AtmosphereAnalyzerRuntime.read`. The cleaned replacement could
allocate the freed cell again while that registered reader still resolved the original fixture
through the old registry. Deleting the station in both objects without restoring their common
identity would still leave the old reader unable to see subsequent commits.

| State or index examined | Ownership and cleanup |
|---|---|
| `stations`, `reservations`, `occupiedCells`, `warpEnergy` | All belong to `StationRegistryModel`. Reservation and allocation share `occupiedCells`; `findAt` uses it to find committed entries. `delete` removes the committed station, its cell claim and balance; `release` removes a reservation's claim. There is no independent allocator cursor to reset. |
| `StationAccessIndex.teams`, `byPlayer`, link count and generation | Belong to the same model. `delete` removes owner/member reverse links. Restoring the complete cleaned model preserves their agreement. |
| `StationRegionBodyContextResolver` and startup consumers | The resolver holds a lookup, not another cell map. Restoring the original registry identity makes existing readers and future allocation use one model again. |
| Station notices, core/controller blocks, platform and region ticket | The F8 fixture already removes its own notices and blocks/platform, clears warp runtime state and releases its ticket. These remain in teardown. |
| Elevator `pairs`, `byStation`, `byEndpoint`, `byColumn` and device observations | The new elevator tests replace only `EndgameSavedData`, rebind its service, and clear the device runtime first. Their fixture close removes the live pair and all its reverse links, removes endpoint blocks, releases ticket/forced chunk, then deletes and flushes its station through its unchanged station authority. |
