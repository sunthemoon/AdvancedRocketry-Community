## Completed scope and files

- [The campaign runner](../../../../scripts/run_v180_integrated_campaign.py) accepts
  integer `--yield-seconds` values from 0 through 600. It records the option in the
  plan, campaign summary and every invocation identity. After a successful lock
  release, it sleeps once before the next selected family, never after the last
  selected family or during a dry run. The preceding family's `yield` records the
  requested and elapsed seconds and UTC start/end times; the start is persisted
  before sleeping and the end is persisted afterwards.
- Each acquisition's `lock_wait` records elapsed seconds, UTC start/end times and
  distinct observed foreign owner lines. The observation survives acquisition
  failure. Owner reads are best-effort, skip linked markers and read at most 4096
  characters of the first line. Missing or unreadable markers do not change the
  existing polling behaviour.
- [Nine new tests](../../../../tests/test_v180_integrated_campaign_yield.py) use fake
  clocks and locks/local temporary directories. They cover default zero, exact
  boundary sleeps and timestamps, no final sleep, foreign-owner handoff, the
  unchanged 10,800-second timeout, stale-lock refusal, dry runs, option bounds and
  unchanged family specifications and child commands. Existing tests were not edited.
- This record is the only documentation change. No product code, other runners,
  build files, status files, ledgers, family selections, ordering, arguments or
  oracles changed. No upstream material was imported.

## Design decisions

No ADR change was required. The baseline already has a 90-second reacquisition
cooldown; it remains unchanged. Default zero adds no explicit yield sleep, rather
than removing that cooldown. The explicit yield consumes elapsed cooldown time,
so the waits are not unconditionally added together. `lock_wait.seconds` measures
the acquisition attempt after any remaining existing cooldown, not the explicit
yield. The three-hour wait and stale-lock limits and ownership-checked release
remain unchanged. No foreign lock is removed.
