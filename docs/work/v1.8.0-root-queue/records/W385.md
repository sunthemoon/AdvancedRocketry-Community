## Concurrent-flight open item

`pendingSpawnDoesNotPauseAnotherCountdownAscentOrTransit` does have an incomplete pre-spawn fixture wait in the old
code: entity ticking at each origin does not establish entity-data readiness throughout the discovery margin.
`37b749d8f` fixes that prerequisite. It does **not** establish or fix the cause of the open W378 failure.

Unlike W383, both its journal and service are detached. The runtime cannot select these records from its world
journal. W378's historical transfer B had already acknowledged DESTINATION_SPAWNED, and A subsequently landed. A
PREPARED entity-loading wait cannot explain that later missing publication/progression. The possible publication or
authority failure is still unestablished. W378's `checkWait` and `describeWait` diagnostics are unchanged. Root should
keep the concurrent-flight item open until cause-specific evidence closes it; these readiness assertions and passing
reruns are not that evidence. No status file was edited.
