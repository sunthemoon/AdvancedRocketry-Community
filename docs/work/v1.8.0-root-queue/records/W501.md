## Pairing design and refusals

An optional baseline-session `paired_world: {path, sha256}` names a candidate
world's `level.dat`. Its SHA-256 pins exactly the metadata bytes read, not an
entire save tree. Validation is offline and precedes platform probing, locks
and process creation. Preparation rechecks the pin before creating the game.
Both session and direct command preparation refuse source/output overlap.
Omitting the field preserves the original seed-0 flat world; null is refused.

The new metadata copies the original NBT types and values for seed,
generate-features/bonus-chest flags, complete Overworld generator, spawn XYZ
and angle, Time/DayTime, vanilla game rules, weather/timers, difficulty/lock
and border settings. Available saved Player Pos/Rotation/Dimension become the
camera inputs of a fresh creative flying player; inventory, UUIDs, effects,
capabilities and other player data are not copied. No chunks, entities, block
entities, modded dimensions, external packs or arbitrary mod/root data enter
the output. Its enabled packs remain only `vanilla`, with three vanilla
dimensions. It always starts as a new disposable world.

Supported generators are deliberately narrow: plains flat generation using
air, bedrock, dirt, grass, stone or water layers, at most 384 positive-height
layers totaling at most 384 blocks, with absent/empty structure overrides;
or named vanilla overworld/large-biomes/amplified noise settings using the
built-in overworld multi-noise preset. Unknown and non-vanilla generators,
dimension types, biomes/blocks, inline noise definitions, custom biome lists,
nonempty structure overrides and extra generator fields are named refusals,
not approximations. Unknown/invalid game rules, wrong/missing NBT types,
unsupported DataVersion, nonfinite state, non-Overworld or malformed/bounded-out
saved viewpoints, external/unsupported enabled packs, malformed/oversized NBT,
links and hash changes are also refused. The existing 4 MiB compressed/decoded,
64-depth and 262,144-tag NBT bounds are reused.

All seven W500 source levels lack saved Player camera data. Their receipts
explicitly say spawn-only; no camera or complete comparison key is invented.
The actual camera, hardware, graphics, window mode, GUI scale, vsync, FPS cap
and sampling plan still require matching live observations. These are not all
stored in level.dat. READY remains a window receipt, not world qualification.

The real input check preserves five exact typed roundtrips: K04's 64/512/2048
and multiple-rocket worlds, and K16/v2. It refuses K07/discovered because its
enabled packs include `file/arce_v100_upgrade`, and K21/component because they
include `mod:arce_adapter_test`. This slice does not establish those packs'
generation effects, whitelist them or remove them. Their exact refusals and
all seven metadata hashes are in `paired-world-checks.json` below. The generator
itself is vanilla bedrock-flat in all seven; unsupported packs are a separate
reason not to claim exact reproducibility.
