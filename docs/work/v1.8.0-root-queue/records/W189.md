## Implementation and decisions

- **B5:** `44008a264` adds the tracker regression; `7c05c7efd` sets vanilla
  `hasImpulse` every two ticks only in ASCENT and DESCENT. The parked interval remains
  20 ticks; COUNTDOWN, TRANSIT and inactive states are unchanged. No velocity or codec
  change. Estimated traffic is 10 position updates per second, at most 11 with an
  offset between the entity and tracker schedules, excluding existing dirty-data sends.
  Relative payloads are approximately 8–12 bytes, absolute updates 28–32 bytes, plus
  roughly 2–3 bytes of Minecraft framing: about 0.1–0.2 KB/s normally and below
  approximately 0.4 KB/s with absolute updates, per rocket per tracking client.
  This excludes TCP and unrelated metadata and is not a packet capture.
- **B7:** `900f32ef7` adds the failing native rest-start case; `f89dcf1bc` adds a
  transient MULTIPLY_TOTAL floor of 0.0031 for positive low-gravity slow falling.
  After 0.98 drag, 0.003038 remains above vanilla's 0.003 motion dead zone. The body
  modifier and fall-damage factor remain separate. The correction starts after vanilla
  installs its slow-falling modifier. Zero gravity, ordinary/rising motion, no-gravity,
  water/lava travel and disabled gravity retain their existing behavior.
  `1463793a8` reproduces a clamping edge case; `eeb6c4305` recomputes the value without
  our floor before sanitization rather than dividing an already clamped value.
  Foreign modifiers and the base are untouched. No Mixin or persistent modifier.
- **B6 / R153:** `fb8cb9d0d`, `d6a0b79ee`, `e1dbcfb03` and later refinements separate
  translated reasons from diagnostics. New failure reports store the reason in the
  existing 256-character `RocketAssemblerData.detail`. Schema, field types and decoder
  are unchanged; old strings load and resave without migration. Player feedback retains
  its translation key but loses the literal diagnostic suffix. The diagnostic code,
  position and sorted map go into the existing failure log line and a transient
  1,024-character operator detail, with control characters flattened. The existing
  permission-gated status command exposes it; loading/replacing a report clears it.
- **B6 localization:** the entity uses its entity translation, not its menu title, and
  preserves custom names. Native tests exposed missing headless-server translations.
  Resource aliases passed native tests but failed the duplicate-key audit; they were
  superseded, not exempted. `b5f2afe05` and `325e40448` reuse packaged English labels
  from the v0.5, v1.3 and v1.8 namespaces in an immutable, bounded rocket-only table.
  Vanilla translatable fallbacks provide server text while an available locale wins.
  Existing English/Chinese entity labels are reused. There are no final generated
  resource/provider changes, duplicate keys, new public IDs or global language changes.

The saved string that changes content is a new failure report's `detail`. Synced player
feedback loses its diagnostic suffix; the operator response gains a bounded diagnostic
suffix. Their existing string/component formats and packet codecs do not change.
No upstream file or artwork was imported, and no ADR decision was changed.
