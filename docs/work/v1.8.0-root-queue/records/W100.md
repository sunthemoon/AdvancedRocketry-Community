## Completed work and design decisions

- Added fluid cargo load/unload and reserved, rejected item-cargo names. The schema-1
  fluid frame contains the persisted snapshot/position/adapter cursor and canonical
  registered Fluid NBT. Both inner and framed byte/depth/node limits are checked.
- The rocket-side accessor now selects either transfer direction for native bindings,
  logical-rocket counting, floor identity/retirement, guard lookup and capacity.
  Ordinary cargo uses `ROCKET_COMPOSITE_V1`; its binding and floor retain the after
  snapshot's invariant. Native TANK and propulsion endpoints share the PRIMARY key.
- Pure capture binds complete before/after envelopes, the same-capture marker section,
  cursor identity and address, both composite signatures and unchanged modern
  propulsion. It grants no mutation permit or native readback authority.
- `prepareTransfer` uses the existing reservation and exact L1 writer. Cargo cannot
  chain or share a logical rocket with queued, captured or durable work. Fuel loaders
  report `ROCKET_BUSY` for cargo. `prepareFill` delegates while retaining its original
  closed `Fill` enum; the generalized `Transfer` enum carries the new refusals.
- RAM turn hints are coordinator-owned, owner-thread-only, bounded to 256 registered
  loaded devices, consumed by the selected admission and removed on registration
  close or coordinator close/abort. They never override holds. Device/fair-queue
  registration integration remains C-E.
- A marker absent from records is not presumed retired. Unknown retained markers
  refuse admission without issuing a sequence, reservation or writer capture.
  Settlement history or a READY head refuses cargo as `RECEIPT_BACKED`.
  Thus the five-marker sizing allowance is not permission to use an unproved wrapper.
- O1/O2/O4 rocket observations retain `FUEL_VECTOR_V1` plus the cargo-sensitive
  invariant, separate from composite endpoint references. O4 selects the frozen
  rocket-side member, including unload, without live resource inputs. Cargo receipts
  retain zero `payload_amount`, the payload hash and UNKNOWN accounting.
- The frozen Q2/Q3/Q4 choices are retained: receipt-backed refusal, modern propulsion
  only, and one live cargo operation. No ADR decision, proof meaning, lease, replay
  exclusion, timeout, assertion in an existing test, or size limit was changed.
  The escrow root is unpublished under R-035, so these operation names need no
  migration. Intent schema 2, receipt schema 3 and the closed receipt dictionary
  are unchanged.
