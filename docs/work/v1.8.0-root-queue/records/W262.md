## The rule change (`3ff68b7de`)

`BayPassengerExitMotion.Point` names the four observation points: PlayerTick START and the server-tick END commit;
PlayerTick END and the fall or attack callbacks only check. The change has three parts.

1. **W257-R1, contact.** At a check-only PlayerTick END sample, ordinary floor contact no longer closes the offer.
   Fluid, unsafe and unknown contact still close it, and a committed sample still closes on any contact.
2. **W257-R1, the landing's airborne flag.** `fall()` reads `Continuity.airborne`. A committed PlayerTick START sets
   it. Any other committed sample sets it too, except a server-END sample in a tick whose PlayerTick START was
   committed: that sample follows vanilla's temporary step, and its `onGround` is the step's. Where no temporary step
   ran since (the exit's own sample, the exit's own tick as in X9, and the GameTests that do not tick connections), the
   flag is the committed state that the landing packet moves from. This is how this record reads R288's "the landing
   packet's pre-move state". The committed baseline itself (`last`) is unchanged; only the flag is kept.
3. **W257-R2.** A check-only sample is held to the per-tick limits (1 block horizontal, 4 down) on its own
   displacement from the committed baseline, without the tick's committed usage, which it still cannot spend or renew.
   No separate code is needed for "a packet handled after the server-tick END counts toward the next tick": a packet's
   callbacks are check-only, so they are no longer charged to the tick that has ended, and the packet's move is first
   committed at the next tick's PlayerTick START, which starts that tick's usage.

The recognized landing frames, the single cancellation, the 3-block fall threshold, the deadline, the radius, the
upward and velocity checks, the single early counter step and the budgets are unchanged. Option (a) was enough: no
change to what counts as an ordinary landing was needed.

## R-042 and R-043 draft for Root

This supersedes W254's draft (`RISK-DRAFTS-W254.md` in the W254 folder) and both drafts in W257's record. It is not an
adopted risk-register entry, ADR revision, Gate waiver or admission decision.

> **R-042, the passenger exit's descent.** The descent's movement is checked against its last committed server
> position: the sample at `PlayerTickEvent` START, after the client's move packet, or at the server-tick END, after
> the connection has restored the committed position. At most one tick passes between committed samples. Committed
> samples spend the per-tick allowance (at most 1 block horizontally and 4 blocks down, cumulative within a tick) and
> must stay inside the 8-block radius, never move up and never gain upward velocity.
>
> Vanilla's server-side player tick moves an airborne rider by its own velocity at `PlayerTickEvent` END and the
> connection then restores the committed position. This temporary step is only checked: it never becomes the baseline
> and never spends or renews allowance. It does not revoke the offer by being lower than the committed position, by the
> return to that position, by standing the rider on an ordinary floor, or by the `onGround` flag it leaves. It does
> revoke the offer when its own displacement from the committed baseline exceeds a per-tick limit or moves up, when it
> meets fluid, a hazard, partial or unknown support, a climbable block or an unproved window, when the rider leaves an
> ordinary mode, and on any authority, counter, deadline or budget failure observed there. The synchronous callbacks
> of a move packet, the landing packet's among them, are checked the same way; a packet handled after the server-tick
> END is not charged to that tick.
>
> A landing is protected only through the landing packet's own fall event: the rider was airborne at the last
> committed `PlayerTickEvent` START (or, when no temporary step ran since, at the committed state the landing moves
> from), the fall is more than 3 blocks with a positive multiplier, the stack shows the recognized vanilla landing
> frames, and the landed position is ordinary floor contact. Exactly one FALL attack in that tick is then cancelled.
>
> Residuals: movement between observation points is not traced. A real client whose last airborne packet ends within
> 0.01 block of the floor is seen standing at the next committed sample, and that landing is not protected. Two move
> packets bunched into one server tick count as one displacement, so a fall faster than 2 blocks per tick can end the
> offer when that happens. A temporary step standing on a floor keeps the offer until the deadline only if no landing
> packet follows, and it grants nothing. The RAM-only residuals and the R264 and R274 limits (one early counter step,
> no rebaselining, the 200-tick deadline, the budgets) are unchanged. W254's residual (the offer ending two ticks after
> the exit on a ticking connection) and W257's open defects W257-R1 and W257-R2 are closed by W257 and W262.
>
> **R-043, cross-reference.** W254's native cuts, rerun by W262, kill a dedicated server at each phase of a held exit
> and restart it twice into the REPAIR hold. For X2 (descent), X4 (landing), X5 (expiry) and X8 (safe exit) a second
> `save-all flush` after the phase puts the exit into the save: the rider's player file holds it detached, at its
> position at the cut, and the first restart's login returns it there. In every case no exit state (live slot, refusal
> or UUID/T history) survives, T stays PREPARED with the exited passenger's seat, the rocket's manifest keeps the seat,
> and the receipt file and the pending charge are byte- and count-equal to the cut. A physically exited passenger
> therefore stays assigned, also after a save that contains the exit, and may later be reconciled by the released or
> ordinary passenger phase. The other cases are crash cuts after an unsaved exit, after which the passenger returns at
> the position of the last player save (the seated position at altitude). None of this is an atomic save of player
> data. The copy, payload, authority-conflict and offline-repair residuals are unchanged.

W254's V2-path residual (the fixture's release-test gate refuses real clients, so V2 needs a separately decided
campaign path) is unchanged and stays Root's to place.

  the login's `mounted=false`, not the file.
