`ChunkMap.m_140258_` / `m_198874_` saving terrain between `C2_SOURCE_AFTER` and `C2_DESTINATION_BEFORE`, at tick 18,

barrier that stores both endpoints together. The driver selects one endpoint at a time; `EscrowBarrier.sameSlot`
separates terrain and entity kinds. Inspection of the installed mapped Minecraft class with `javap -c -p` confirms
that `saveChunkIfNeeded` starts an existing 10,000 ms cooldown after a normal save; the forced fixture baseline
alone did not establish that cooldown.

of tank 5,000/epoch 0, and then opens its per-device gate. It does not suppress saves, alter cooldowns, dirty state
after admission, store order or proofs. `native-03-1` observes that priming save, then DESTINATION-first force and
the required mixed raw snapshot. K5 is applicable and covered; no loss, duplication or wrong recovery was found.
