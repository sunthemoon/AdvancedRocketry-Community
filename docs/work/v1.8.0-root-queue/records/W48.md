The command is the one AUDIT-01 section 5 names, plus the two client JARs:

```text
python -B tools/import/import_v180_assets.py generate --upstream <Advanced Rocketry clone at the UPSTREAM.md commit>
    --plan tools/import/v180-<batch>-plan.json --record docs/provenance/v1.8.0-c18d-<batch>.json
    --vanilla 1.12.2=<client.jar> --vanilla 1.20.1=<client.jar>
```

`verify` takes the same arguments. The plan format is documented in the importer's docstring and shown by the test
fixture in `tests/test_import_v180_assets.py` (`base_plan`).

- **Plan.** A plan has a batch ID, the asset-coverage table and one to eight named sources: the pinned upstream
  (`UPSTREAM_AR_MIT`) and repository-owned `NEW` files at an authoring commit. Each of its 1 to 512 entries names:
  - the source, its path and its SHA-256;
  - the target;
  - one transformation;
  - the owning ledger units;
  - the asset-coverage row.
- **Transformations.**
  - `copy`: a byte copy into the mod namespace.
  - `rename`: a byte copy to a new asset path.
  - `obj` and `mtl`: a byte copy except the `mtllib`, `usemtl` or texture-map values the plan maps.
  - `lang`: legacy keys extracted to the modern keys the plan names. Every legacy key is either mapped or listed
    as discarded.
- **Checks.** These run for every entry, in `generate` and in `verify`:
  - the source SHA-256 against the plan, the legacy manifest and the coverage row;
  - the asset-plan handling (IMPORT or IMPORTED) and the owning units against the ledger and the coverage row;
  - the derivation re-run (`tools/audit/vanilla_derivation.py`) on the source, and on the target when its bytes
    differ, against the two pinned JARs and every legacy asset, with the section 4.8 inheritance;
  - the section 4.9 upstream history from a full clone at the `UPSTREAM.md` commit;
  - the OBJ/MTL format, budget and reference check over the whole plan.
- **Failures.** Any failure fails the whole run. The per-entry checks report every failure; a plan or pin error
  stops the run at once. `generate` stages all files and writes nothing unless every check passes. `verify` recomputes the record and every target and compares them
  byte for byte.
- **Record.** The record is schema 2:
  - every schema-1 entry field;
  - top level: a `sources` list, the combined notice, the plan's SHA-256, the derivation and client pins, and the
    model-set totals;
  - per entry: the units, the coverage row, the derivation verdicts and related files, `upstream_history` (in the
    form of the C18b jackhammer record), `format_check` for OBJ and MTL, and `authoring_commit` for NEW entries;
  - the v0.1.0 `review` block, reused from `import_v010_assets.py`, so an APPROVED review survives only an
    unchanged record.

## Choices where the ADRs left room

1. **`--vanilla` is required.** The derivation re-run needs both client JARs. Each JAR's SHA-256 must equal the
   ADR-061 pin, and the pinned results must name the same clients.
2. **Derivation.**
   - The importer requires CLEAR from the re-run and from the pinned results.
   - A HIT is always refused. The `overrides: HIT` appeal is not implemented.
   - SUSPECT, UNSUPPORTED, an inherited review or a history trigger needs a CLEARED origin finding for the asset.
     The importer reads only the decision; `validate_v180_content_ledger.py` checks that the finding is complete.
   - A NEW entry has no finding path, so anything other than CLEAR fails it.
3. **Inheritance.**
   - The rule is the one in ADR-061 section 4.8, as the ledger validator applies it: a related file tainted by a
     HIT, SUSPECT or UNSUPPORTED verdict, a REVIEW handling or an EXCLUDED finding.
   - Relations come from the pinned results (either direction) and from the re-run.
   - EXCLUDE handling for other reasons does not taint. Reading section 4.4's "derivative of an EXCLUDE file" more
     strictly would block K6's `oremapper.png` and `satelliteidchip.png`. Their only related files are the icons
     of deferred owners.
4. **History.**
   - The history is the union of `git log --follow` and `git log --follow --first-parent`, so a pull request
     merged with a merge commit is seen.
   - A shallow clone fails. A `--filter=blob:none` clone, as CI uses, passes.
   - The record lists each commit's hash, date, author and subject. Commit bodies are scanned but not recorded.
   - Triggers:
     - the AR 16x set: `#1809`, `#1811`, `#1889`, `#1976`, or one of the five C14 commits (two only by their
       eight-character prefix);
     - phrases that bring in material: "from an issue", "from #N", "unmerged", "taken from", "ported from",
       "courtesy of", "thanks" or "credit", and URLs.
   - A plain issue reference such as "fixes #86", or a merged pull request number, is not a trigger.
   - Applied to the C14 history file (subjects only) in the Temp folder, the patterns find no trigger in the 38
     class B rows. They find "thanks" credits in three of the seven class F translations: `de_DE`, `es_ES` and
     `ru_RU`.
5. **OBJ and MTL.**
   - A baked object is one OBJ file. Its vertices are the larger of its positions and four corners per face,
     since Forge bakes each face into one quad.
   - The model set is the plan's OBJ and MTL files and the PNG files their MTLs name. The budgets apply per plan;
     the runtime-wide budget belongs to the K1 loader.
   - Refused:
     - faces outside a material, which Forge drops;
     - polygons with more than four corners, which Forge truncates;
     - a second `mtllib`, or `mtllib` after `usemtl`;
     - unknown statements and texture-map options;
     - `#variable` textures, textures without a namespace, other namespaces, absolute paths, URLs and parent
       directories.
   - Every material a face uses needs a `map_Kd`; without one, Forge shows the missing texture.
   - `l` elements, which Forge ignores, are checked and counted. `rollingmachine.obj` has two.
6. **Reference rewrite.** Only the `mtllib`, `usemtl` and `map_Ka/Kd/Ks` values change. Every reference must be
   mapped and every mapping used. No statement is added or removed, so an OBJ without materials (the jetpack)
   cannot be fixed by a rewrite.
7. **`.lang`.**
   - The parser is strict: UTF-8 without BOM, `key=value` lines, no duplicate keys and no `#PARSE_ESCAPES`.
   - Values are kept verbatim, as 1.12.2 reads them; the v0.1.0 tool strips lines instead.
   - Modern keys must contain the `advancedrocketrycommunity` component, as every current English key does.
   - The output has the Forge LanguageProvider format.
8. **Targets.**
   - Targets are only under `src/main/resources/assets/advancedrocketrycommunity/`, which is recorded per
     ADR-061 section 4.7 and is not a DataGen root that `runData` would rewrite.
   - A target that also exists in a DataGen root, or that another provenance record names, fails.
   - An existing file is never overwritten unless this batch's record names its hash.
   - A target dropped from the plan makes `generate` fail until it is removed deliberately.
9. **NEW sources.**
   - A NEW source is a file under the target root at an authoring commit of this repository.
   - Its `source_commit` must be that commit's first parent, as section 4.3 says.
   - Its notice must appear in `LICENSE`.
10. **Coverage rows.** Every upstream entry names its asset-coverage row (a 1-based data row). The row's path and
    SHA-256 must match, and its owning units, if any, must equal the plan's. An asset outside the C18d coverage,
    such as C18b's jetpack, cannot use this version.
11. **File types.** The importer accepts PNG, `.png.mcmeta` (a JSON animation object, with its image in the same
    plan), OBJ, MTL and LANG. OGG, JPEG and JSON are refused because they have no format or origin check
    (ADR-066 section 7.3).

## Known risks

- **History triggers.** They are heuristics on commit messages; the maintainer's record review (ADR-061 section
  4.5) still has to read `upstream_history`. The real upstream history was not run here, because there is no
  local clone. The smoke covered the derivation and the parser only.
- **`fi_FI.lang`.** The file is not UTF-8 and has a line without `=`, so X2 cannot import it as it is.
- **Run time.** Each upstream entry runs `git log --follow` twice. On a `blob:none` clone that can fetch objects,
  so a plan of hundreds of entries may be slow. The derivation re-run takes about a minute.
- **Line count.** `import_v180_assets.py` is 565 lines, over the 500-line review threshold of AGENTS.md section
  3.4. I reviewed it: it is one pipeline (plan, checked targets, record and files). The OBJ/MTL set check moved
  out for that reason.
