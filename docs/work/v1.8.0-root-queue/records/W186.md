# W186: bounded loaded sealed discovery revisits

## Design note (before implementation)

The write scope is `SealedLoadedDiscovery.java`, its new unit test, and this record. The branch is
`codex/v1.8.0-w186`, based on `073a97f7f45dd41a1a22725cf54ba17db0b0aa7c`. W186 implements Root decision R146;
it does not change an ADR. The initial worktree was clean and other running workers use separate worktrees.

At entry to a loaded chunk, capture its block-entity count N. If a publication or another structural map edit
invalidates the iterator, restart that chunk's iterator rather than advancing to the next chunk. Allow at most N
such restarts during this visit, without replenishing the allowance when the map grows. A stable map still finishes
normally. Removing each published carrier therefore permits the remaining carriers to be discovered in the same
sweep. Repeatedly changing maps eventually exhaust their restart allowance and yield to the next chunk; remaining
carriers remain eligible on the next sweep. The cursor does not copy the map or collect all its positions.

Every iterator advance or restart occupies one caller-charged discovery step. The 256-unit `SealedWorkBudget`, the
64-entry hint queue, publication charges, and the discovery/attempt alternation in `SealedLazyWork.tick` stay
unchanged. Restarts may repeat a non-carrier prefix, so the visit is not guaranteed to be linear in N; each repeated
inspection is still charged and resumes on later ticks. The allowance is local cursor state, not a new saved bound.

Before every entity step, including a restart, the existing Level identity and `getChunkNow` identity checks still
apply. If the Level disappears or the chunk unloads or is replaced, discard that visit immediately, without adding
tickets or loading a chunk. No saved format, journal entry, existing bound, budget, fixture, forced-chunk policy,
status file, ledger, or asset changes. Independent read-only review and integration remain Root's responsibility.

## Remaining work and known risks

- Root still needs the independent read-only review before merge, integration, and hosted regression. No version
  status, Required Gate, ADR decision or ledger entry is marked passed by this work.
- A chunk with many ordinary block entities can require repeated prefix inspections while carriers publish. With
  removals alone this can be quadratic in the entry count over the whole visit, but every step remains charged to
  the unchanged 256-unit tick allowance. A map invalidated more often than its initial count still yields to the
  next chunk; its remaining carriers wait for another sweep. The change does not promise progress while a provider,
  journal, unavailable chunk or other existing publication guard holds a carrier.
- There are no saved-format, network, asset or data-generation changes. `runData`, a full `clean build`, native
  restart cases, and visual/multiplayer acceptance were not rerun for this scoped cursor task. This is not evidence
  that v1.8.0 satisfies all Required Gates. The next task action is Root's W186 review, not a later-version feature.
