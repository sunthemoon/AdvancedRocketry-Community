**Classes.** New classes stay under 500 lines; `RocketTransferService` and the transfer writer are unchanged.
- `persistence/CheckedRootWriter`, the shared core. It is generic over a root's capture, request attributes and
  completion payload, and takes an executor seam. It holds one active attempt (with an attempt number), a one-slot
  completion queue, one pending flag (merged attributes and the sequence they asked for), the 10-tick cadence and the
  200-tick backoff on the caller's tick, the shutdown latch, the final-target drain with its one three-attempt budget,
  and `close`, `abort` and the join. Every public method checks the owner thread.
- `rocket/persistence/RocketTransactionWriter`, the journal's half: the capture record (sequence, at most 256 unsorted
  entry references, the previous acknowledgement and outcome), the IO encoder (today's `save(CompoundTag)` layout,
  sorted on IO), the NC1 record and root digests at depth 35, and one `CheckedJournalCheckpoint` per attempt, seeded
  with the captured acknowledgement. `CheckedJournalCheckpoint` gains only that seeded constructor.
- `rocket/persistence/RocketTransactionCodec` receives the entry codec moved unchanged out of the SavedData, so that
  `RocketTransactionSavedData` stays under 500 lines with the writer state added.
- `rocket/persistence/RocketTransactionLifecycle`: tick START at HIGHEST polls; `ServerStoppingEvent` at HIGHEST
  latches shutdown; the Overworld `LevelEvent.Unload` at HIGHEST closes (drain, then join); `ServerStoppedEvent` aborts
  as the backstop.
- Release-test seams for the native cuts, inert unless `advancedrocketrycommunity.releaseTestHooks` is set on an empty
  dedicated server: a cut seam and a `transaction-writer` command group. `RocketTransactionReleaseProbe` now awaits
  the journal's acknowledgement before it logs its pause, because `saveEverything` only requests a write.

**Rules**, as ADR-074 items 2 to 4, 8 to 10 and 12. `requestCheckpoint` answers REQUESTED or REFUSED (not
operational, stopping, closed). Publication is at tick START only: COMMITTED sets the acknowledgement, NOT_COMMITTED
keeps it (UNKNOWN stays sticky), UNKNOWN clears it, and dirty becomes `!acknowledgedCurrent()`. B14 gains
WRITE_PENDING after PROVED. A running `save(File)` makes a request and returns. A stopping `save(File)` sets F and
drains; `close` resumes the same drain and joins; `abort` joins without publishing. Logging: one ERROR at the first
FAILED or UNKNOWN publication of a streak and one INFO at the commit that ends it; one ERROR per unexpected writer
`Throwable`; one WARN per shutdown when a drain gives up (F, last committed sequence, attempts, reason).

**Choices where the documents are open** (conservative):
1. A root that needs no write at shutdown (not dirty, nothing active or pending) is covered by its file, as today's
   `save(File)` only writes dirty roots; a loaded clean journal is not rewritten.
2. The drain clock starts at the last observed tick, so a backoff in progress still holds into shutdown.
3. A refusal of a non-operational journal does not log; its load already logged once (W123 added no loop).
4. A COMMITTED capture at s clears a pending request whose sequence is at most s and whose policy is equal. FAILED or
   UNKNOWN keeps it, and it starts after the backoff.
5. Only a detached journal (no server: unit tests, diagnostics) may await IO in the calling thread. The release-test
   probe's await is refused unless the release-test property is set on an empty dedicated server.
6. The IO thread ends after 60 idle seconds, so detached test journals leak no threads; at most one exists.
7. A poll during shutdown neither publishes nor starts; only the drain does.

**Tests.** `CheckedRootWriterTest` (section 6.2's core list with an executor seam and fake roots), the W123 matrix in
`RocketTransactionProofTest` with every case and assertion kept and awaited, its new cases, a capture/encoder
byte-identity test on fixtures including W128's 256-record 64 MiB fixture, nested-alias tests, and lifecycle tests on
a fake host for both shutdown flag combinations. A1: `RocketTransactionProofGameTests` awaited, plus autosave and
`/save-all` requests that publish on later ticks. Each deliberately driven ERROR or WARN gets its manifest entry.

**Benchmarks and native cuts.** B2 and B3a as standalone `main` classes under `src/test`, Xmx4G, under the heavy-JVM
lock, with W128's classes kept as the baseline and the stop rule unchanged. Native: T1 (paused before encode, kill), T2
(staged, kill), T3 (moved, kill), R6 (normal stop, stale capture, mutation after shutdown entry), R7 with a thrown tick
exception (stale success, failure exhaustion, UNKNOWN exhaustion) and R7 interrupted by the watchdog; two restarts each.

**Not in this slice.** W129b, W129c, W27d, the Q6 ground preflight, ADR status or text, other ADRs, the risk register,
status files and the ledger. No saved field, schema, filename, format epoch, digest algorithm or bound changes.

### What changed, by area

- **Writer core** (`persistence/CheckedRootWriter`, 306 lines). It is generic over a root's capture, request
  attributes and completion payload, and takes an executor seam. It holds one active attempt with an attempt number,
  a one-slot completion queue, and one pending flag that merges attributes and remembers the sequence it asked for.
  Every start path observes the 10-tick cadence and the 200-tick backoff, and requests cannot reset either. The
  shutdown latch counts an attempt in flight, published or not, as attempt 1. One budget of three attempts is shared
  by every drain, close and the abort backstop. Close resumes the same drain and then always joins. Abort joins
  without publishing and never reports success. An interrupted join waits for termination and restores the
  interruption afterward. `CheckedJournalCheckpoint` gains one constructor seeded with a previous acknowledgement,
  so each IO attempt classifies its readback against the acknowledgement its capture carried; the transfer path
  still uses the old constructor.
- **Transaction journal** (`rocket/persistence`). `RocketTransactionWriter` holds the capture record, the IO encoder,
  the digests and one seeded checkpoint per attempt. `RocketTransactionCodec` holds the moved entry codec.
  `RocketTransactionSavedData` (486 lines) holds the owner-side acknowledgement and outcome, the request API, the
  publication rules, WRITE_PENDING, the running and stopping saves, the latch, close, abort and the streak logging.
  `RocketTransactionLifecycle` polls at tick START, latches at `ServerStoppingEvent`, closes at the Overworld unload
  and aborts at `ServerStoppedEvent`, all at HIGHEST.
- **Release-test seams.** `RocketTransactionWriterCuts` and `RocketTransactionWriterCommands` are inert unless the
  release-test property is set on an empty dedicated server. `RocketTransactionReleaseProbe.persistAndPause` now
  awaits the journal's acknowledgement after `saveEverything`, which only requests the write.
- **Tests.** `CheckedRootWriterTest` (11 cases), `RocketTransactionWriterTest` (4), `RocketTransactionShutdownTest` (7),
  `RocketTransactionLoggingTest` (3), `RocketTransactionProofTest` (the 14 W123 cases kept and 4 added), the A1
  GameTests (three kept and one added), and `tests/test_run_v180_transaction_writer_cuts.py` (9).
- **Benchmarks** under `src/test`: `RocketTransactionWriterBenchmark` (B2), `RocketWriterOverlapBenchmark` (B3a, with a
  transfer-only comparison mode), `WriterBenchmarkEnvironment`, and fixture accessors in the registry and transfer
  test packages. W128's `RocketTransactionCheckpointBenchmark` and W11B2's `RocketJournalBenchmark` only had two
  fixture helpers each made package-private.
- **Log manifest.** One ERROR expectation, `w129a-transaction-journal-unacknowledged`, and the pinned ERROR total in
  `tests/test_check_gametest_log.py` raised from 107 to 108.
- **Native harness.** `scripts/run_v180_transaction_writer_cuts.py`.

Unchanged: `RocketTransferService` (empty diff from `a355e687`), the transfer writer and its checkpoint, and every saved
field, schema, file name, format epoch, digest algorithm and bound. No ADR, status file, ledger or risk entry was
edited.

**Test conversions, assertions kept.** In `RocketTransactionProofTest`, `mutationDuringReplacementCannotAcknowledgeNewerRamOrClearDirty`
used to mutate from inside the committer. The IO thread may not touch the journal, so the owner now mutates while the
committer is paused; every assertion is kept, and `COMMITTED` is asserted after the await. The autosave case awaits

a rerun would measure end-to-end wall time on the calling thread, not W128's synchronous encoding. Its recorded W128
figures remain the baseline.

### Choices where the documents were open

The seven choices in the plan hold. These were added during implementation:

8. A detached journal (no server) counts one tick per monotonic millisecond, the drain clock's unit, so a later
   request on it can start without an await. A live journal uses the server tick.
9. A release-test CAPTURE cut travels in the capture as a nullable hook; production captures carry null.
10. When the stop path needs no write, the drain reports success with `target=0`. That happened at the GameTest server's
    stop: the journal was clean at sequence 698, so both the save and the close drains covered it without an attempt.
11. The abort backstop logs the same once-per-shutdown WARN (reason ABORTED_UNRESOLVED) when it finds work that no drain
    reported.

### For Root: protections, holds and log lines that this code introduces (not registered here)

- **Holds and protections.**
  - From `ServerStoppingEvent`, or from the first observed shutdown flag, gameplay requests are refused
    (`requestCheckpoint` answers REFUSED) and no tick start begins a write.
  - The final-target drain has one three-attempt budget per root and shutdown.
  - Close and abort join the IO thread before world storage is released, without a timeout, so stuck IO holds the
    world until the operator ends the process.
  - A failed or unknown write starts the 200-tick backoff.
  - Refusals of blocked or unreadable journals keep the unchanged rule, now applied on the owner thread.
- **Log lines** (no total cap):
  - `ARCE_TRANSACTION_JOURNAL_UNACKNOWLEDGED outcome= sequence= acknowledged=`: ERROR at the first failed or unknown
    publication of a streak.
  - `ARCE_TRANSACTION_JOURNAL_ACKNOWLEDGED sequence= after an unacknowledged write`: INFO at the commit that ends a
    streak.
  - `ARCE_CHECKED_WRITER_THROWABLE root= sequence=`: ERROR with the Throwable, once per unexpected writer Throwable.
  - `ARCE_CHECKED_WRITER_DRAIN_INCOMPLETE root= target= committed= attempts= reason=`: WARN, once per root and
    shutdown. The reason is BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, INTERRUPTED, ABORTED_UNRESOLVED or an exception class name.
  - `ARCE_CHECKED_WRITER_DRAINED root= target= committed= attempts=`: INFO when a drain covers the root.
  - The proof GameTest drives the ERROR once; its manifest entry is `w129a-transaction-journal-unacknowledged`.
- **Operator meaning.** `/save-all` and `/save-all flush` no longer leave this file current when they return.
- **Residuals.**
  - Shutdown wall time is unmeasured.
  - A watchdog-ended teardown keeps only the committed-or-unknown whole-file guarantee (R7 interrupted).
  - An exhausted drain leaves its RAM work unsaved (R7 failed).
  - Detached test journals keep an idle IO thread for up to 60 seconds.
  - The overlap GC finding above.
