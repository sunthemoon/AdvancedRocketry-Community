The rule, derived rather than listed: a material form is a form tag of the material table. The
folders are the products' tag folders (`MaterialCatalog.Product.tagFolder()`, with this mod's
namespace for sheets, coils, fans and boules) plus `ores` and `raw_materials`. The names are the
table's materials and ore names plus every name this mod ships a tag file for in those folders,
which adds `carbon` (`forge:ingots/carbon`, the carbon brick) and `thermite`. The members come from
this mod's, Forge's and vanilla's classpath copies with nested tags expanded: 210 tags, 106 items.
Any `item` entry that is one of them fails. The W56 inputs stay allowed because they are not
material forms of this mod (redstone, sand, charcoal, logs, bone and the ender eye are not in the
table), and one test asserts that. No recipe ID is exempted. All 82 recipes pass.

plates. The four shipped circuit plate recipes name the item instead (`V180CircuitPlateData.java:48,55`
and `V180C16cMachineData.java:68`). The wafer is a component, not a material form, so my test does
not flag it, and it does not affect this row. I changed no recipe; Root decides.
