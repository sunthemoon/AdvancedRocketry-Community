## Selection

for every other row. In those rounds `per-step-results.json` holds 93 steps with the result `P_PENDING`,

all matched.

## Method

- For each step I read its `original` text, and the row in `CAMPAIGN-CHECKLISTS-*.md` where the text was
  short. The text after the separator is the oracle.
- I read the machine readbacks of the step from the session `results.jsonl` and say in each judgment
  whether they agree with the frames. No readback was rescored.
- I opened 246 distinct native frames and cite only those. I also viewed 15 sheet pages for an overview.
  The table below gives the native frames opened per row. Rows that repeat one screen across display
  cells or states (J16, A13, C06, E05, F07, H06, I07, J19, B06) were sampled. The frames that were
  neither opened natively nor seen on an opened sheet page are a gap, described under "Not done".
- Every `VISUAL_FAIL` was confirmed on native frames. No verdict rests on a file name, a caption or a
  log line alone.
- Three helper scripts under `J/tools/` supported the reading. `crophash.ps1` hashes the decoded pixels
  of a screen region; it showed that 120 E05 frames and 48 B06 frames are or are not pixel-identical in
  the region that matters. `brighten.ps1` and `cropzoom.ps1` wrote ten viewing aids to `J/tools/aids/`
  for dark sky frames and small models. The verdicts cite the native frames, not the aids.
- My own rule for oracles with a part that cannot appear on screen (it is an interpretation, not part of
  the TASK): the verdict is `VISUAL_PASS` when the visible part is shown and an agreeing readback covers
  the rest, and `STILL_PENDING` when the oracle names something visible that no frame shows.
- None of the 93 steps asks for sound in its text, so no verdict has a sound half.
