### 1. Sealed hopper test: tick count against wall-clock proof work

The diagnosis holds. The fixture vetoes the first proof on purpose, and the second proof then has
`ATTEMPT_TICKS = 100` for its store, force and readback. These run on the IO worker and the readback thread in
wall-clock time. On unpaced ticks of about 2 ms the deadline expired first. The fixture now calls `pacer.pace()` at the
start of its single `onEachTick` callback, as the W152H and release fixtures do. The 400-tick budget, the 100-tick proof
deadline, the 200-tick retry and every assertion are unchanged. The 60-tick automation checks count ticks and are
unaffected.

This failure was not forced locally, because it depends on the host's tick speed. Its timing was measured instead, with
the GameTest progress line printed every 20 ticks:

- Unpaced, on the local red run, the batch ended after 16 progress lines (about 320 of its 400 ticks) in 1.7 seconds.
  The hosted run `bb0eda86f` took 17 lines in 1.3 seconds, and the failing hosted run `5796ebc01` took 20.
- Paced, it ends after 15 lines (about 300 ticks) in about 15 seconds on every run: diagnostic, three repeats and
  the full run (14 lines there). That is the deterministic part, the 60-tick automation phase plus the 200-tick retry
  plus the 20-tick tail. The second proof now completes within a tick or two of its admission, so about 100 ticks of
  the budget stay free whatever the host's speed.

### 2. Leaked landed Moon rockets

The diagnosis holds. `EscrowRetireBeforeFillGameTests` cached the Moon arrival while its record existed and discarded
that object after F8 had released the flight tickets. By then the column had usually unloaded, so the discard hit a
stale instance (`RocketEntity.remove` still logs `REMOVED reason=DISCARDED`) and the copy on disk loaded again later.

- **Leak shown first.** The fixture now ends with a reload check: after the discard it lets the arrival's column
  unload, loads it again, waits for its entity sections and fails if the arrival's UUID, or any rocket of its logical
  ID, is in the column. The red run applied this check to the base behaviour, which held no column and discarded
  the cached instance. All three `escrow_retire_before_fill` tests then failed with `The discarded Moon arrival ...
  came back from disk (1 copies in [0, 0])`, `[4, 0]` and `[-4, 0]`: Moon pads 0, 1 and 2. The same run went on to
  reproduce failures 2 to 4:
  - the margin test in `machine_menu_channel` failed with `DESTINATION_LOADING null`, after its PREPARE used 3
    candidates;
  - both passenger tests failed with `No value present`, after `DESTINATION_PAD_BLOCKED` fail-backs.

  With the fix, the same check passes in every run, and every Moon PREPARE in the targeted runs selects its first
  candidate.
- **Fix.** The fixture holds its own ticket (`FluidFuelLoaderEscrowFixtures.Area`, radius 2) on the arrival's column
  from the first tick it sees the landed rocket, while the flight's tickets still hold it. It discards
  `moon.getEntity(arrivalId)` while that column's entity sections are loaded, then runs the reload check. The batch-end
  cleanup also discards by UUID in the held column, so a failed or timed-out scenario does not leave its arrival
  behind. The check has its own bound of 200 paced ticks inside the unchanged 2,400-tick timeout.
- **Shared pad cleanup.** `clearPadRocketsWhenLoaded(helper, driver, ticks, then, levels...)` primes the pads, waits
  paced and bounded until each fixed pad's chunk and its eight neighbours have loaded entity sections, discards every
  loaded rocket in those Levels and continues; at the bound it fails. The pads sit in the middle of their chunks
  (`FIXED_FEET_POSITION` is 8, 80, 8 and the pads are 64 blocks apart), so the 3 by 3 check covers a parked rocket.
  It replaces the unwaited clear before the flight in every fixture that primed and then cleared the pads:

  | Fixture (batch) | Bound | Timeout and existing waits |
  |---|---|---|
  | `RocketFlightGameTests` (`flight`) | 100 | 1,000; the clears at ticks 20 and 50 are replaced by the waiting clear at tick 80 |
  | `RocketFlightGameTests` (`flight_concurrency`) | 100 | 500 |
  | `RocketFlightSecurityGameTests` (`flight_security`) | 40 | 200; about 140 ticks used, so a shorter bound. Earth's pads are now primed as well as cleared. |
  | `MoonArrivalGameTests` (both batches) | 100 | 1,000; the clear at tick 80 now waits |
  | `ClassicMilestoneAdvancementGameTests` (`classic_moon_landing`) | 60 | 800 against 700 of existing waits |
  | `MoonLandingCase` (`moon_landing_rule`) | 100 | 9,000 |

  The immediate clears at test start that some fixtures kept only discard rockets that are already loaded; the waiting
  clear is the one that runs before the flight. `RocketTransferRecoveryGameTests` primes the pads but never clears them
  before it flies; its `clearRecoveryScenario` discards the rockets it has just counted as loaded, so it is unchanged.
- **Passenger fixtures.** `afterLanding` in `RocketPassengerContinuedUseGameTests` and
  `RocketPassengerPersistenceGameTests` uses `RocketFlightGameTestFixtures.landedDestinationId`. Without a destination
  identity it fails with the transfer, the record's phase and the transfer service's last diagnostic branch from
  `RocketRuntime.transferFailureDiagnostics`, for example `PREPARED ... last service branch DESTINATION_UNAVAILABLE`.
  The record has no reason field; `DESTINATION_UNAVAILABLE` is the branch that leads to the `DESTINATION_PAD_BLOCKED`
  fail-back, and the message says so.

### 3. Autosave inside B0's window

- **Autosave period.** `javap -c` of `MinecraftServer.tickServer` in
  `forge-1.20.1-47.4.10_mapped_official_1.20.1.jar` shows `tickCount++`, `tickChildren(...)`, then
  `tickCount % 6000 == 0` (`sipush 6000; irem; ifne`) followed by `saveEverything(true, false, false)`.
  `tickChildren` runs `GameTestTicker.SINGLETON.tick()` after the levels, and `GameTestServer.tickServer` calls the
  superclass first. A GameTest callback in tick N therefore sees the autosave at the end of the first tick whose count
  is a multiple of 6,000, counting N itself. The disassembly is kept in the evidence.
- **Reproduced.** A diagnostic patch made the base fixture call `server.saveEverything(true, false, false)` (the
  autosave's own call) in the first fixture tick in which the coordinator's B0 entry was `capturing`. The
  coordinator logged `state=B0`, then `state=B0_FAILED` one second later, and the test failed with the hosted
  message `S2 completed before the retained-rider departure was exercised`. That reproduces failure 5 deterministically
  and confirms that a vanilla save inside B0's window drops the admission.
- **Gate.** `GameTestAutosave.ticksUntil(tickCount)` is `Math.floorMod(-tickCount, 6000)`; `within(server, window)` is
  true while that is at most `window`. The boarding fixture admits only when the next autosave is more than
  `CAPTURE_WINDOW_TICKS = 60` tick ends away. B0 normally ends about 20 paced ticks after admission, with its Level's
  20-tick force, so 60 ticks is three force batches. The fixture's own native saves (`saveSlot`, for the old copies
  before admission and the new assignment after placement) use the same gate, because an autosave would supersede them
  too and the fixture treats that as `Native fixture save failed`. Only one autosave can fall in a run, so at most one
  wait of up to 61 ticks happens, inside the fixture's existing 380-tick bound (about 306 ticks used on the hosted
  green run `9ae106795`). B1's deliberate `storeChunkSections` is unchanged.
- **Truthful assertion.** When the fence lifts, the fixture first asserts that the admission reached B1 or published
  its S1 batch (`coordinator.stage(release)` is not `NONE`). Otherwise it reports
  `B0 dropped the admission before S1 (B0_FAILED): a native save superseded the B0 capture, so S2 never ran`.
  With the same forced save applied to the fixed fixture, the test fails with that message instead, and the other 8
  tests of the run pass. The repeats and the full run show the normal B0, S1, E1, CAPTURE_FAILED, B1, J1, S2 sequence
  with exactly the one declared B1 `CAPTURE_FAILED` WARN.

## Fixtures that keep a rocket across ticks

Method: every fixture that flies to the Moon or to Earth (`RocketDestination.MOON` or `EARTH`, or a Moon or Earth
body-surface target) and every fixture that places a rocket on the Moon was read for where it discards its rockets. A
discard is safe when the instance is looked up in the same tick, or when its column stays held until the discard.

- **Fixed:** `EscrowRetireBeforeFillGameTests`, the only one that discarded a cached arrival on a shared Moon pad after
  its column could unload.
- **Shared pads, safe:**
  - `EscrowGovernedMarginGameTests`, `EscrowGovernedWarmupGameTests`, `EscrowGovernedWarmupRefusalGameTests` and
    `EscrowMonitorGameTests` add an observation ticket when the record leaves and look the arrival up by UUID each
    tick.
  - `EscrowPendingLandingGameTests` reads its arrivals by UUID each tick under pad tickets.
  - `EscrowMoonLandingRuleGameTests` holds tickets on the destination columns until its cleanup.
  - `RocketSettlementIdleGameTests` holds `FLIGHT_FIXTURE` tickets on both ends until `close()`.
  - `RocketFlightGameTests`, `MoonArrivalGameTests`, `RocketLegacyFuelGameTests`, `RocketJournalInterlockGameTests`,
    `RocketPropulsionGameTests`, `RocketMonitorGameTests`, `RocketTransferRecoveryGameTests`,
    `ClassicMilestoneAdvancementGameTests` and both passenger fixtures find the arrival and discard it in the same
    callback.
  - `MoonLandingCase` discards by scanning loaded entities in its pass or fail listener. On a pass that runs in the
    tick of the landing check. After a timeout it could miss an unloaded arrival; the next case's and the other
    fixtures' pad cleanups now wait for entity sections, so that rocket is cleared before the next Moon flight.
- **Not on shared pads:** `FirstMoonLandingGameTests` and `MoonConcurrentLandingGameTests` (far Moon origins under
  `FORCED` tickets, discarded before the ticket is removed), `RocketPredecessorLaunchGameTests` (far Moon origin under
  `keepLoaded` tickets), `RocketArrivalFootprintGameTests`, `RocketDestinationReadinessGameTests` and
  `RocketLandedDisassemblyFixture` (far origins under fixture tickets), `EscrowFillLaunchGameTests` (own area; the
  launch is cancelled), `RocketFlightSecurityGameTests`, `RocketReleaseBoardingGameTests` and
  `EscrowCargoPreparationGameTests` (rockets in the force-loaded test structure), and `EscrowRetireBeforeFillGameTests`'
  own `rocket` and `oldCopy` (its held overworld area and a far old origin).

## Choices where the task was open

- The "code" in the passenger assertion is the transfer service's diagnostic branch, because the record stores no
  fail-back reason.
- The arrival's column is held from the first tick the landed rocket is visible, not from PREPARED, so the fixture
  does not change the flight's own destination readiness.
- The B0 window is 60 ticks, not B0's 200-tick deadline: waiting up to 200 ticks would not fit the fixture's 380-tick
  bound. B0 has never taken more than about 20 paced ticks in the logs.
- The boarding fixture's own native saves use the same gate. The task named only B0; the saves fail the test the same
  way and the gate costs nothing when no autosave is near.

## Not done, other flaky tests and risks

- **Residual autosave exposure in the boarding test.** The coordinator's B1 retry starts 200 ticks after the deliberate
  `CAPTURE_FAILED`, at a tick the fixture does not choose. An autosave inside that retry's capture window, about 20 of
  6,000 ticks, would cost another 200-tick retry and exceed the fixture's 380-tick bound. Gating the admission against
  that later window as well would cost up to 80 more ticks, which do not fit the bound, so it is not done.
- **Pad 0 before the passenger batches.** In the full run, as on the green hosted run, the selector refused Moon
  pad 0 for both passenger batches (2 candidates), so something from an earlier fixture still blocks or occupies it.
  Pad 1 was free and both tests passed. The cause was not traced, because the task keeps other leftovers out of
  scope.
- **Tighter pad bounds.** `flight_security` (40 ticks) and `classic_moon_landing` (60 ticks) have short pad bounds to
  fit their timeouts. Loading primed pads from disk took a few paced ticks in every run, but a filtered run in which
  one of them is the first to generate Moon terrain could reach the bound. It would then fail with
  `The fixed pads' entity sections did not load ...` rather than silently.
- **Other flaky tests:** none failed in the complete run.
- **Not done:** no change to production, ADRs, the risk register, `build.gradle`, status files or the ledger. Full
  `clean build`, `runData`, a native restart and visual gates were not run for this fixture slice. Root still merges,
  runs the hosted regression and arranges any review. No Required Gate is approved by this record.
