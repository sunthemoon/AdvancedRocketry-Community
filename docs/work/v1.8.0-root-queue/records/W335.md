## Contract established before the change

Root's reading is correct. `DESTINATION_LOADING` does not promise an already
selected warm-up. In
[RocketTransferService](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/rocket/server/RocketTransferService.java),
lines 148-161, an unresolved terrain request returns that code, and a terrain
demand enters `prepareTerrain`. In
[RocketDestinationWarmups](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/rocket/server/RocketDestinationWarmups.java),
lines 131-140, successful `terrain.start` also returns `DESTINATION_LOADING`.
[RocketPadTerrainPreparation](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/rocket/server/RocketPadTerrainPreparation.java),
lines 38-49 and 51-80, first acquires capacity and footprint tickets, then polls
FULL columns and advances at most one selection per tick. Only successful
selection calls `completeTerrain`, which creates WARMING in
[RocketWarmupRegistry](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/rocket/transfer/RocketWarmupRegistry.java),
lines 296-320. Waiting does not authorize or continue a launch.

The W207 notes in [W90-DESIGN sections 7.1-7.4](W90-DESIGN.md) explicitly distinguish
TERRAIN from the selected warm-up, retain the original allocation deadline and
require a fresh launch request. [W207's design and implementation record](W207.md)
documents that asynchronous promotion and the earlier sibling fixture corrections.
W330's reported STARTED, failure and SELECTED ordering agrees with this contract;
there is no production finding requiring a production edit.

## Completed changes and design decisions

Commit `aee2adf0e4997fb4c8423bd4d957e936adddc584`
(`test(rocket): await bounded terrain preparation before warmup checks`) changes:

- [EscrowMonitorGameTests](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/resourceescrow/EscrowMonitorGameTests.java).
  Step 10 checks the unchanged no-journal and exact-fuel assertions on every
  tick, including the pre-selection interval. Before observing a warm-up it may
  wait only while this rocket's terrain preparation exists. Every observed
  warm-up must belong to the offline monitor owner. Once observed, its absence
  fails even if a new terrain allocation exists. READY, the original request's
  20-tick hold, the fresh edge, F1/F2, arrival and exactly-once debit assertions
  remain unchanged. The timeout is still 2,400 ticks.
- [EscrowGovernedWarmupGameTests](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/resourceescrow/EscrowGovernedWarmupGameTests.java).
  The capacity fixture's station retry after a removal has the same weakness:
  the station was predicted in an earlier tick, so its FULL columns can be
  unavailable by the retry. It now submits that retry once, waits only for its
  pending terrain allocation and retains the exact station-level, ticket-count
  and eight-live-warm-up assertions before proceeding to logout. No timeout,
  expiry bound, quota or other assertion changed.

This is not a loosened safety assertion: it distinguishes the two documented
stages instead of requiring the second stage one tick after the first. Missing
preparation, missing or wrongly attributed warm-ups, launch/debit on a held
edge, and failure to finish within the existing timeout remain failures. The
original allocation clock is not restarted. No ADR choice or new production
contract was necessary.
