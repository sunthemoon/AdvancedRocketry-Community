against its before image; normal recovery is then checked on both restarts.

cut attempts. Complete UUID/owner, snapshot/native resources, fuel and debit
history, unrelated transfer and pad payloads are compared, not just entity
counts or callback logs. The oracle receives mutation tests before use.
