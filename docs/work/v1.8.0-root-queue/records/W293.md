## Implementation and files

- The satellite terminal and builder, rocket satellite bay, electrolyzer, railgun, elevator,
  laser drill, laser target, black-hole generator and microwave receiver draw two-tone frames
  from every menu slot, using the existing `ContainerSlotBackground` helper.
- The terminal gives Launch, Claim and Cancel enough space, moves the removal controls to a
  second row, and uses a short bilingual power label. The builder uses its own ready labels.
  A successful build with an occupied output is shown as ready, but another refused build
  retains its real `OUTPUT_BLOCKED` result. Actual assembly eligibility is unchanged.
- `SatelliteTerminalMenuData` refreshes the displayed historical `PENDING_DISCOVERY` result
  when the selected satellite's server state is idle again. Discovery completion clears the
  satellite's current-mission reference; checking for a current `CLAIMED` mission would not
  work. Missing or held registry data does not become success, and `REGISTRY_PENDING` is not
  changed. The stored terminal result and discovery/registry persistence are untouched.
- The bay's player inventory moves down twelve pixels. Its notices and the flight console's
  route use bounded text with full hover text. The electrolyzer charge slot moves to the
  unused left-hand area inside its panel. All slot indices and filters are unchanged.
- The cargo gauges move above the receive row instead of moving railgun/elevator slots.
  Every positive charge has at least one visible pixel. Titles, status text and receiver
  output are bounded; the drill and generator reserve two rows for their status.
- The drill's full diagnostic tooltip wraps to the screen width and has a height-bounded,
  wheel-scrollable viewport with a bilingual row indicator. Current `OK` suppresses old
  stop/confirmation notices, without editing the server's historical stop code.
- `WarpControllerMenu` and `StationNoticeService` use the existing body translation keys for
  orbit, target and countdown names. Notice envelopes, arguments, codec and budgets do not
  change. Unknown datapack body names retain their existing ID fallback.
- New text comes from `V180ScreenLayoutLanguage` through `V180LanguageProvider`. Only v1.8
  generated `en_us.json` and `zh_cn.json` change, with five entries each. No older generated
  root is edited.

The Java files above are under `src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/`.
`ScreenText`, `CargoScreenLayout`, `SatelliteScreenLabels`, `EndgameScreenLabels`,
`TooltipPageLayout` and `ScrollableTooltip` hold the small shared display helpers. No ADR
decision needs changing; these are display choices within the task's authority.
