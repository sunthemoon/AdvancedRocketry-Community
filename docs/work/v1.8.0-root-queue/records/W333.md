owner polling, `restartForTests` and `unqueueForTests`. No production seam is added. Registry,
knowledge and station substitutions are restored, and owned files, entities and tickets are
cleaned up. The F2 matrix is serial so its clock/retention variants cannot overlap.

research. The source really is in Space with a station target. The station resolver captures
Earth, the destination body is Moon, and a checked station relocation changes the orbit to Mars
before D0. REGISTERED recovery additionally exercises DATA and solar after the physical catalog
is made unavailable. RT.7 combines obsolete registry-session evidence with the real consumption
step's four stale-hash refusals and obsolete/one-use source-permit execution checks.

Reload and coordinator restart seams are not an actual process restart or native acceptance.
Detached coalesced/autosave transport fixtures do not replace the existing live tick-publication
tests. No ADR decision was changed or new exemption taken.
