### Observed cause and selected fix (2026-10-23, before behavior changes)

The diagnostic commit `e0b1f4fcf0ec954a55d09804b60f23891037e89a` reproduced all eight
F1 callback discards in `native-before-03/native/start.log`, lines 288–313, at
15:50:07 +08:00, coordinator tick 176. Every observation had `closed=false`,
`unavailable=false`, exactly three ordinary admissions, no transitions, zero queued
or active flight requests, and writer active/successor/completions/running/unknown
all false or zero. Writer generation and durable generation both equalled 4.
Thus the exact rejecting predicate was `!queued.isEmpty()`, not writer failure,
flight capacity or a transition. The three ordinary operation UUIDs are retained
in each raw log line; they were not inferred from ledger topology.

Separate admission from capture readiness: admit into the existing flight queue
while unrelated ordinary admissions/transitions drain. Keep `tick`'s empty ordinary
queue/transition checks, writer-idle/availability checks, ten-tick cadence, proof
and journal lease checks, scope validation and capacity preflight unchanged. A
waiting request holds its existing logical lock and proof; it does not acquire a
serial or acknowledge before capture. Actual queue refusals will have a typed
reason reported by the F1 caller, rather than treating them as an unspecified wait.
Tests will cover ordinary and transition contention, retained proof invalidation,
exact acknowledged-root composition, capacity and duplicate/closed refusals.
