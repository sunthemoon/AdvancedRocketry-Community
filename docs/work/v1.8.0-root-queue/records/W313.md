## Matched reproduction and cause

The reproduction is the **local unsharded full run**, not the initial filtered
experiment. Both full runs execute 1,751 tests and place FIFO's anchor at
`(228, -56, 1465)` in seed `0`.

- Before, at `16da88631`, only FIFO fails. Its first winner is exactly the reported
  candidate 29 at `(229.5, -56.0, 1464.5)`, with 29 probes and 1,305 reads.
- The census finds no fluid in any of the 31 windows. Each window receives its
  full 45 reads from an available column. Gravel occupies the feet of the first
  eight ground columns. The saved-chunk read corroborates gravel at y -56 through
  -53 over their stone supports; it is explicitly a post-failure saved observation,
  not a tick-exact snapshot.
- Candidates `5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26` intersect this gravel. Their raised
  alternatives `6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27` have falling-block support and gravel
  in their bodies. Their lowered alternatives intersect the stone pad. Candidate
  29 is the first clear column. The refusal precedes entity traversal, so neither
  entities nor unloaded chunks explain these rejections.
- The fixture cleared those feet explicitly before waiting over 70 server ticks,
  but left its shaft open above y +54 and used neighbor-notifying writes. In the
  matched after run, the recorded original ceiling at `(228, -1, 1465)` is gravel.
  These observations identify falling terrain refilling the fixture, rather than
  a production selector or budget defect.
- After, at `d15681bc3`, FIFO at the same anchor selects candidates **5 and 8**,
  spending 225 and 360 reads respectively, with unchanged attempt/capture order.
  All 1,751 required tests pass.

The initial filtered attempt passed one test at anchor `(3, -56, 3)`. Its
`--spawnPos 225,-60,1462` changed world spawn, not vanilla's hard-coded test-grid
origin. It is retained as an unsuccessful placement experiment, not reproduction
