## Design and files changed

- `atmosphere/life/PlayerLifeSupportInput.java`, `PlayerLifeSupportDecision.java`, and
  `PlayerLifeSupportEngine.java` add a finite underwater demand and an air-restoration decision. The
  existing five-argument constructors retain dry-land behavior. One shared phase advances for vacuum,
  water, or both; a scheduled successful one-unit payment is the only air-restoration decision.
- `atmosphere/life/SuitReserveTransition.java` carries underwater demand through the existing reserve
  replenishment and single debit. Breathable ambient air does not satisfy underwater demand, but water
  alone never causes vacuum damage.
- `compat/atmosphere/SuitEquipmentService.java` uses the pure unpaid re-evaluation after a rejected
  commit. Failed commits cannot return an underwater air grant.
- `atmosphere/server/PlayerLifeSupportService.java` samples native eye submersion, applies only a
  committed air grant, and keeps the existing ServerPlayer-only listener. It does not cancel or replace
  native breathing/drowning events.
- `gametest/UnderwaterBreathingGameTests.java` and `UnderwaterBreathingFixture.java` exercise real
  connected survival ServerPlayers, actual water, and native player ticks through the registered listener.
- `src/test/java/.../atmosphere/life/UnderwaterLifeSupportTest.java` adds seven pure tests. The preceding
  production paths are beneath `src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/`.

The existing `equipment.classicEnabled` switch governs this new equipment behavior; no configuration key
was added. Water breathing and conduit power suppress water-only suit demand. Respiration, turtle helmet
effects, aquatic air handling, and vanilla drowning remain native. Air is restored only on the paid tick,
including the last available oxygen unit, not speculatively during the preceding 19 ticks. Existing vacuum
consumption remains enabled when classic equipment is disabled. There is no new schema, public/persisted
ID, packet, registry entry, language string, generated asset, or upstream import.
