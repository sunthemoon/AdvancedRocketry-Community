## 1. Suit tank tiers

**ADR.** Section 5.1 (lines 606 to 640): pressure tank schema 1 with gas `EMPTY|OXYGEN|HYDROGEN`, an integer
stored amount and a snapshotted maximum; proposal tiers 1,000/2,000/4,000/8,000; one oxygen and one hydrogen
tank at most; default combined oxygen 10,000; with the multiplier 32,000 per tank and 34,000 combined.
Section 9 item 1 asks for this review.

**Shipped.**

- `equipment/suit/TankData.java` lines 16 to 24 give the bases 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 and 8,000. Line 13 caps any
  tank at 32,000. Lines 25 to 39 accept a saved maximum only if it is the base times 1 to 4, and refuse every
  other maximum without clamping.
- `equipment/suit/ModuleLayout.java` lines 20 to 21 allow two tanks per chest and never two of the same gas.
- `atmosphere/life/SuitReserveTransition.java` lines 35 to 39 and 51 to 57 move at most the missing active amount
  (at most 2,000) from the oxygen tank, only on the scheduled debit tick, only in unbreathable air.
  `compat/atmosphere/SuitEquipmentService.java` lines 84 to 96 publish both edits in one armor tag.
- Tests: `TankDataTest` (lines 10 to 31, all four bases times 1 to 4 and refused maxima), `SuitReserveTransitionTest`
  (ten cases), and the GameTests `SuitWorkstationGameTests.duplicateTypesAndGasLimitsRefuseWithoutMutation`,
  `equippedSuitAndWorkstationSurviveNativeSaveLoad` and `reserveQueriesArePureAndScheduledDebitIsConservative`.

**Legacy.** `item/components/ItemPressureTank.java` lines 33 to 36 and 75 to 77: capacity is the constructor value
times 2 to the power of the metadata. `AdvancedRocketry.java` line 507 constructs four variants with
`1000 * suitTankCapacity`. The chest has no buffer of its own; its air is the oxygen in its tanks
(`armor/ItemSpaceChest.java` lines 56 to 68 and 229 to 262), and it has six module slots
(`AdvancedRocketry.java` line 514), so two oxygen tanks were allowed.

**Comparison.** The numbers match exactly, but a shipped unit lasts twice as long as a legacy mB:

| Configuration (default multiplier) | Legacy in vacuum | Shipped in vacuum |
|---|---|---|
| Chest with no tank | 0 | 2,000 units, 33 min 20 s |
| One 1,000 tank | 8 min 20 s | 16 min 40 s, plus the 2,000 active units |
| One 8,000 tank | 66 min 40 s | 133 min 20 s, plus the 2,000 active units |
| Largest suit | two 8,000 tanks, 133 min 20 s | 2,000 + 8,000 units, 166 min 40 s |

Halving the tiers would match legacy per tank but leave the largest suit at 100 minutes, below legacy, because D1
forbids a second oxygen tank. The shipped numbers keep the top end within 25 percent of legacy. I recommend no
change.

## 2. `OXYGEN.suitTankCapacity` (and `spaceSuitO2Buffer`)

**ADR.** Lines 627 to 639: an integer multiplier 1 to 4, default 1, snapshotted per created tank; lowering it keeps
stored gas and saved maxima; unsupported saved maxima are refused, not clamped. Lines 631 to 634:
`spaceSuitO2Buffer` becomes the built-in active target, 1 to 2,000 units, default 2,000.

**Shipped.** `config/CommonConfig.java` lines 368 to 373 define both keys with exactly those ranges and defaults;
line 410 falls back to 1 before the configuration loads. `CommonConfigTest` lines 160 to 161 pin both. The
snapshot is written at the first of: crafting or the default instance (`equipment/suit/SuitModuleItem.java`
lines 15 to 25), or the first workstation install of a tank without a root, for example a `/give` stack
(`equipment/suit/ArmorEquipment.java` lines 45 to 51). An installed tank must carry its snapshot
(`equipment/suit/ArmorRootCodec.java` lines 38 to 44). I read "snapshotted per created tank" as "at the first
initialization", which is the conservative reading.

**Legacy.** `api/ARConfiguration.java` line 442: a float, default 1.0, range 0 to `Float.MAX_VALUE`. It is applied
once when the item is constructed at startup (`AdvancedRocketry.java` line 507), so after a restart every old and
new tank follows the new value. A value of 0 makes tanks useless, and large values overflow the integer
capacity. Legacy `spaceSuitO2Buffer` (line 441, default 30 "minutes") set the maximum `air` of space-breathing
enchanted armor to 36,000 (`util/ItemAirUtils.java` lines 112 to 114), which lasts about five hours at one unit
per 10 vacuum ticks. Its documented meaning (30 minutes) is close to the shipped 2,000 units (33 min 20 s).

**Verdict.** Agrees with the ADR. The narrower range and the per-tank snapshot are deliberate ADR choices. There is
no configuration import from 1.12.2, so legacy fractional values need no migration.

## 3. The scrubber

### 3.1 The fourth-debit schedule

**ADR.** Section 3.1 lines 169 to 176 and section 9 item 5: one usable scrubber skips one debit in two, two give
at most three skips in four, and the fourth debit is mandatory (owner decision D5).

**Shipped.** `atmosphere/device/ScrubberSavings.java` lines 6 to 12: one usable scrubber skips phases 0 and 2;
two or more skip phases 0, 1 and 2; phase 3 always pays. `atmosphere/device/ScrubberBank.java` lines 50 to 57
count a scrubber as usable when it is supplied and either holds a charged cartridge or
`OXYGEN.scrubberRequiresCartrige` is false; up to six faces count, and more than two behave as two.
`atmosphere/vent/OxygenVentBlockEntity.java` lines 100 to 103 replace only the oxygen debit; the FE of
`atmosphere/life/VentSupplyEngine.java` lines 25 to 33 (one unit per 20 ticks, 20 FE per tick) is still paid.
Tests: `LifeSupportArithmeticTest` lines 7 to 18 (400 debits for 0 to 6 devices: 400, 200 and 100 paid; every
fourth pays) and `LifeSupportDeviceGameTests` lines 28 to 49 (80 ticks: two or one paid units, 80 x 20 FE, two or
three cartridge uses).

**Legacy.** `tile/atmosphere/TileOxygenVent.java` lines 346 to 347: the gas multiplier is
`max(0.01 - 0.005 * scrubbers, 0)`, so one scrubber halves consumption and two make it zero. Line 254 drains in
proportion to the sealed volume every tick.

**Verdict.** One scrubber matches legacy exactly. Two scrubbers keep a quarter of the debit instead of none, as D5
chose. Agrees with the ADR.

### 3.2 The cartridge balance

**ADR.** Lines 166 to 168: proposal 32,766 uses; lines 175 to 176: one use per skipped debit, committed with the
phase.

**Shipped.** `atmosphere/device/CarbonCartridge.java` line 14 (`MAX_USES = 32_766`), line 20 (a cartridge without a
root is full) and lines 26 to 29 (one use per spend). `ScrubberBank.java` lines 59 to 72 spend one use per skip
from the first charged supplied face at or after the old phase index. The spent-cartridge refresh is the chemical
reactor's special recipe (40 ticks at 20 FE per tick; `CarbonCartridge.java` lines 35 to 37); a new cartridge is
four iron plates and a carbon brick.

**Legacy.** `AdvancedRocketry.java` line 488 sets the cartridge's maximum damage to `Short.MAX_VALUE - 1`, which is
32,766 uses. `tile/atmosphere/TileCO2Scrubber.java` lines 26 to 38 spend one use per call, and
`TileOxygenVent.java` lines 243 to 250 call it for every scrubber every 200 ticks while the room is sealed,
whatever oxygen is saved.

**Comparison.** The count matches. The rate does not:

| Scrubbers | Legacy use rate | Shipped use rate | Legacy cartridge life | Shipped cartridge life |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 per 200 ticks | 1 per 40 ticks | about 91 hours | about 18.2 hours |
| 2 | 1 per 200 ticks each | 3 per 80 ticks in total | about 91 hours each | about 27 to 30 hours for the pair, depending on the faces (see O3) |

One use saves exactly one oxygen unit, so a full cartridge saves 32,766 units, about 33 canisters. Because the
refresh costs only 800 FE and returns the same cartridge, I recommend keeping 32,766 (finding F2).

## 5. Findings for Root

- **F1 (context, already known).** No shipped route puts gas into a pressure tank, so the tiers, the D1 reserve and
  jetpack hydrogen cannot be reached in survival until the gas charge pad lands (ADR-066 D4). W06's record (line 132)
  and [W174-DESIGN](W174-DESIGN.md) lines 166 to 168 already say so. The tier review above is therefore numeric
  only; balance in play waits for the pad.
- **F2 (balance, no change recommended).** Cartridges drain 3.75 to 5 times faster than legacy (section 3.2). If the
  owner wants legacy-like cartridge life, the alternatives are a larger use count or a different spend rule; both
  change ADR-066's proposal values and need an owner decision.
- **F3 (scope source).** W172's N9 overstates the gap: the in-place ADR-025 note exists (section 4).
- **O1 (scrubber energy).** Legacy charged `(scrubbers * 10 + 1) * OxygenVentPowerMultiplier` FE per tick
  (`TileOxygenVent.java` lines 168 to 170, multiplier default 1.0 at `ARConfiguration.java` line 440): 1, 11 and 21
  FE per tick for 0, 1 and 2 scrubbers. The shipped vent pays a flat 20 FE per tick (`AtmosphereLimits.java` line 19)
  and scrubbers add nothing. ADR-066 does not mention scrubber energy. At two scrubbers the costs are within 1 FE per
  tick, so I recommend no change.
- **O2 (stale test name).** `OxygenVentPersistenceTest.schemaOneRoundTripsEveryFixedField` encodes schema 2 since
  W14. Real schema-1 coverage is `LifeSupportCodecTest` line 14. Renaming is a test change outside this task.
- **O3 (cartridge order).** With two scrubbers the spend starts at the face index equal to the old phase (0 to 2). A
  pair on faces 4 and 5 (west and east, as in the GameTest) drains the west cartridge alone, about 12 hours, after
  which one usable scrubber remains and the saving falls to one half for about 18 more hours. A pair on faces 0 and 1
  splits the uses two to one. Each skip still costs exactly one use, as the ADR requires.
- **O4 (conservative failure).** `ScrubberBank.skipDebit` advances the phase before it spends. If the spend were
  refused (lines 65 to 66), the debit is paid and the skip is lost. Admission normalizes the cartridge root first
  (`ScrubberBank.java` line 141), so I found no reachable case.
