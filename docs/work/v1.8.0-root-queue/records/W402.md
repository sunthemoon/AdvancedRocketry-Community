The rules, as the generator applies them:

- **High-risk, all checked.** An asset is high-risk for any of these reasons, and the CSV names each one.
  - *OBJ model:* every `.obj` and `.mtl` file, every JSON model whose parent chain has the `forge:obj` loader, and
    the textures those bind.
  - *Multiblock:* for each of the 16 IDs under `data/advancedrocketrycommunity/machine_patterns/`, its blockstate,
    the models that blockstate names, its item model, their parents inside the JAR and their textures.
  - *Armour:* paths under `textures/models/armor/`, `textures/entity/suit_components`, `models/item/space_suit_`
    and `textures/item/space_suit_`, with the textures those models bind.
  - *Corrected:* W244's `models/spaceelevator.obj`, its MTL and `textures/models/spaceelevatorcapsule.png`; W261's
    landing-pad blockstate, block and item model, `rocketpad_.png` and the retained `landing_pad.png`; W265's
    `models/item/basic_laser_gun.json`, `basic_laser_gun.png` and `basiclasergun.png`.
  - A blockstate that names a high-risk model is high-risk as well.
- **Model family.** The family of a model is the parent it shares with other models: a parent outside the JAR
  (for example `minecraft:block/cube_all`), or a parent inside the JAR that at least two models inherit. An item
  model that is the only child of its own block's model takes that block model's family. A model without a parent
  is its own family. Block and item models are separate families, because they are seen in different places.
- **Pick.** In each family, the pick is the first path in ascending sorted order among the members that are not
  high-risk. 44 of the 62 families have a pick; the other 18 consist only of high-risk models.
- **Other files.** A texture or blockstate is SAMPLE when a picked model needs it, and FAMILY_MEMBER when only
  unpicked models do.
- **UNREFERENCED.** 48 textures are named by no model, blockstate or MTL in the JAR. Eight are fluid textures under
  `textures/block/fluid/`, which only code can name. The other 40 (for example `block/arc_furnace.png`,
  `item/jetpack.png`, `block/rocket_motor`-style `_top`, `_side` and `_bottom` tiles) look like retained earlier
  art. C30 has no family for them, so the campaign never looks at them unless Root says so.

## 6. Access matrix (C14, I-6)

`access-matrix.csv` has 35 rows: 28 menus, the three client-to-server messages of the
[network pin](../../../../src/test/resources/network-protocols.txt), and the four devices that checklist I11 names
but that have no menu. Each row gives who may open and act, the operator level, the distance check and its value,
the loaded-chunk check, the rate limit, the refusal message or log line, the cells that the GameTest menu matrix
exempts, and source locations. The generator finds each location by its source text on every run and fails if
the text is gone.

Summary of the policies:

- Every menu and every targeted intent uses a reach of 8 blocks. No handler loads a chunk from a client value: the
  three messages carry a container ID, an entity ID or nothing, never a position.
- No owner (any admitted player): the combustion generator, electrolyzer, rolling machine, precision assembler,
  solar generator, solar array, classic controllers, microwave receiver, satellite builder and suit workstation.
- Owner or operator level 2: the satellite terminal (an unowned one is open to all), the satellite bay, the rocket
  monitoring station, and the six endgame devices under ADR-054's table (station members get a public view only).
- Owner only, with no operator access to the menu: the fluid fuel loader and the four cargo devices.
- Station controllers: opened by the station's owner, a member or an operator; mutations are checked again by the
  station services.
- Rocket flight: any player riding or within 8 blocks may open, board and leave; launch and cancel need the owner,
  a creative player or operator level 2.

Findings that need Root:

1. **Most refusals leave no server log line.** C14 says "the server's log proves receipt and refusal". Only the
   rocket flight service logs (`ARCE_FLIGHT_INTENT`), and it skips two branches: an intent without an open valid
   flight menu, and a rate-limited intent that the limiter does not audit. `classic_controls` drops an intent for
   a wrong or invalid menu with no message and no log, and returns other refusals only in the view packet.
   `suit_motion` is silent for RATE_LIMITED and IGNORED. Endgame refusals are an action-bar status line only.
   Menus that are no longer valid are closed without a message. K18 therefore has to prove refusals by its own
   receipt record and unchanged state, or those branches are NOT_COVERED.
2. **`suit_motion` has no distance check.** This is the one channel without one. It has no target: the intent acts
   on the sender's own worn jetpack. I read it as not applicable and not a defect; Root decides.
3. **Checklist J15 step 1 quotes the old preview namespace** (section 5). The step fails as written on C4.
4. **I11 names four devices that have no menu and no intent**: the planetary beacon, landing pad, force-field
   projector and pump work by block use. Their rows are in the matrix. The projector lets only operators bind it,
   and a non-operator's use passes with no message.
5. **Eleven screens are named by no specific row** (section 4).
6. **48 textures are outside C30's sampling** (section 3).

## 7. The two resource packs

Both are built by the generator from the JAR's own paths, with `pack_format` 15. The ZIPs are stored (not
compressed), with sorted names and a fixed timestamp, so the same input gives the same bytes; the generator was
run twice on C4 and `diff -r` found the two output directories identical. `pack-contents.csv` lists every
override with the SHA-256 of the original and of the replacement.

**`k13-missing-resource-pack.zip`** (2,116 bytes, for I07 step 15). Six textures are replaced by a 54-byte text
that is not a PNG, and one model by a model whose parent `advancedrocketrycommunity:block/k13_missing_parent` does
not exist. All paths are under `assets/advancedrocketrycommunity/`.

| Override | What the campaign should see |
|---|---|
| `textures/models/railgun.png` | W241's fallback: the railgun's own controller face. |
| `textures/models/blackholegenerator.png` | W241's fallback: `railgun_front.png`. |
| `textures/models/rollingmachine.png` | W241's fallback: `lathe.png`. |
| `textures/models/spaceelevatorcapsule.png` | W244's fallback: the per-rider line cage. |
| `textures/models/armor/space_suit_layer_1.png` | No project fallback is promised: the game's own. |
| `textures/block/rocketpad_.png` | No project fallback is promised: the game's own. |
| `models/block/rocket_motor.json` | No runtime fallback is promised (I07 step 15): the game's missing model. |

**`k13-long-text-pack.zip`** (409,303 bytes, for J12 to J16). It overrides all 32 language files at their own
paths, with every key kept in its order. The rule for each value: the value, then a space and the value again
without its format specifiers, repeated until the result is at least twice as long, cut at 256 characters. The
first copy is never cut, so each placeholder appears exactly once and the number of `%` characters does not
change; I checked this for all 3,224 values. 3,220 values were lengthened. Four English values are already 256
characters or longer and stay as they are: `jei.advancedrocketrycommunity.rocket_satellite_bay` (330),
`jei.advancedrocketrycommunity.solar_array` (576), `message.advancedrocketrycommunity.bay_exit.capacity_or_used`
(257) and `message.advancedrocketrycommunity.bay_exit.fall_offer` (371).

## 9. Choices I made where the TASK was open

- Model family and pick: the rules of section 3.
- A "file that does not resolve" is a file that is present but cannot be decoded or linked, because a resource
  pack cannot delete a file of a pack below it.
- The long-text rule repeats the text without its format specifiers. Repeating the specifiers would ask for more
  arguments than the game supplies, and the game would then show the raw pattern.
- "Rows that cover a screen" is the keyword search of section 4.
- The source-derived tables are curated data inside the generator, checked against the source and the JAR on every
  run, because the write scope allows one generator file. That file is 1,267 lines, of which about 370 are tables.
