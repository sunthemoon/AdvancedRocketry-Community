## Findings beyond W107C's record

- **Starvation (found by reading; not run).** A held fill is retried every tick and takes the driver's one capture
  per tick. With at most eight records, the scheduler keeps the same visiting order, so it defers the first capture of
  every younger fill in another component. The note's R-4 addresses this. A unit test should confirm it before the
  fix.
- **F1b, the post-retirement floor fence.** Once a named fill retires, its floor makes `classify` fence the landed
  record with FLOOR_PROOF_REQUIRED at every start. That proof needs both ends already loaded. A rocket refuelled on the
  Moon then cannot launch until its Overworld launch site is loaded. W107C's fixture hides this because both ends are
  in the same Level, 16 blocks apart. This is decision D2 in the note.
- **Shutdown fencing.** At shutdown, every record is fenced ESCROW_PENDING_OR_TOMBSTONE, because a closed coordinator
  counts as locked. The note's self-guard predicate therefore requires an operational coordinator.
