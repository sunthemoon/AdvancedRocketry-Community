## Choices where the ADR was open

ADR-065 §10 leaves the exact disk authority, the save-observer limits and any residual to this
design. The choices below are explained in the design, and items D-1 to D-5 are listed there for
the reviewer.

- The authority is a separately forced ledger (`AtomicSavedData` plus `CheckedSavedDataFile`),
  not the entity file or the chunk writer. A ledger record is the commit point. Reconciliation
  only rolls forward, with no abort path once the record is durable.
- Endpoint durability is proven by a readback that runs before a completed
  `IOWorker.synchronize(true)`. No save event is used. This needs six Access Transformer entries
  and one `build.gradle` line, which belongs to Root (D-2).
- No torn-save residual is selected. Resources are kept and the record shows `REPAIR` whenever an
  outcome is lost or unknown.
- The rocket marker makes the rocket entity schema 3. A rocket with a passenger is refused,
  because a vehicle with exactly one player passenger is saved in that player's file (D-3).
- Transfer floors stop transfer recovery from rebuilding a refuelled landed rocket from its
  arrival snapshot (D-4).
- The target bank is selected automatically, and an ambiguous Fluid is refused. This avoids a new
  packet in W11b.
- For D-1, I recommend the linker's rocket-binding half over the ADR-027 six-block search.

## Known risks

- The vanilla storage facts come from bytecode signatures and call chains, not from running code.
  W11b's barrier GameTests are the first runtime check.
- The ledger force runs on the server thread, and the barrier's cost is unmeasured.
- An endpoint stalls in `BARRIER_STALLED` when its chunk record is stored externally or its chunk
  save is vetoed (the R-021 class).
- Entity schema 3 changes every rocket's saved form.
- Collecting floors only at server start could exhaust their 256 places during a long uptime.
- The ADR-027 `fuel_loader` and hand exchange keep their documented two-store limits, which are
  outside D3.
