**Carried-over fixes.** `884f4426c` moves D6's source serialization after END_LOADING, DUPLICATE_PENDING,
BARRIER_UNSUPPORTED and capture-availability checks; none of these waits needs a save. R65 widens the checked low
edge from `min - 1` to `min - 2` blocks, covering the home column of a 1.5-block-wide rocket whose box intersects
the lookup box. R1, R2, D6 and ticket release share that set. The maximum stays 16 columns per end; a placement that
now exceeds it is refused/held, never truncated. Existing boundary assertions are updated to the wider geometry,
with their count, arithmetic and overflow checks retained.

**Recognition and election.** Read T's exact source UUID before the bounded loaded queries at both recorded ends.
Count identities across both ends together, including blocked images naming L; 64 is a conflict, not a truncated
result. Recognition binds L, owner, the end's snapshot ID and before/after hash, physical end and neutral wrapper.
Unexpected, protected or observed outside-bound copies hold the claim for repair without deleting the copy or
retiring receipts. Prefer the exact source; a destination copy is never PREPARED authority. If absent after both
admissible ends load, reuse the bay constructor with T's original source UUID and altitude/TRANSIT authority.

**Consumption and removal.** Reuse the frozen group's S'/T' reconstruction and the existing snapshot-work budget.
D5 publishes only missing halves and consumes every recognized copy with its own end's after hash, preserving
REJECTED packages and existing consumed markers. Recheck identity, hash, physical end and escrow neutrality at each
effect. Remove only consumed neutral non-survivors, after a valid exact survivor exists and any assigned online
passengers are routed to it. Non-assigned passengers hold removal. A newly joining copy invalidates the group and
is processed again; no ordinary `keepOne` or global hash-tolerance exemption is used. W27d2's runtime-only
DUPLICATE_PENDING state needs no migration: the next start recognizes its existing receipts and copies.

**Absence and tokens.** Build a deduplicated census with the source origin first and every column of both checked
end sets, including the widened low strip. Include each recorded origin column explicitly when it is outside its
rectangle: the 16-column rectangle limit, W98's existing 129-slot census bound and shared 64-match conflict limit
remain unchanged. Loaded recognition checks that separate origin's home column as well; it does not join it to a larger
rectangle or acquire a ticket for it. Box overlap alone does not include a neighboring home column in this extra query.
Use W98's slot validators, capture port and ordinary barrier quota.
Every census column must be entity-loaded before capture, even an in-session source margin or non-admissible
destination that W27d2 did not await. No cold slot counts as absent. Missing Levels or invalid/over-budget sets
hold without extra tickets; a valid but unticketed slot waits for loading. Existing recorded ticket limits are
unchanged. The survivor slot must match frozen S' at altitude; all other slots must contain no L/source UUID,
including nested passengers. Keep every proof, the checked T' lease and the group token through D7's pre-replace
guard. A save, join, leave, movement or changed authority invalidates the set; release it and repeat D6. Cold-chunk
tests cover these waits rather than treating an unloaded entity index as absence.

**Passengers.** The existing manifest in T remains authority for offline seats. A login while the claim is held
stays queued, with no ordinary election, settlement or journal removal. Coordinator movement, when needed to
empty a recognized duplicate, is narrowly scoped to an online assigned passenger and the exact survivor;
escrow and release guards still apply. Offline players are not required for D6/D7 and later follow ordinary
acknowledged arrival. No assignment is cleared merely because everybody is online.

**Saved structures and limits.** Writes use existing receipt entries/removal, T's existing snapshots/checksum,
existing rocket entity payloads and entity-slot deletion, and vanilla passenger position/dimension on movement.
Registry registration remains W27d2's path; copies never register. No new field, schema, file, saved token, bound,
budget, quota or timeout is intended, and no line of `RocketTransferService` may change. Existing 64 matches,
16 columns per end, W98 census bound and barrier quotas, 32 coordinator steps and snapshot budgets apply. If these
cannot support the implementation, stop before extending them and report to Root.

**Verification.** New unit classes run alone, then the whole suite. Run all bay batches three times and native
B5, B6 copy variants, B7, and B3/B4 with copies/offline seats, with two restarts each and at most 60 counted starts
per tranche. Measure bounded recovery reads against existing quality budgets. All heavy commands use the machine
lock and 90-second hand-off after checking both drives and memory. Hosted regression replaces a full local
GameTest. Root supplies the risk-register update and independent read-only review; this worker approves no Gate.

### Choices where the review or the ADRs left room

- **R5's quiet-store wait applies only to a parked claim.** Gating every D6 start looked more conservative, but it made
  claims that share census slots start capturing on the same tick, and they superseded each other's slot proofs. The
  wait reuses the existing reason name.
- **R4's held login.** A login whose record a bay claim holds stays in the reconnect queue for at most
  `RocketPassengerReconnectQueue.MAX_WAIT_TICKS` (200 ticks). If the claim still holds it then, the queue drops it with
  WARN `ARCE_PASSENGER_RECONNECT_EXPIRED player=<uuid>`, which production can reach once admission is on. While the login
  is queued, `return false` also skips that player's later journal records. After READY_SOURCE the next retry completes
  without a mount (ready-source records are skipped, as before). After RELEASED_TO_ORDINARY within the 200 ticks,
  ordinary reconnect handles the seat. Offline seats are untouched.
- **R2 for a non-admissible source end.** A valid source set that does not contain its origin gets only the origin
  ticket, as R1 tickets nothing for such an end; D6 still waits for it. Root's R-042 and R-043 updates cover that case.
- **R1's lower edge.** The boxes keep the Level's minimum build height at the bottom: a recognized image never stands
  below its origin.

### Choices where R154 left room

- **Claim order is the order in which claims first ask, at the point where D6 would capture.** Claims that ask on the same

  place while it keeps asking. One that passes the turn on, or leaves the line, queues behind every claim already waiting
  when it asks again, as R154 says for a parked claim. When the turn is free, only the first claim in line can take it.
- **The turn is one per coordinator, across every Level,** as "at most one bay claim at a time" reads. It is not limited to
  claims whose slots overlap; that would need a second rule over slot sets.
- **One pass-on event beyond R154's list, for liveness.** A holder that holds no capture and no proof, and must wait at one
  of D6's checks (T' not yet acknowledged, the source not loaded, END_LOADING, DUPLICATE_PENDING, END_UNAVAILABLE, a slot

  bay claim on the server. A claim that is not the holder never holds a proof.
- **D5's copy pass during a capture.** `BayConsumptionSteps.prove` runs D5's copy pass before D6 on every PROVE step, so

and readback of one chunk, followed by D7's one guarded receipt generation. The n-th claim in line therefore waits for
n - 1 turns, plus any W98 retry or barrier-quota wait the holders meet. A claim that D7 parks, or whose removal is
refused, also waits for every claim that was in line when it asks again. The log does not count ticks, so these local
figures are wall-clock time:

- In `bay_registered:1` (twelve tests, nine of which reach D6 together), one turn took 0.13 to 0.30 s in the three
  bay-only runs (median 0.18 to 0.23 s), and 0.18 to 0.68 s (median 0.37 s) in the complete GameTest.
- The nine claims all ended READY_SOURCE within 2.0 to 2.3 s of the batch's first D6 proof in the bay-only runs, and
  within 3.9 s in the complete run.

  in the complete run, where that claim had been parked twice on a busy store and had queued again each time.

  about 4 to 6 s there, which is closer to that bound than the local runs are (see the risks).

**Ordinary single captures can still supersede a bay proof.** F1's flight checkpoint and W98's ordinary retirements take
single-slot captures through the same barrier and do not take the bay turn. One that captures a column the holder has
already proved supersedes that proof. The holder then retries within its turn: at once after SUPERSEDING_SAVE, as before,
or after W98's 200 ticks when the capture reports another failure (the `NONE` refusals that earlier rounds saw). Every
claim in line then waits that much longer. Bay claims no longer supersede each other, because only the holder captures.
