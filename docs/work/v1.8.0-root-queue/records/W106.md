### Preparation scope finding

The existing private `RocketStructureSnapshot` constructor performs content and
structure hashing synchronously. Its codec calls that constructor again. A
codec-valid, palette-shared 2,048-block fixture serializes to 91,285 bytes while
the two hash inputs alone total 18,002,243 bytes, above the fixed 16,777,216-byte
tick budget. The test also covers a tank and a deterministic one-mB cargo delta.
These are synthetic model/codec fixtures, not assembled native block fixtures.
They measure a lower bound and do not count copies or codec work as free.

A cursor outside that constructor cannot split its work. Accumulating budget
credit across ticks and then calling it would violate the per-tick ceiling.
The TASK names minimal existing-class hooks in the adapter list, runtime and
escrow core, but not the snapshot model/hasher/codec. Scope clarification was
requested before changing those files. No limit, constructor, hash, codec,
runtime, permit, proof, lease or admission setting has been changed. The complete
C-P slice remains IN PROGRESS pending genuine resumable construction and the
associated runtime/publication hooks. A read-only review is still required.
