## Cause and design

A FULL chunk is not necessarily ready to tick block entities. After native reload without players, the vent has
not observed the room, and the atmosphere scan queue can be empty. The previous Level-wide settlement check treated
that absence of work as final noncombustible air and extinguished the restored torch.

`CombustionAir.settled` now also checks the torch's position. A roofed cell waits for BLOCK_TICKING status, the
server's block-ticking eligibility, and loaded entity storage. The check runs after the existing atmosphere tick.
An exposed column can still establish ambient noncombustible air without ticker readiness: it reads the current
MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES heightmap directly from the already-loaded chunk, not sky light. The existing dirty/scan
settlement checks still apply. Unknown roofed cells remain in the existing fair deferred queue.

This is a conservative lifecycle check, not an arbitrary grace period or a second atmosphere cache. It requests no
chunks or tickets in production, adds no world scan or persistent state, and changes no schema, public ID, protocol,
lighting action, or vent/scrubber behavior. The 8,192-position cap, 256-check budget, sweep limits, protection events,
drop rules, and existing 3-by-3 FULL-chunk mutation preflight remain unchanged. ADR-066 section 3.2 is retained;
no ADR decision or waiver was changed.
