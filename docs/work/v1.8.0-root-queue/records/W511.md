### `720c1fa4c`: Centrifuge height

**JEI's arithmetic.** I read it from the classes of `jei-1.20.1-forge-15.56.0.205.jar` (the copy at
`D:/GitHub/ARCE-Task-Evidence/root-queue/W188/jei-inspect/`), disassembled with `javap`:

- `RecipeGuiSizing.calculateInitialSize`: the window's height is the screen's GUI height minus 58, at least 175
  and at most the configured maximum (default 350).
- `RecipesGui.init` and `getRecipeLayoutsArea`: the window's top is at 45, its header takes 34, and the recipe
  area's height is the window's height minus 40.
- `RecipeManager.createRecipeLayoutDrawable`: a recipe has a border of 4 on every side.
- `RecipeGuiLogic.getVisibleRecipeLayoutsWithButtons`: at least one recipe is shown per page, whether it fits
  or not.

| Window and scale | GUI height | Recipe area | Centrifuge before, 192 + 8 | Centrifuge now, 130 + 8 | Other classic, 104 + 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1280 by 720, scale 2 | 360 | 262 | fits | fits | fits |
| 1280 by 720, scale 3 | 240 | 142 | **58 too tall** | fits, 4 to spare | fits |
| 1280 by 720, scale 4 | 240 | 142 | as scale 3 | as scale 3 | as scale 3 |

Minecraft does not apply scale 4 to a 1280 by 720 window: it raises the scale only while the height divided by
the scale stays at or above 240, and 720 / 4 is 180. The setting 4 therefore gives scale 3. Since no window has a
GUI height below 240, the recipe area is never below 142, and the new category fits at every scale of every
window. At scale 3 the border runs from y 81 to 219 inside an area that ends at 221.

**Most rows.** C6 bounds a classic recipe to 4 item inputs, 4 item outputs, 2 fluid inputs, 2 fluid outputs
(`machine/classic/adapter/ClassicValidatedRecipe.java:76-80`) and 16 chance outputs, Centrifuge only (`:69-70`).
A special recipe shifts its outputs by one row but may have only 3 (`:84-86`), and is a Chemical Reactor recipe.
With these maxima:

- the lowest item slot's frame ends at y 75;
- the cost line is at y 78 to 87;
- the chance slots' frames run from y 91 to 127 in two rows of eight, and from x 3 to 147.

All of it is inside 156 by 130 and nothing overlaps.

**Other categories.** The six other classic categories keep 156 by 104 and the cost line at y 84
(`client/compat/jei/ClassicJeiLayout.java:6,9`); the Electrolyzer, Rolling Machine, Precision Assembler and
Small Plate Press categories are not touched.

### `90a896c6c`: recipe fluids

All four fluid slots now pass the recipe's amount as the renderer's capacity, with a 16 by 16 icon and no
capacity in the tooltip. No other file under `src/main/java` calls `setFluidRenderer` or `addFluidStack`.

- JEI rejects a capacity of 0 or less (`FluidTankRenderer`: "capacity must be > 0"). No recipe can reach it:
  a classic fluid row is 1 to 16,000 mB (`machine/classic/adapter/ClassicFluidRow.java:9`), the Electrolyzer's
  water is 1 to 4,000 mB (`machine/electrolyzer/ElectrolyzerRecipeSpec.java:26`), and the Rolling Machine's fluid
  is at least 1 (`machine/rolling/RollingMachineRecipe.java:98`, and `client/compat/jei/MachineRecipeView.java:125`).
- An amount above 16,000 mB cannot occur in a classic recipe. Where a category allows more, the capacity equals
  the amount, so the icon is full for every amount.
- The tooltip shows the recipe's amount alone. Before, the classic categories showed it against 16,000 mB, and
  the Electrolyzer and Rolling Machine showed it against itself.

### `af4f5179a` and `60109476e`: Start and Cancel

The rule at the tip (`client/ResearchControllerScreen.java:48-55`):

- Start: owner, Observatory, status neither UNFORMED nor UNLOADED, and no missing-cell diagnostic.
- Cancel: owner, Observatory, and the displayed job's phase is ACTIVE.

It is applied when the screen opens or is resized and on every tick. Against C6's server:

| Viewer and device | State | Start drawn | Server on Start | Cancel drawn | Server on Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not the owner, any device | any | inactive | UNAUTHORIZED (`machine/classic/runtime/ResearchService.java:205`) | inactive | UNAUTHORIZED |
| Owner, processor | any | inactive | no Start for a processor (`:220`) | inactive | not ACTIVE: a processor selects only READY jobs (`:96-97`), and `GroundSurveys.cancel` refuses them |
| Owner, Observatory, formed | no ACTIVE job | active | starts, or names its refusal | inactive | JOB_NOT_FOUND (`:214-215`) |
| Owner, Observatory, formed | ACTIVE job | active | DEVICE_BUSY or its like from the registry | active | cancels (`:216`) |
| Owner, Observatory, unformed or unloaded | no ACTIVE job | inactive | UNFORMED or UNLOADED (`:142`, `:218-219`) | inactive | JOB_NOT_FOUND |
| Owner, Observatory, unformed or unloaded | ACTIVE job | inactive | UNFORMED or UNLOADED | active | cancels: Cancel is handled before the formation check (`:212-216`) |

Why the two sides agree:

- **Cancel.** The server cancels `selected(controller)`, which exists exactly when the owner has an ACTIVE job
  (`:93-98`, `:107-112`). The menu sends the phase of `displayed(controller)` (`:113-121`,
  `machine/classic/runtime/ResearchControllerMenu.java:46-49`). That is ACTIVE in the same case: `work` and
  `selected` return only ACTIVE jobs for an Observatory, and the third choice is reached only when the owner has
  none. The phase has five values and is sent as ordinal plus one in three bits, so it cannot be cut.
- **Start.** While unformed, the controller's tick sets UNFORMED or UNLOADED every tick
  (`ResearchService.java:81`), so an intent's answer shows for one tick at most. The diagnostic is set by each
  revalidation (`machine/classic/runtime/ClassicControllerBlockEntity.java:207`) and is empty whenever the
  structure forms, so it never disables Start on a formed Observatory.
- The owner relation in the menu is 1 exactly when the viewer is the device's owner, which is the server's test.

Four things that these commits did not introduce and do not make worse, for Root:

1. **Start can still be drawn active on an unformed Observatory** when a status of higher rank hides UNFORMED and
   no cell is missing. The tick reports DEVICE_COLLISION, OFFLINE_REPAIR_REQUIRED, REGISTRY_BLOCKED or DISABLED
   before it looks at formation (`ResearchService.java:77-81`). The server refuses Start, and the status line
   names the reason. A formed Observatory with one of these statuses also draws Start active, as before.
2. **Cancel can be drawn active and be refused without an answer** when the device is a quarantined copy or the
   directory is full (`:213`, `admit`). Before these commits Cancel was always active for the owner.
3. **Cancel acts on the selection, not on the job whose progress is shown.** An owner with two Observatories,
   each with an ACTIVE job, sees this device's job (`work`, `:114`) but cancels the selected one (`:214`), which
   after a restart is the first by job id and can belong to the other Observatory. This is C6's service logic.
4. Previous, Next and Rebind stay active while unformed, and so does Claim on a processor. The server refuses
   Claim and Rebind then (`:218-219`). TASK W507 names only Start and Cancel.
