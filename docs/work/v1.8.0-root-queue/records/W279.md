## Question

ADR-061 section 4.9 keeps every asset whose history includes the AR 16x texture set (Advanced Rocketry pull requests
#1809, #1811, #1889 and #1976) under REVIEW until an authorship finding is recorded, because the same contributor's
LibVulpes commit `984d6747` took textures "from the unmerged Cl1ff PR". The owner asked whether the original art, for
example the pressure-tank icons, can be used.

  ADR-061 should correct the wording; the rule's effect is the same.

## Finding

The public record does not settle authorship. The LibVulpes files are explicitly credited to an unmerged pull request
by Cl1ff, who was an AR collaborator but left no public licence statement. The AR files carry no credit at all; the
wording of #1889 ("Updates sawblade texture", "Makes tanks ... 16x") reads like the contributor's own work, but
nothing says so. No authorship finding can be recorded from this, so the files stay as ADR-061 and W274 leave them:
the owner's fallback applies, and the NEW art of W275 stands in.

A short written statement would settle each part:

- from `voidsong-dragonfly` (Silfryi): which files in AR #1809, #1811, #1889 and #1976 are their own work, offered
  under the repository's MIT licence, and which came from Cl1ff;
- from `Cl1ff`: that the textures taken into libVulpes #41 and #42 (and any in the AR set) were contributed under the
  MIT licence;
- `zmaster587`, Advanced Rocketry's author, merged all of them and may know the history.

The owner plans to ask the authors once the mod is playable. With such a statement recorded under ADR-061 section
4.2, a later revision can move the named files from EXCLUDE to IMPORT; W274's exclusions do not prevent that.
