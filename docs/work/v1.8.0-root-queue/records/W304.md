### Cause

`RocketRejoinWorld.choose` takes each candidate's height from the Overworld surface heightmap. The rejoin GameTests
are `rocket_test` structures placed at about y -60, below the natural terrain of the GameTest world, so the floors their
fixtures build inside the structure are invisible to it. The terrain above the structure decides instead: where it is
dry land the player lands on it (`target=PAD`, on natural terrain the test never restores), and where it is sea every
pad-ring candidate stands on water and placement falls back to the ring around the world spawn, where only (4, -4) is
dry. In the filtered run of the 29 rejoin batches at the base, 6 batches placed players on the spawn ring 8 times.

### The fix

- **`RocketRejoinFixturePad`** (new, in `rocket/rejoin`): builds two stone layers at y 299 and 300 and four air layers
  above them, over every column of the pad ring and of its 3-by-3 safety envelopes (the batch footprint plus 5 blocks).
  It records every cell before the first write and restores it on `close()`, with
  `UPDATE_CLIENTS | UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE`, so no neighbour is notified and nothing flows. It refuses to start if a column's
  chunk is not already loaded or if a cell holds a block entity, and it logs one `ARCE_REJOIN_FIXTURE_PAD` INFO line on
  install and one on restore, with the count of unrestored cells (it throws if that is not zero). Each candidate's
  height then comes from the pad, so no candidate reaches the terrain or the spawn ring.
  - The second stone layer keeps a candidate whose support a fixture removes (Pre, Final, Residual) on the pad: the
    relocation lands one block lower on the pad, not on the terrain below.
  - The fourth air layer covers the envelope of Ack's relocation, one block higher than the first attempt.
- **Commit and Readback fixtures:** build the pad over the same 11-by-11 columns as their existing floor in the
  structure, and close it last in `close()`, after their player has left.
- **Admission h1/h2 scenarios:** build the pad around `RocketRegion.fromSnapshot(rocket.snapshot())`, which is the
  region `RocketReleasePlan` records in the batch, and close it last. The refusal and view scenarios build none.
- **AfterBatch hooks:** each of the 18 batches that builds a pad has one, which calls
  `RocketRejoinFixturePad.closeAbandoned()`, so a pad is restored even when its test fails or times out without
  reaching `close()`. No rejoin batch had a hook before.
- The batch records, the floors inside the structures, the boat fixture and every assertion and timeout are unchanged.
  No chunk is loaded beyond the ones the test structure already forces.

## The experiment

`W304FloodExperiment` (only on the two experiment branches) is a GameTest holder with a `BeforeBatch` hook for each of
the 29 rejoin batches. Each hook finds the spawn-ring columns whose 3-by-3-by-4 envelope holds no fluid, puts a water
source on that column's feet, on its support, with stone on its four sides, so it cannot flow, and logs the ring's dry
and production-safe column counts before and after (`W304_FLOOD`). In both flooded runs the first hook found exactly one
dry and safe column, `[4, 68, -4]`, flooded it, and every hook then logged `dryAfter=0 safeAfter=0`.

Each case ran the 29 `rocket_rejoin_*` batches (29 tests) in one filtered GameTest server, in the same order, so the
structures stood at the same positions in every case.

## Choices where the task was open

- The task suggested a fixture-built pad "as W303 did". This pad has two stone layers and four air layers instead of
  one and three, so that the fixtures that remove a candidate's support or fill its feet still relocate on the pad,
  and its restore uses `UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE` as W301F's contained water does.
- No rejoin test can pass only through the spawn fallback, so none was kept there.
- The floors inside the structures were kept: the Readback Vehicle fixtures stand their boat on that floor, and
  removing it would change more than the landing area.
- The batch records keep their pad position inside the structure. The candidates use only the batch footprint's x
  and z, so only the surface height above it changed.

## Known risks

- The pad lies at y 299 to 304. A future test that builds there in the same batch, or a world whose surface reaches
  y 299, would conflict; the pad never replaces a block entity and restores every other cell.
- A fixture closes its pad on success and when its own tick fails; the AfterBatch hooks cover a test that ends any other way. Vanilla allows one
  AfterBatch method per batch, so a later hook on one of these 18 batches must call `closeAbandoned()` too.
- This does not satisfy or approve any v1.8.0 Required Gate.
