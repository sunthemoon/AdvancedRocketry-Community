## Design choices

The Overworld SavedData reserves one bounded record per admitted projector
generation, including live reservations in the 4096-entry and 1 MiB limits.
Each root has its own schema. Native roots preflight before copying; unsupported
roots remain inert and retain their data. Removal converts a reservation to an
orphan before source deletion. Unavailable or unreadable sources do not prove
removal, and unavailable target claims remain pending.

The shared 64-cell budget counts neighbor and lifecycle reads, not just mutations.
Cooldown is per projector UUID across generations and permits at most one work
operation per five server ticks. Scheduling remains bounded and fair. Production
code creates no chunk tickets. Test-only tickets are explicitly removed.
Sealing and collision depend only on the full-cube BlockState. Room invalidation
occurs synchronously at physical commit, not canceled permission preflight.

Offline-owner FakePlayers have permission level zero. Owner binding requires a
connected, living non-FakePlayer; an operator can locally bind an unclaimed device.
The server switch disables live projection/retraction but leaves registration and
orphan recovery available. There is no energy charge. Projector items carry no
owner/claim identity. Pre-removal vanilla world-destruction loot is conservatively
suppressed to prevent a refused removal minting a duplicate; post-removal player
loot and successful explosion drops are covered.
