## Which save it was

I reproduced the failure with temporary instrumentation, and the record of it follows.

- **Instrumentation.** The temporary changes were:
  - a trace behind `-Dadvancedrocketrycommunity.escrowSaveTrace=true` in `EscrowSaveMonitor`
    (watch, Save events and every generation increase, with a stack trace) and at both
    `SUPERSEDING_SAVE` decisions in `EscrowBarrier`;
  - per-tick diagnostics in the joint test: whether `ChunkHolder.getChunkToSave()` was complete, the
    chunk's unsaved flag, the `ChunkMap` save cooldown, and a diff of `ChunkSerializer.write` output
    taken at the capture and again when the chunk became dirty.

  None of it is committed. The patch and its base are `W36/repro-20261013/instrumentation.patch` and
  `instrumentation-base.txt`.
- **Reproduction.** Run 02 of `W36/repro-20261013/` failed with "Joint terrain proof did not confirm
  both endpoints: SUPERSEDING_SAVE, proofs 0". The trace (`run-02.log`, lines 248 to 269) shows the
  path:

  ```text
  ServerLevel.tick -> ServerChunkCache.tick -> ChunkMap.tick (ChunkMap.java:454)
  -> ChunkMap.processUnloads (:490) -> ChunkMap.saveChunkIfNeeded (:796) -> ChunkMap.save (:830)
  -> ChunkDataEvent.Save -> EscrowRuntime.saveAttempt -> EscrowSaveMonitor.observed
  ```

  This was the save of chunk (33125, 33125) at tick 357. The chunk became saveable that tick. The
  barrier's force followed at tick 359 and its readback saw generation 2 instead of 1.
- **The other candidates are ruled out.**
  - The barrier's own store was not misattributed. Its own Save event was traced with `own=true`
    and did not change the generation.
  - It was not an entity-section save: the slot is terrain, and the save came through
    `ChunkMap.save`.
  - It was not autosave, template cleanup or another test. The stack is the per-tick sweep, and
    the chunk is the fixture's own.
- **Why the pre-save from `4b116fae` did not help.**
  - `ServerChunkCache.save(false)` reaches the chunk only through `saveChunkIfNeeded`. That method
    returns without saving while `ChunkHolder.getChunkToSave().getNow(null)` is not a `LevelChunk`
    (offsets 10 to 39).
  - `chunkToSave` combines every save dependency of the chunk:
    - `ChunkMap.getChunkRangeFuture` adds each generation step's range future to every holder in
      range (offset 287);
    - `ChunkHolder.updateFutures` adds the full, ticking and entity-ticking futures (offsets 208,
      299 and 414).
  - So a freshly generated chunk cannot be saved until the generation around it finishes. In all
    six reproduction runs the pre-save found `chunkToSaveDone=false`. The chunk stayed unsaved, and
    no cooldown was set.
- **What dirtied the chunk after the capture.** One tick after the barrier's capture, the chunk's
  serialized form differed in the sky-light and block-light arrays of sections 0, 6 and 7. In three
  of six runs it also differed in `PostProcessing` and `block_ticks`.
  - A light change marks the chunk unsaved: `ChunkHolder.sectionLightChanged`, offset 46.
  - Promotion to ticking runs `LevelChunk.postProcessGeneration`, in
    `ChunkMap.lambda$prepareTickingChunk$41`.
  - The barrier and the test change nothing after the capture.
- **The race.** The barrier forces 20 ticks after its capture and confirms one tick later. The
  chunk became saveable at +18 ticks in the failing run, at +18 and +21 in two passing runs, and not
  before the result in the other three. Once the chunk is saveable, the sweep saves it at once,
  because no cooldown was ever set.
- **Why it failed in full runs and not in a batch alone.**
  - `processUnloads` saves only while `hasMoreTime`, which is `MinecraftServer.haveTime()`, that is,
    `Util.getMillis() < nextTickTime`.
  - `GameTestServer.waitUntilNextTick()` runs tasks and never sleeps. Unpaced batches therefore run
    far faster than 20 ticks per second, and `nextTickTime` moves ahead of the clock.
  - In W35's failing full run, the batches before `escrow_core` covered about 17,500 ticks in 393
    seconds. That leaves `nextTickTime` about 480 seconds ahead, so the sweep runs on every tick.
  - In an `escrow_core`-only run, the batch starts with no such lead and all its tests pace, so the
    sweep rarely runs. Run 01 shows this: the chunk became saveable at +18 and stayed dirty.
  - Putting `endgame_elevator_zone` (about 14 seconds of lead in W35's log) before `escrow_core`
    reproduced the failure in 1 of 5 runs.
  - Why hosted CI has not failed is not established. A plausible reason is that generation there
    finishes after the barrier.

`W36/javap-citations.txt` has the `javap -p -c` excerpts for every vanilla method cited here, from
`forge-1.20.1-47.4.10_mapped_official_1.20.1.jar`.

## The fix (`d88c1716`)

- **What the test does now.** `jointTerrainSlotProofRetainsTheOtherEndpointLease` no longer calls
  the Level-wide `save(false)`. Each tick, it waits until the existing `ChunkSaveWatcher` has
  recorded a save of the fixture chunk. Until then it calls `VanillaChunkSave.saveIfNeeded`.
- **What `VanillaChunkSave` does.** It runs vanilla's own `ChunkMap.saveChunkIfNeeded` for this one
  chunk's holder, through `ObfuscationReflectionHelper` (SRG `m_140327_` for
  `getVisibleChunkIfPresent` and `m_198874_` for `saveChunkIfNeeded`). The behaviour is vanilla's:
  - nothing happens until `chunkToSave` is complete;
  - a save sets the chunk's 10-second cooldown, which the per-tick sweep and autosave honour.
- **The barrier attempt.** After the recorded save, the watcher stops and the single barrier attempt
  starts. The assertions, the single attempt, `timeoutTicks = 200` and the pacing are unchanged.
- **The traced runs of the fix** (`W36/fix-trace-20261013/`):
  - vanilla saved the chunk at +18, +37 and +18 ticks, with about 9,998 ms of cooldown;
  - the barrier captured in the same tick and confirmed 21 ticks later, at +39, +58 and +39 of
    200;
  - in two runs the chunk became dirty again after the capture (block ticks and block light). The
    cooldown kept the sweep off it, and about 8.9 seconds of cooldown remained at the result.

## Choices

- **Which TASK option.** I chose "wait, paced, for vanilla's pending save of the chunk to finish
  before starting the barrier". It is not a retry.
  - The explicit `saveChunkIfNeeded` call is needed because, without a tick lead, vanilla's sweep
    may not run. A wait for the sweep alone could then reach the timeout.
  - Calling it for one chunk avoids saving the other `escrow_core` tests' chunks, which the old
    Level-wide save did.
- **Repeated runs with a lead batch.** The TASK names an `escrow_core`-only loop, but such runs
  cannot exercise the failing path, because the sweep does not run. So the 30-run loop puts one
  unpaced batch first, and 10 extra `escrow_core`-only runs cover the no-lead case.
- **Reflection instead of an access transformer.** This is test code, as in
  `PlanetaryExposureGameTests` and `ChunkSaveWatcher`.
- **Where the batch filter lives.** It is in the main `gametest` package, next to
  `GameTestTickPacer`. It does nothing without the system property, or on a server that is not a
  `GameTestServer`.
  - It is not in the `arce_adapter_test` mod, because that mod is the API-only compatibility
    fixture.
  - A filter that matches no batch fails the start rather than running nothing.
- **No product change and no product unit test.** The barrier and the monitor behaved correctly.

## Known risks

- **The fix relies on vanilla's 10-second cooldown.** The chunk can still be dirtied after vanilla's
  save, by block ticks and late lighting. The barrier's window was 21 ticks, about 1 second, in the
  traced runs. A failure would need the window to exceed about 10 seconds of wall-clock time, which
  is also about the test's 200 paced ticks.
- **The wait adds 18 to 37 ticks before the barrier starts.** The test now ends around tick 40 to
  60 of 200. On a much slower host the chunk could become saveable later. The failure would then be
  a timeout, not `SUPERSEDING_SAVE`. I did not observe it.
- **SRG names.** `VanillaChunkSave` and `GameTestBatchFilter` use the 1.20.1 names `m_140327_`,
  `m_198874_` and `f_177587_`. A mapping change fails when the class loads or the filter runs, and
  does not fail silently.
- **The same race elsewhere (not changed).** `ownTerrainAndEntityStoresConfirmTheExactFrozenFrontier`
  (chunk 32500, 32500) also captures a freshly generated chunk with no earlier vanilla save, so the
  same race can apply to it. It did not fail in any of the 50 runs here (49 batch runs and the full
  run). Changing it is other escrow
  behaviour, outside this TASK, so Root may want to queue it.
