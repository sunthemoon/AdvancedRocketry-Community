## The new case: `allFiveRecoveryPhasesAndFourPresenceCutsReleaseClaimsAfterReload`

Hosted run 40725066567 at `29c0411d`, `job-122945997201.log`:

| Line | About tick | Event |
|---|---|---|

| 7064 | 6,014 | `ARCE_TRANSFER_RETIREMENT_WAIT transfer=76d5a576 site=RECOVERED_SOURCE reason=SUPERSEDING_SAVE` |
| 7065 | 6,014 | The same entry: `reason=BUSY`, 0.3 ms later. |
| 7067 | 6,034 | `ARCE_TRANSFER_RETIREMENT_PROVED ... attempts=1`, one force period later. |
| 7069 | 6,034 | `ARCE_TRANSFER_RETIREMENT_ABANDONED ... reason=JOURNAL_STOPPED` |
| 7070 | 6,034 | `ARCE_TRANSFER_RECOVERY ... phase=COMMITTED ... action=KEEP_SOURCE status=RECOVERED` |
| 7071 | 6,034 | "Recovery cut COMMITTED/1 failed: NOT_FOUND" |

Ticks come from the progress lines, as in W184. The cut runs on a detached `RocketTransferService` against the world's
journal, which the cut reloads from disk. That service's KEEP_SOURCE retirement captured the survivor's slot at about tick
5,994. The autosave at tick 6,000 stored that section, and the capture completed as superseded at the Level's next force.
`RocketOrdinaryRetirement.fail` then released the attempt's reservation; only a fail-back on its creation tick keeps it.

The runtime's own `RocketTransferService.tick` calls `recoverNext` on every tick, with its own live and settled sets. To it,
the fixture's record was unclassified, actionable (no reservation) and loaded at both ends, because the fixture retains the pad
chunks. In the same tick it recovered T itself and began its own proof. The detached entry's retry found the reservation taken
(`BUSY`). The runtime's proof removed T; the detached entry was then abandoned with `JOURNAL_STOPPED`, because
`logicalAcknowledged` is false while that removal is unacknowledged. The `RECOVERED` line is the runtime's: had the detached
service completed, its `completedResults` would hold T and the second `recover` would not answer `NOT_FOUND`
(`RocketTransferRecoveryService.recoverById` lines 145 to 154).

So the autosave caused it: it made the first attempt fail and release T. Fix `44b878d54` starts each source-side cut only when
the next autosave falls after the cut's own 40-tick wait for the proof.

Other logs show the same takeover only once, in W116's local run, after that test had already failed for another reason
(`W116/gt-full-4ffa8c56/latest.log` line 5778). The other `JOURNAL_STOPPED` abandonments in the 141 logs on disk are
deliberate journal stops or reloads by later tests (`scripts/abandoned_context.py`).

## Audit

- A superseded **F1** discards the governed launch (`EscrowFlightCheckpoint.begin` line 157). Only fixtures built on
  `EscrowGameTestLaunch` request it again, after the product's 100-tick backoff.
- **Later governed stages** (F4's absence proof, F6, and the boundary return's absence and F6 proofs) retry a superseded proof
  after 100 ticks (`EscrowSuccessorRetry`). They log `ARCE_ESCROW_SUCCESSOR_HOLD` at INFO. An **ordinary retirement** retries
  one tick later in its own coordinator, and T stays settled there.

## Guard windows

Each guard waits, paced, while `GameTestAutosave.within(server, window)` holds, and only before its step. The wait is at
most `window + 1` ticks, once per 6,000-tick period. Every window is derived in a comment at its constant.

- **F1, 60 ticks.** F1's source capture completes with its Level's next 20-tick force, and the ARMED write must capture while
  that proof is valid: three force periods, as W184 chose for `moon_landing_rule`.
- **Detached returns, 40 and 60 ticks.** The test's own wait for the proof. The recovery matrix allows 40 ticks per cut; the
  cancellation and occupancy tests allow 60. An autosave after that wait cannot reach the test, which has passed or failed.
- **Pinned queue, 120 ticks.** The governed force, the 40-tick drain, the ARM write and the fail-back's force (green: 60 to 80).
- **Contention, 100 ticks.** The primer's and the paired batch's forces, plus the shared case's 40-tick retention and the
  ordinary force (green: 20 to 60).

Every wait was checked against the test's timeout and bounds, using green hosted runs at `29c0411d` and `1af1d76ed`
(`scripts/batch_lengths.py`, `scripts/batch_lines.py`).

## Choices where the task left room

- **Takeover fixtures.** No hosted run has shown the takeover for a fail-back yet. I guarded the three fail-back tests anyway,
  because they share the recovery matrix's journal, retained pad chunks and detached service. The cost is one short wait.
- **The eight concurrent launches** wait per request, not once for the sequence, so no assumption about the spacing is needed.
  A pause leaves at least four launches concurrent, which is enough for the asserted callback overflow.
- **The two exposed fixtures** stay unguarded, because any guard I could fit would change a timeout.

## Known risks

- The filtered runs reached about tick 6,400. Their tick-6,000 autosave fell during `rocket_warmup_stale_copy`, whose relaunch
  takes no capture, just before `rocket_warmup_kept_pad` started. So they show that the changed fixtures pass, but almost
  certainly no guard had to wait. Root's hosted run on the pushed branch stands in for a complete GameTest.
- The guards cover the vanilla periodic autosave only. An explicit `saveEverything` or an unload in a concurrent test could
  still supersede a capture.
- A takeover after a superseded capture is not limited to the autosave. Any failed attempt that releases a detached service's
  reservation on the world journal opens the same gap, for example a busy capture budget after the creation tick.
