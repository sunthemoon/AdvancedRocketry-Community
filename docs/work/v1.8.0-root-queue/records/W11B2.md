### Implemented prerequisites

- Rocket wrapper schema 3 accepts genuine schema 1 and 2 and adds the exact
  `resource_escrow` section. Its canonical neutral encoding omits `ledger_id` only
  at epoch 0 with no markers. First binding uses the actual ledger UUID, never a
  fabricated UUID or a production ledger creation. Invalid sections preserve and
  quarantine the whole rocket wrapper. Future schema 4 and unknown wrapper fields
  are tested separately.
- The transfer journal now uses checked file replacement for explicit boundaries,
  autosave and stop-save. Acknowledgements identify the session-local mutation
  sequence, canonical root digest and individual record digests. The public
  `checkedWriteResult()` exposes COMMITTED, NOT_COMMITTED, UNKNOWN or UNACKNOWLEDGED,
  the current and acknowledged sequences, and whether that acknowledgement is current.
  An older COMMITTED attempt does not authorize a newer RAM mutation. The digest streams
  NC1-compatible sorted-key framing because W11b1's native canonical buffer has a
  262,144-byte cap, below the legal journal bound. The journal keeps its schema,
  checksums and quotas. Definite failure retains dirty authority; ambiguous outcomes
  use bounded target readback and clear acknowledgement when unknown. Failed writes
  retry at most once per 200 ticks through ordinary recovery; normal fresh mutations
  are not delayed by that failure backoff.
- Launch does not start countdown before acknowledgement. Destination publication,
  passenger movement and source removal follow their checked phase writes. Recovery
  acknowledges destination identity before publishing a rebuilt destination or
  discarding sources. Landed reservation removal is checked before disassembly.
  Failed preparation keeps captured authority instead of deleting an uncertain
  record. These changes need the required injected live/native tests before acceptance.
- Read-only escrow queries guard the existing Runtime/Manager/flight mutation
  layers, boarding, leaving, countdown cancellation and live flight dispatch. Forge
  mount, teleport, spreadplayers and dimension events are canceled for protected
  rockets. `/kill` is overridden. Live `/data` retains the escrow section and ignores
  protected mod payload changes, but does not intercept vanilla base-field changes
  or promise a command refusal message.
- Current-candidate native component, adapter, fuel and planetary rocket validators
  require entity schema 3 and an exact neutral section. Their snapshot, flight,
  transfer and transaction schemas are not bumped. Historical evidence is untouched.

### Policy at the nine original journal flush sites

| Site | Failure policy |
|---|---|
| Launch admission and landed-record replacement | No success, countdown or flight-ticket effect after failed acknowledgement. Retain captured authority. |
| Prepare exception cleanup | Do not perform the former remove-and-flush cleanup over uncertain authority. Retain the record for recovery. |
| Landed reservation release | No release classification/audit or disassembly materialization until exact removal is acknowledged. |
| Committed-record migration | Do not report migration or settle the record until acknowledgement. |
| Return/cancel cleanup | Old source authority permits restoring its stationary state before the removal; do not report cancellation success or release its reservation after failed removal. |
| Phase advancement | Acknowledge before new destination publication, passenger movement or source destruction; remove transient live classification on failure. |
| Recovery destination rebinding | Acknowledge before publishing the new destination UUID, source discard, duplicate discard or remount. |
| Recovered-source removal | Do not report recovered release after failed removal. |
| Reconnect-source settlement | Do not clear classifications or report released settlement after failed removal. |

Dirty/unacknowledged journal state blocks dependent transfer processing and resource
mutation. The entity guard covers current and last acknowledged logical identities;
unknown authority without a retained acknowledgement conservatively blocks all
identities until an exact retry. Transfer-service admission still uses a journal-wide
clean acknowledgement, so an
observed failed journal write can temporarily block unrelated rocket resource
operations too. This conservative availability effect must be reviewed alongside
the checked writer's measured cost.

## Proposed risk and ADR text for Root

These are proposed entries for the **finished integration**, not assertions that
this checkpoint implements every protection. No ADR or risk-register file is edited.

### Risk A: D3-A protection scope and availability

A future or invalid escrow root refuses whole-world startup and preserves the raw
file. An invalid rocket or block-entity envelope quarantines that object and its
connected records until audited settlement or offline repair. Unserializable BE
roots inherit `GuardedChunkSaves`: denial affects the entire terrain chunk and,
after 256 denial coordinates saturate, all later terrain saves in that ServerLevel
until Level close, with no production online reset. Entity and other independent
stores may still save and diverge. Pending and uncertain operations retain shared
capacity (64/server, 8/rocket, 1/BE); admission refuses when exhausted. Tombstones
(64) are not collected in v1.8; exhausted tombstones refuse abandonment and floor
settlement. A classified fenced transfer blocks only overlapping moving station
regions; this claim requires R2 to be complete. Fenced transactions lock resources
and disassembly. Failed checked journal writes retain authority and prevent dependent
effects; journal-wide acknowledgement can temporarily block unrelated rocket resource
operations. Logs have one coordinator state/backoff line per transition, but retries,
transitions and inherited EventBus/ChunkMap/EntityStorage/IOWorker/force errors have
no lifetime cap. Protected `/data` edits are ignored; vanilla base relocation and
raw/third-party replacement are detection-only and require proof invalidation and
locked repair. Relocation, credited assembly fences, veto stalls and unsupported
floor conflicts require the whole-world offline procedure. Stop the server, back
up all Levels' terrain/entities/POI, journals/ledger/global data, playerdata and
metadata together with hashes, repair only a copy while retaining raw originals,
validate identity/prefix/conservation and run two restart cycles plus a new barrier
before replacing the complete world while stopped. Restoring single data files,
including migration backups, is unsafe. Restart alone is not repair. This entry
registers mechanics and accepts neither R-021 nor another save-veto mechanism.

### Risk B: unobservable storage failures after confirmation

S2 covers stopped-process ordering with successful forces, not power loss, OS crash,
per-file rollback, or environmental storage faults the mod cannot observe. W11b1 E1
requires a retained own-store failure to block proof and L2 even if a later force
succeeds. W11b1 E3 distinguishes such observed failures from an unrelated region-cache
eviction-close failure for which no store future reports an error. Any later write
that corrupts or replaces a confirmed record remains a residual throughout the
post-confirmation window, not only after L2. Direct IOWorker, RegionFileStorage or
ChunkStorage writes bypass Save-event/entity-decorator monitoring. After retirement,
vanilla may regenerate or omit unreadable chunk contents, causing loss with no
remaining escrow diagnosis. Consistent whole-world backups mitigate this residual.
Diagnostic reproduction is not a guarantee PASS or owner acceptance.
On Windows, portable directory forcing is unavailable; the checked writer forces
the staged file before atomic replacement. S2 does not establish power-loss durability.

### ADR-065 implementation note: W11b2 D3-A rocket integration

The integration adds checked stage/force/readback/atomic replacement for every
transfer-journal write on the server thread. The non-throwing boundary exposes exact
snapshot acknowledgement; failed or unknown writes retain authority and dirty state,
and callers may not publish success, advance dependent authority effects, clear
reservations or destroy protected objects before acknowledgement. Launch, phase,
cleanup, migration, replacement and reconnect policies are those in the nine-site
table. An absent journal baseline is checked on first need; blocked roots are never
rewritten. Rocket schema 3 has a neutral optional-ledger section only at epoch 0
without markers and binds to the actual ledger at the first acknowledged operation.
Recovery consults records, markers, floors and tombstones before readiness or effects;
FENCED classification is preserved during bounded loaded-only re-evaluation with no
ticket-adding helper. ADR-044's classified-record region rules remain unchanged.
The finished settlement protocol requires durable intent, every survivor/absence
checkpoint, checked retirement/tombstones and exact acknowledgement before unlock.
Protection scope, duration, log volume and recovery are those in Risk A; unobservable
writer residuals are those in Risk B. This note changes no accepted decision, grants
no risk acceptance and does not replace native cuts or independent implementation
review. Root must not insert unfinished mechanisms as delivered capability.

### Settlement design, written before code

An operator decision is distinct from ordinary reconciliation. A bounded intent
freezes the entire identity-connected component, the existing record digests,
actor identity, selected choice, preview digest, observation ticks and every
survivor's ledger/epoch/resource signature/markers/quantity. Missing observations
are loaded, ticking, identity-checked observations, not durability proofs. A
confirmation must match the preview, and missing observations must be repeated
at least 200 ticks later. Unloaded, duplicate, relocated, malformed or unsupported
endpoints refuse intact. Canonical hashing includes the selected choice, actor,
component, journal guards and observations. Oversized intents and tombstone
reservations refuse before any ledger mutation; there is no truncation.

O1 changes the connected records to a settling phase in one checked generation.
The optional intent is part of the operation envelope; a separate explicit kind
can represent a transfer-floor retirement without pretending it is a fluid fill.
Loaded settling records without a frozen intent remain locked legacy development
records; they cannot be treated as decisions. Unknown writes retain the exact
candidate. The matching acknowledgement, not a RAM phase, authorizes O2.

O2 must use the existing retained own-store, original-worker force and stable
readback barrier for every survivor and missing referenced slot. All proofs must
remain jointly valid through replacement. A missing observation alone cannot
authorize tombstones, journal removal or release. A veto, changed survivor,
superseding save or lost proof retains the complete decision and all locks.
After a restart every O2 proof is reacquired; no serialized boolean is proof.

O3 durably installs bounded returning-identity tombstones before any matching
journal removal. Named removals must match the frozen journal record digest and
wait for the exact checked snapshot acknowledgement without that transfer.
O4 retires records/floors only after those prerequisites, then prunes retired
markers without reducing resource epochs. No resource is reconstructed or
refunded. Actor, quantities, hashes and signed accounting remain auditable.
Floor settlement is restricted to missing protected identities and the frozen
transfer/floor, not a general floor-conflict override. Tombstones are never
collected online.

Implementation and evidence below distinguish supported boundaries from
unfinished adapters. An unimplemented O2/O3/O4 adapter must refuse, never remove
records or report settlement success. The ordinary scheduler must likewise keep
`proved=false` until connected-prefix application, identity elections, joint
proofs, L2 and pruning actually exist. Native cuts require final committed source
and JAR pins, a stop on each side of each implemented acknowledgement and two
restarts. The complete matrix, real historical-world/JAR G1, reference load and
Root's independent review remain separate acceptance requirements.
