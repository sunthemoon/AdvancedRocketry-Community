## Design choices

The cap seed uses 16 durable REPAIR operations on 16 distinct other rocket IDs,
charging the real shared 64/server, 8/rocket, 16/owner authority. Actual cargo
attempts compare complete ledger, device and rocket envelopes before/after
refusal. Restart checks retain the complete capped device root and pending
records; owner B completes exactly once without consuming owner A's capacity.
The fixture waits for actual checked journal proof before testing the cap.

All applicable C-S4 and D-S2 settlement boundaries have load counterparts. The
fluid destination-only load veto leaves 1,000 mB on the rocket and 5,000 mB on the
device as locked raw surplus, not spendable cargo. G2 is absent from the two cut
runners, so the wrapper delegates its existing S1 campaign and requires the
original historical JAR/source and start counter. No production admission,
algorithm, schema, public ID, migration, configuration or resource was changed.
No ADR decision was changed, and no upstream material was imported.
