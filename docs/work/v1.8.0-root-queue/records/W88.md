## Cause

`ResearchService.intent(controller, owner, 4)` returns false in these cases only:

- off the server thread, or `owner` is not the controller's research owner (`UNAUTHORIZED`);
- `basic()` refuses: the controller is not physical (its chunk is not FULL, or the block entity was replaced), the
  registry is not operational, the identity is missing, directory admission reports a collision or capacity, repair
  is required or a debit fell short, ground surveys are disabled, **the controller is not formed** (`UNFORMED`, or
  `UNLOADED` when its wait reason is `UNLOADED`), or a footprint chunk is not FULL;
- no READY, uncredited job is owned by `owner` (`JOB_NOT_FOUND`);
- the registry's `claim` refuses (job missing, foreign owner, already claimed, not READY, processor busy, or a
  storage, invariant or capacity preflight).

There is no rate limit on this path (the two-tick spacing is the menu's `allowIntent`), and game time only feeds the
registry clock. Of these conditions, only formation changes without the test doing anything:

1. `ClassicRuntime.neighbors` forwards every `NeighborNotifyEvent` to `ClassicMachineManager.changed`. Its `enqueue`
   calls `controller.invalidate()` at once, so `formed()` is false until the dirty queue revalidates the controller
   in the server's END tick. A GameTest step runs after the Level tick and before that END tick.
2. The shared readiness step `ResearchGameTests.ticking` checked only `isPositionEntityTicking(origin)` for
   processors, not formation.
3. The fairness test builds its 17 Earth processors at `absolutePos(5 + 16k, 6, 5)`, at y −55 to −54. That is
   raw seed-0 cave terrain, outside every test's cleared space. The world position follows the batch order:
   - Vanilla advances a one-test batch by the template's AABB x size plus 5 (5 blocks for `empty`), and wraps rows
     only inside batches of eight or more tests.
   - In all 18 W11b2 full runs from C33 on, the same 91 batches and 187 tests came before `research_fairness`.
   - The test's structure block was therefore at (110, −60, 91). The logged nested-bed home `47, −59, 93` (relative
     (2, 1, 2), structure (45, −60, 91)) and the x steps of the six tests in between confirm it.
4. At that position, fixture 18 (origin 259, −54, 96) stands in a mineshaft. The blocks touching its footprint are
   17 gravel, 9 cave air, 2 rails and 4 tuff; rails or planks touch fixtures 8, 10, 12, 16, 18 and 20. Building
   it sends shape updates to that gravel. The gravel falls once its chunk ticks, and every fall unforms the processor
   for the rest of that tick.
5. In a tick where the readiness step passed and gravel fell, the claim for fixture 18 was refused.

Other batch orders put the test elsewhere in the terrain. W78's shared-branch run on 2026-10-16 ran it 153rd, with
330 tests before it, and this branch's base runs it 89th, with 185 tests before it. No run on record outside the
W11b2 prefix has failed it.

The product behaviour is right: an unverified structure must not take a claim. A player whose click lands in such a
tick sees `UNFORMED` for two seconds and can retry.

## Fix (`c2ec73e6`)

- `ResearchGameTests.ticking` also requires `fixture.controller.formed()`, which is what an intent needs from a
  fixture. Every research GameTest that waits on it builds formed fixtures, so none of them changes meaning.
- New GameTest `aNeighbourUpdateHoldsReadinessUntilTheProcessorRevalidates` (batch `research_neighbour_update`).
  It sets one block beside a formed processor that has a READY job, then asserts, in that tick:
  - the processor reads unformed;
  - the readiness step does not pass;
  - the claim is refused with `UNFORMED`.

  After the readiness step passes again, it asserts that the claim succeeds. With the old step, the readiness
  assertion fails, as the diagnostic shows.
- The fairness refusal message now names the fixture, its Level and its status. The survey set-up moved into a
  helper, `readySurvey`, with unchanged assertions and messages.

No assertion, timeout, budget, production class, log expectation or central file changed.
