## Method

- **Cells required.** I read each step's text and its row in `CAMPAIGN-CHECKLISTS-*.md`. Where either names
  language, scale or window cells, the required set is the one mapping C2/R535 gives in
  [CAMPAIGN-CHECKLISTS-A](CAMPAIGN-CHECKLISTS-A.md), section "Accepted procedures and delivered tooling":
  windowed 1280 by 720 at scales 2 and 3, and native fullscreen at scale 4 where offered, each in English
  and Chinese. The host display is 2560 by 1440 and three rows hold fullscreen scale-4 frames, so the
  fullscreen cell is offered. That gives six cells.
- **Cells captured.** Language and scale come from the session's start identity in `identity-index.json`
  and agree with `client-options.txt`. Window mode comes from the PNG size and from the client size the
  harness recorded before each capture in `results.jsonl`; the two agree for all 594 references. A
  2560 by 1440 frame follows an F11 key operation and is native fullscreen. The scale-4 sessions of A13,
  C06 and B06 are 1920 by 1080 windows: the F3 identity frame of `a13-s2` reads "Display: 1920x1080".
  A 1920 by 1080 window at scale 4 is not one of the six cells.
- **Frames W482 had seen.** W482's `visual-judgments.json` gives its native frames and the sheet pages it
  opened. I derived which frames are on which page from the image order in `sheets/<ROW>.html`, 24 per
  page, and confirmed that layout on two pages.
- **No grouping by pixel hash.** I compared the windowed, hover and reload frames of every F07 and H06
  screen pixel by pixel (`K/tools/diffbox.ps1`). All 84 pairs differ across the whole frame, so no frames
  were grouped and each one was opened.
- For a step that fails the cell check I did not open its remaining frames; the TASK asks for that only
  where the step still passes.
