## Design choices

The allowance is `min(800, ceil(80 / factor))` for positive factors below one; zero uses 800. The cap is 40 seconds at 20 TPS and covers the 0.10 g area-field minimum without permitting arbitrarily long floating near zero gravity. Moon gravity receives 485 ticks. At native counter 80, each additional permitted tick spends one credit by changing 80 to 79 before vanilla increments it again. The counter is never reset by this mechanism, and vanilla itself disconnects a stationary Moon hoverer at tick 486. These are consecutive vanilla-flagged floating ticks, not a replacement for vanilla's movement classification. Native resets, including paid suit flight, retain their existing semantics. Normal or higher gravity, creative/spectator flight and elytra receive no allowance changes. Budgets are connection-bound runtime state, cleared on logout, dimension change, respawn and server stop.

Inside a committed station region, an unsupported, airborne survival/adventure player with an applied gravity factor of zero becomes eligible after 40 ticks. The existing safety-return query, VISIT authority, loaded safe-arrival probes, TELEPORT protection, translated messages and commit revalidation perform the return. The existing 200-tick attempt spacing and query budgets remain unchanged. The ordinary outside-region threshold remains 100 ticks. A foot-level collision check has a fixed footprint bound, checks loaded chunks before block access and never searches downward or adds tickets. Paid suit ticks retain their existing handling. Reconnecting restores the saved unsupported position and starts the same bounded recovery process.

[ADR-040](../../../decisions/ADR-040-STATION-REGIONS-AND-MIGRATION.md), [ADR-041](../../../decisions/ADR-041-STATION-ORBIT-ENVIRONMENT.md), [ADR-058](../../../decisions/ADR-058-AREA-GRAVITY-CONTROLLER.md) and the ADR-066 safety-return design were followed. They define attribute-based gravity, not a separate zero-gravity jump control. Jumping is unchanged, including the reported zero-gravity behavior. No ADR decision text was edited.

## Remaining work and risks

The task's automated verification passes. A real-client check follows in Root's scripted run: Moon crater descent, zero-gravity station drift and reconnect recovery with default `allow-flight=false`. Root's review, hosted sharded regression, push and merge remain outstanding. This record does not approve the version or any Required Gate; not all v1.8 Required Gates are established by this slice.

The allowance is deliberately finite, including at zero gravity. A prolonged destination-query backlog, unavailable authority, unsafe arrival or protection refusal can delay or prevent recovery; the existing query bounds, refusals and retry spacing remain authoritative, rather than granting unlimited flight or bypassing permissions. Gravity values, jetpack behavior, fall damage, station controls, jump controls and other findings were not changed.
