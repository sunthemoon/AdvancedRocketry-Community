## Changes to the design forced by the code

None of these changes the commit, receipt, award or DELIVERED order, an absence proof or a hold.

1. **N5 wording for `foldWarpCredits`.** Design section 10 says the changed
   `StationWarpRelocationTest` asserts that `foldWarpCredits` and `addMember` "throw as they do for a
   registry that is not operational (`requireOperational`)". In the code, `foldWarpCredits` never
   throws for a registry that is not operational. It refuses every credit and returns
   `WarpCreditFold(0, offered)`, because the warp service calls it from the server tick, where a
   throw would escape the tick. Implemented: under the committed override, `addMember` throws (as
   `requireOperational` does), and `foldWarpCredits` refuses every credit exactly as for a registry
   that is not operational. The test asserts the full refusal.
2. **Size bound.** Besides `StationCheckedCandidate` and `StationCheckedWriter`, two more small
   classes were extracted from `StationRegistrySavedData` so that it stays at or under 800 lines
   without an ADR: `StationWarpEnergyFold` (the existing fold loop, unchanged) and
   `StationRootPresence` (the section 3.6 bookkeeping). The root encoder moved to
   `StationRegistryPayload.encodeRoot`.
3. **The first-write re-check.** `AtomicSavedData` gains a `beforeCommit(Path)` hook beside
   `writable()`. It is how the first write of a proven-absent progression root re-checks absence
   immediately before the atomic move, for the barrier and for ordinary saves alike (section 3.6,
   item 3).
4. **A1 isolation.** Detached roots need the delivery service and the Moon completion to take their
   sources and dispositions as parameters (`FirstEventSources`, `FirstEventDispositionSource`).
   Production passes the world's. GameTest fixtures (classes named `*GameTests` or
   `*GameTestSupport`) are excluded from the A0 check that only the warp service builds an electing
   request.
5. **Directory force for named station operations.** Named station operations now force the data
   directory after the replacement, as design section 1.3 asks ("both root-only and named station
   operations call the same one writer ... R1 directory-force policy"). On Windows this is the
   existing no-op.
6. **GameTest log expectations.** Two expectations were added to `scripts/gametest_expected_log.json`:
   - `w44-first-moon-landing-startup-baseline`: at most one WARN
     `ARCE_FIRST_EVENT_HELD kind=first_moon_landing reason=SOURCE_UNCONFIRMED` per start. The journal's
     startup baseline is not yet confirmed at the delivery service's first END tick, so the Moon kind
     logs its one temporary-hold WARN and then one INFO when the hold clears (section 3.3).
   - `w44-a1-refused-first-warp-replacement`: exactly one ERROR
     `ARCE_STATION_UPDATE_WRITE_FAILED ... stage=replace` in the `classic_first_event` batch. The A1
     fixture refuses the detached registry's replacement on purpose.
7. **Seam details the design leaves open.**
   - The participant seam returns the fixed list [P1, P2] for any active cut, not only for named
     rows. Every M and WARP row uses it, and only the release-test gate can reach it.
   - FP7a, FP7b, FP9, FP10 and FP11 halt at the first delivery step after startup
     (`first_step`). FP7a's "election attempt" is therefore approximated by the first step, which is
     where the unavailable progression root first refuses.

### A1 (GameTests, batch `classic_first_event`)

All three use detached source and progression files, so no other GameTest can take their slot.

- `ClassicFirstEventGameTests.theFirstWarpAwardsOnlyPresentAuthorizedParticipantsAfterTheDurableReceipt`:
  - a DEFER request is refused before any write or debit, and a refused replacement elects nothing
    and keeps the FE;
  - the real capture returns exactly the owner and the member (present, in the region, with VISIT
    and eligible);
  - nobody is awarded before the receipt is on disk; afterwards both are, while a player without
    VISIT and a member outside the region are not;
  - a later joiner is never awarded, and the source is acknowledged DELIVERED with the same event.
- `ClassicFirstEventGameTests.deferredGrantsFollowEligibilityAndADisconnectKeepsNothing`: spectating
  and dead participants are deferred, a disconnect removes the pending entry, the others are granted
  within 20 + ceil(2P / 16) ticks of becoming eligible without reconnecting, and a reconnecting
  participant is granted at login.
- `FirstMoonLandingGameTests.theFirstMoonLandingElectsTheAboardPassengerAndLandsOnlyAfterTheConfirmation`:
  a real destination rocket in the Moon Level elects exactly its aboard passenger. It stays in
  DESCENT, with no award, until the exact journal confirmation; a repeated completion does not elect
  again; after the confirmation it lands and `moon_landing` is granted, but `one_small_step` only
  after the receipt is on disk. A watcher and a later joiner are not awarded, and the source ends
  DELIVERED.
- The negatives the design names (`ClassicMilestoneAdvancementGameTests` and
  `ClassicCrystallizerAdvancementGameTests`) were reframed with explicit fixture state: the Moon
  slot is occupied, and the station test branches on an open or elected slot. Their behavioural
  negatives are kept.
