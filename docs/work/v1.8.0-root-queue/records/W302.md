## Delivered scope and design

Implementation commit `d32a93438af0ff7d201bce89d5defdfdeb0f1dd8` changes six
files in `rocket/server`. `SealedProductionTestAccess` supplies a bounded
10-by-7-by-9 obsidian enclosure, built before clearing its 280 interior cells or
installing resources. It isolates `SealedWorldProtectionGameTests`,
`SealedAutomationGameTests`, `SealedDisassemblyGameTests`,
`SealedRecoveryGameTests`, and `SealedSupersessionGameTests`. Its held-provider
setup also isolates the independent provider automation fixture without changing
that adapter test's code.

Obsidian keeps ambient fluids out after the existing explosion; glass would not
provide that guarantee. The world-protection test additionally checks every
shell block at age 61, after its explosion, fire, water, and fence-clear steps.
All original assertions, the 400-tick timeout, the bedrock fence, its age-60
removal, the 200-tick retry, and the native full-column proof remain unchanged.
`source-audit.json` verifies the original world-protection body is preserved
apart from these two fixture calls, all changed GameTest annotations are
identical, and the experimental difference is exactly the delivered fix.

This is fixture setup, not a product-rule change. There are no save, schema,
network, escrow, retirement-guard, registration, asset, provenance, build,
expected-log, ledger, QUEUE, or status changes. No ADR decision needed changing;
the conservative open choice was an explosion-resistant enclosure material.
