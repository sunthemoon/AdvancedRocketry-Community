# W14F review fixes

## Scope and implementation decisions (2026-10-12)

This task owns the scrubber bank/facade removal and recovery code, the vent recovery
interaction, its language data and generated output, the pipe-seal forwarding code,
and their tests. It does not change rocket code, the charger, chemical refresh,
status files, the ledger, build scripts, other tasks or other worktrees.

F1 keeps the vent as the only cartridge persistence owner. A bound facade is
removable only when its exact face and identity are admitted by the bank. An
empty-hand sneak-use at the vent retrieves at most one orphaned cartridge in
direction order. Only the six adjacent already-loaded cells are inspected.
Unloaded cells, missing/pending facade entities and unreadable facade roots are
not reconciled; a matching facade
identity is never treated as orphaned, even if it is not presently bound. Recovery
clears the cartridge and association at their vent owner before returning the
stack, revokes a conflicting loaded facade's old binding, and reports a dedicated
server message. It neither loads chunks nor creates a facade identity, and safe
withdrawal remains available when new device work is disabled.

F2 retains one loaded opposite target per operation. A local operation context
revalidates the original seal generation, exact seal/target identities, Level,
configuration and active operation after foreign inspection and immediately
before a mutating call. Revocation aborts with the offered item unchanged or an
empty extraction. Existing no-chain, simulation and expired-view tests remain.

## Remaining scope, choices and known risks

Recovery returns one cartridge per empty-hand sneak-use in DOWN, UP, NORTH,
SOUTH, WEST, EAST order. Only the owning face can claim its identity. Matching
but unbound identities are retained conservatively; missing/pending or unreadable
facades and unloaded chunks are not reconciled. An empty conflicting association
has no cartridge to retrieve and remains protected until normal observation can
admit it or the vent is removed. No identity or schema field is invented and no
ADR decision was changed.

W14F's requested fixes and automated checks are complete. The gas charger/D3
escrow prerequisite, chemical cartridge refresh, packaged restart/crash cuts,
R-027 risk acceptance, independent integration review and V1/V2 evidence remain
outside this task. The mixed-root test is an actual loaded cross-chunk snapshot
replay, not a native interrupted-save/restart proof. Manual recovery follows the
existing manual withdrawal boundary; player-file/terrain crash atomicity is not
claimed. The inherited save guard's chunk/Level denial and unbounded retry logs
remain documented risks, and forced replacements or policy-bypassing movers are
not covered by ordinary removal protection.

**Not all W14/v1.8 Required Gates are satisfied.** No version/Gate status, tag or
ledger conclusion was changed. Root should review and integrate these commits,
keep the historical rocket failure available for integration diagnosis, and
continue the remaining v1.8 charging, refresh, persistence and visual work.
