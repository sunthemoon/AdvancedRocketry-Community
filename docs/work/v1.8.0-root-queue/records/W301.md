## Changes and design choices

The reproduction confirms finding 6-1: an actual connected survival fixture with flight disabled spends at least 100 zero-gravity connection ticks outside every station region, returns to the Overworld without a loaded station arrival, and arrives with native counter 80 on the unchanged production code. Its next connection tick, before teleport acknowledgment, disconnects it for floating. The test fails with `arrival counter=80` and the native kick log. The accepted reset fixes that exact test.

`SpaceSafetyReturnService.move` resets `aboveGroundTickCount` once, only after confirming arrival, before the announcement. Both station and Overworld returns use it. Additional assertions cover station success, protected refusal and a dropped job; refusals and dropped jobs retain their counter. `GravityFlightBudget`, its allowance values, station thresholds and `SafeCelestialTravel` are unchanged.

Only the safety return's Overworld branch invokes the new Forge-side arrival selector. Without an intersecting rocket entity, the existing destination is unchanged. Otherwise it considers at most 81 columns in a radius-four square, chooses minimum squared horizontal distance, and breaks ties by ascending x then z. Each candidate uses the loaded chunk's `MOTION_BLOCKING` height, rejects fluid at the feet or floor, checks the player's actual box for block/rocket collision, and requires the collision iterator's one-cell margin to be loaded. No chunk ticket or loading lookup is added. Player boxes larger than 2 by 4 by 2 blocks retain the existing arrival rather than expanding the probe budget. The existing TELEPORT protection check runs on the selected column. An unavailable alternative retains the original point and adds `rocket_avoidance=no_free_column` to the existing INFO audit; successful avoidance and the no-rocket case are also distinguished there.

The choice remains a small Minecraft adapter rather than a pure model, so its heightmap, fluid, collision, rocket-query and loading behavior are tested together in GameTests; no new unit-test class was needed. The original no-rocket Overworld test and its expected position are unchanged. Three new rocket tests cover a nearest free arrival, the blocked/liquid fallback, and rejection of unloaded, liquid and fence-collision alternatives without loading chunks or adding tickets. The connection regression verifies reset, announcement, the unacknowledged arrival tick, and another 100 connected Overworld ticks. Existing suit-motion tests are included in verification.

No operator guide previously described W285's allowance, so the celestial data guide now explains the finite allowance, its application to Slow Falling, the Moon's approximately 0.15-block-per-tick slow descent and the remaining flight-disabled kick boundary.

## Read-only inspection of adjacent moves

- `endgame/elevator/ElevatorRides.java`, at its arrival commit, reuses the rider and connection, calls `teleportTo`, and resets fall distance but not the floating counter. `ElevatorEndpointBlockEntity.onPlatform` checks position, not `onGround` or the native counter. A normally grounded departure may already have cleared the counter, but the move itself does not prevent carrying a held zero-g counter into a 1-g arrival.
- `rocket/rejoin/RocketRejoinGuards.java`, in `pin`, also reuses the connection and calls `teleportTo` without resetting the counter. `RocketRejoinPlacement` can change the guarded session's Level and position before pinning. A fresh login starts with a fresh connection, not the previous login's counter, but an unsupported zero-g wait can accumulate a held counter on that new connection before an Overworld placement. The guard does not independently exempt vanilla's connection floating check.

Both are code-inspection risks, not additional reproduced failures. Neither subsystem was changed; Root can queue and verify any follow-up separately.

## W301F: fixture water left around the Overworld spawn

This section records Root's follow-up task `docs/work/v1.8.0-root-queue/TASK-W301F-LEAKED-TEST-PLAYER.md`, which is on the shared branch only. The work was done on this branch from `5a9a35db0`. Commit `d99d29fd31fe753d56c6437eb22bdca773f1574e` fixes the fixtures, and the next commit adds this section. Nothing was pushed or delegated. Production code, the rejoin code and its tests, the expected-log manifest, the ledger, the status files and QUEUE were not changed.

### Cause

- **No player or entity leaked.** A temporary diagnostic, which was never committed, logged three things before every rocket and rejoin batch: the server's player list, rocket entities within 32 blocks of the spawn, and every fluid cell within 16 blocks of the spawn, from the bottom of the world up to y 210. Before every batch the player list was empty and there were no rockets. The line `safetyRocket left the game` never appears because the fixture logs players out with `PlayerList.remove`. That call fires the logout event but does not broadcast the chat line, and the same is true of every player in this class.
- **Water leaked.**
  - The GameTest world's spawn is (0, 60, 0), beside the sea, with 1,633 natural water sources within 16 blocks.
  - `blockedRocketAlternativesKeepTheOriginalOverworldArrival` placed 81 water sources at y 199 above the spawn. Before the fixture restored them, the sources fell, and the sources on the edges also spread outward.
  - The restore used `UPDATE_CLIENTS` only. It reset the 81 source cells without notifying the water below them, so orphaned waterfalls from y 198 went on falling into the sea.
  - After that batch the surface height at the spawn was 199 instead of 63, and the fluid cells near the spawn grew from 1,639 to 17,500.
- **Why the rejoin tests then fail.**
  - The rejoin fixtures' pads are inside GameTest structures at about y -55, but `RocketRejoinWorld.choose` takes every candidate's height from the surface heightmap. Where the surface above a pad is sea, every pad-ring candidate stands on water, so placement falls back to the ring around the spawn.
  - Before the leak, only one of the 32 spawn-ring columns, (4, -4) at height 68, had no fluid inside the 3-by-3-by-4 envelope that `RocketRejoinWorld.safe` checks. On the shared head's hosted shard, `unsafeImmediatelyBeforeUnguardingRelocatesAndRechecks` was placed with `target=OVERWORLD_SPAWN`.
  - The waterfalls covered that column too. `choose` then found no candidate, the guard logged `state=unsafe`, and it disconnected the player at its 200-tick deadline. This is the hosted failure `Commit fixture stalled: Final phase=1`.

### Reproduction and fix

The sequence was one filtered GameTest server with the three `space_safety_return_rocket_*` batches and all 29 `rocket_rejoin_*` batches, which ran in the full run's order. It was run under the heavy-JVM lock with the diagnostic copied in.

- **Before, at `5a9a35db0`:** 32 tests, 28 passed and 4 failed, all after the blocked batch. The failures were `clonebeforep2installationretainspendingandverifiesnewbody` (`Clone phase=1`), `quitbetweenpinssavesexactpositionandcompletesoffline` (`phase 1 mode=Quit`), `unsafebeforeinstallationretainsobligationacrossloginandservicerestart` (`Pre phase=1`) and `postcommitnocandidatedisconnectpinsandnextloginisordinary` (`Residual phase=1`). Each logged `state=unsafe` immediately after the guard. The Final test passed in this run because its pad ring had a dry candidate (`target=PAD`). The hosted Final failure is the same mechanism with a different pad position.
- **The fix, in `SpaceSafetyReturnGameTests` only:**
  - The new `Fixture.overworldWater` places a water source that cannot flow. Before placing the water, it records stone under the cell and on every side that is not fixture water. Both water placements use it: the blocked variant's 81 cells, and the single water cell of the far loaded-chunk test at (4,000,001, 199, 4,000,002), which had the same uncontained fall into its far chunk.
  - `close()` now restores the Overworld cells with setup's flags, `UPDATE_CLIENTS | UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE`, and before the clean-ups. The restore therefore notifies no neighbour, cannot load the far test's unloaded neighbour chunks, and runs while that test's ticket still holds its chunk.
  - The free variant, every assertion and every timeout are unchanged. Contained water never flows, so the fix holds whether a test passes, fails or times out, and the existing `@AfterBatch` hooks still close any fixture that a failed test leaves open.
- **After, at `d99d29fd3`:** all 32 passed. Before and after the blocked batch, the spawn height was 63 and there were 1,639 fluid cells. Clone, Quit, Pre and Residual were placed on the spawn ring and passed.
