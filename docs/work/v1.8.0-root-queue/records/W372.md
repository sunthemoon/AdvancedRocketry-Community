### `ordinaryL2` and shared scenario progression

`RocketSettlementIdleGameTests.ordinaryL2` no longer supplies model-only barrier
tokens to `coordinator.retire`, edits saved marker NBT, reloads an artificially
pruned rocket, or manually clears source markers. Its real loader admission
captures the numeric baseline and L1 generation; the runtime must perform native
C3-C8 validation, apply, joint readback, checked retirement and pruning. The
SETTLEMENT and NORMALIZED sibling scenarios share that accounted native
preparation, then exercise their own O1-O4/normalization and flight progression;
they do not all call `ordinaryL2`.

The old callback's `records.noneMatch` success assertion is now the equivalent
progress requirement `if (records.anyMatch) return`. A retained operation cannot
reach `released()` or either successful flight. While it exists, the test also
asserts that the rocket is not idle and its acknowledged journal has no transfer.
Writer-idle and both marker-empty conditions likewise gate progress instead of
being manufactured by fixture surgery. After those gates, explicit assertions
require the issued sequence, writer generation greater than L1, journal action
authority, removal of the account row, both epochs equal to 1, loader/rocket
amounts 4,000/1,000, idle authority and exact native envelope equality after an
unedited reload. Each sibling still has its unchanged 1,200-tick timeout and
must finish two passenger flights. An invariant violation fails at its assertion;
a stuck record, writer, marker or later flight never reaches success and times
out within that original budget. The conditional rewrite changes failure timing,
not the set of states allowed to succeed.
