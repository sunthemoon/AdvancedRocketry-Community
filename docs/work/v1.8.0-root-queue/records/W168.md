### Reservation note, written before the fix

A reservation is computed once, from the record as admitted or as restored, and is
held until the record is removed; no transition recomputes it. It must therefore
bound every state that shares the record's fixed fields: every state the record can
reach later, and every state a loadable file can hold. The loadable states are
exactly what each record constructor accepts, because the codecs decode into those
constructors and `finishRestore` only adds quarantines and recoveries. NBT numbers
and UUIDs have fixed widths, so the largest encoding is the legal state with every
optional entry present, the longest enum names and the longest strings.

| Kind | Largest legal state | Today's sizer | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mission | QUARANTINED from `claim_pending_discovery` (the longest previous status, and the only one that keeps both `ready_at` and `resolved_at`), with a 48-character reason and, for asteroid and gas missions, the longest terminal display, a paying terminal and an acknowledgement epoch. A DATA mission that needs discovery reaches it by transitions: CLAIM_PENDING_DISCOVERY, then the load repair's MISSING_SATELLITE quarantine. Every kind can load in it, because the needs-discovery check applies only to the current status. | The larger of CLAIMED with both times, and QUARANTINED from READY with `ready_at` only. | **40 bytes short for every mission**: `resolved_at` is 22 bytes (1 + 2 + 11 + 8) and the previous status is 18 characters longer. This is the review's 1,389 against 1,349. |
| Satellite | OPERATIONAL with `current_mission_id` (41 bytes) and, for solar, a `receiver`. RECOVERY_REQUIRED is 6 characters longer but cannot hold a mission; RETIRED is shorter. Survey charge fields are fixed-width; orbit body and blueprint never change. | The larger of OPERATIONAL with a mission and RECOVERY_REQUIRED, both with the solar receiver. | None. |
| Instance | QUARANTINED (the longest state name) with `expires_at` and `allocated_mission`; QUARANTINED and DEPLETED accept every combination. Yield, origin and identifiers never change. | The larger of ALLOCATED and QUARANTINED, both with expiry and allocation. | None. |
| Ground job | QUARANTINED from PROCESSED: both times, a CREDITED analysis with its claim, 128-character source and processor levels, and a 48-character reason at three bytes per character (ground reasons accept any string). CANCELLED has an equally long name but no completion or claim. | Exactly that state. | None. |
| Account | Fixed-width fields: 121 bytes as a named root and 118 inside the list. | `ACCOUNT_BYTES`, 128. | None. |

The proof also needs the root framing to stay within `ROOT_FRAMING_BYTES` (4,096)
and each list element to be its sizer measurement less the 3-byte named-root
header. The new tests measure both.

Two related observations are outside the fix. `SatelliteRecordFit.resourceMissionBytes`,
the table-load fit check, still sizes QUARANTINED from READY. That is the largest
state an asteroid or gas mission reaches by transitions, a loaded record was already
within its 4 KiB bound when decoded, and a resource mission held from
CLAIM_PENDING_DISCOVERY can be neither released nor cancelled, so it is unchanged.
Releasing a non-discovery mission held from CLAIM_PENDING_DISCOVERY throws in the
`MissionState` constructor instead of returning a refusal. Only a file this code did
not write can hold one; it is noted for Root and not changed.

**Fix.** `missionBytes` sizes QUARANTINED from CLAIM_PENDING_DISCOVERY with both
times in place of QUARANTINED from READY, for every kind, and keeps the CLAIMED
variant and the record-bound cap. A kind-aware reservation would save 40 bytes on
missions that cannot reach that state by transitions, but a loaded file can still
hold it, so the uniform variant is required. R132 below supersedes the claim that
its capped result always proves a fit. The other sizers, the ground
proof and the whole-root fallback are unchanged; the fallback still runs wherever the
reservations exceed 16 MiB.

**Tests.** The review's probe becomes a regression: START must refuse with
STORAGE_BUDGET, as the whole-root preflight does, leaving the root, accounts,
reservations, clock and mutation sequence unchanged. A worst-state test per kind
enumerates every constructor-legal status, previous status and optional field, and
checks each encoding against the reservation computed from every other legal state

### Capacity effect

Corrected under R132/W168-R4: only mission reservations change in R129, and each
grows by **0 to 40 bytes**, capped at the unchanged 4,096-byte record limit.
Instance and ground-job reservations are unchanged. For a homogeneous mission
population the capacity is `min(3072, floor(6291456 / reservation))`; for other
sections it is likewise the smaller of the byte and count limits. The admission
count limits are 3,072 missions, 2,048 instances and 2,048 ground jobs (16 per
owner). The following are measured shapes, not global size maxima or loss rates.

| Shape | Reservation before → after | Fit in its budget before → after | Effect on admission |
|---|---|---|---|
| DATA mission, fixture IDs and `data-v1` | 571 → 611 | 3,072 → 3,072 | None; the count limit applies first |
| DATA mission, long IDs (the review's shape) | 1,349 → 1,389 | 3,072 → 3,072 | None for this homogeneous population |
| SURVEY mission, long IDs | 1,026 → 1,066 | 3,072 → 3,072 | None for this homogeneous population |
| ASTEROID mission, 17 long reward items | 3,777 → 3,817 | 1,665 → 1,648 | 17 fewer (1.0%) |
| GAS mission, W128 shape | 3,519 → 3,559 | 1,787 → 1,767 | 20 fewer (1.1%) |
| Mission at saturation | 4,090 → 4,096 / 4,096 → 4,096 | 1,538 → 1,536 / 1,536 → 1,536 | +6 / +0 reservation bytes |
| Instance, fixture / 17 long yield items | 498 / 3,194 | 2,048 / 656 | Unchanged |
| Ground job, fixture / longest | 1,221 / 1,406 | 858 / 745 | Unchanged |

For a homogeneous population, the mission byte budget limits admission first when
its reservation exceeds 2,048 bytes (6 MiB / 3,072). Shapes whose old reservation
was 2,009 to 2,048 bytes therefore cross from count-limited to byte-limited. Loss
depends on the shape: for example 2,049 → 2,089 bytes gives 3,070 → 3,011 records,
about 1.92%, while saturation gives smaller or zero growth. A mixed section filled
by large records can also refuse a small new mission; the crossover is not a
per-request admission guarantee.

**A restored root at today's budgets.** Restore reserves without checking any budget,
so nothing refuses to load. `aRootFilledToTodaysMissionBudgetStillLoads` restores
1,787 W128 gas missions, the most the old reservations admit (6,288,453 of
6,291,456 bytes). It loads with no repair and re-encodes to an equal root. Its mission
reservations are now 6,359,933 bytes, 68,477 over the budget. A new gas mission is
refused with STORAGE_BUDGET; a data-satellite launch, which admits a first mission,
would be refused the same way until retention or a purge frees space. Its existing
records keep working: a purge succeeded and released 3,559 bytes, and a ground
START succeeded through the reservation proof.

The R129 capacity correction alone does not refuse already-admitted work on the
tested canonical budget-filled root. This replaces the earlier universal claim;
R132's raw-NBT counterexample is a separate, previously missed proof failure. Only admissions of new
satellites, missions, instances, jobs and accounts consult section budgets, and the
mission budget is consulted only for new missions. A ground change on an existing
record keeps its answer: when the proof fails, the exact whole-root preflight
decides, as before. The proof still holds for every root within today's budgets.
Their reservations total at most about 13.5 MiB: the four record sections' 13 MiB of
budgets, 4,096 accounts at 128 bytes, and the framing. The change adds at most
320 KiB (40 bytes × the 8,192-mission load bound), leaving them below 13.9 MiB
against 16 MiB.

For these canonically sized fixtures, the slow path runs on roots restored above
their section budgets, and now slightly earlier. R132 supersedes the earlier
claim that this describes all possible slow-path roots: failed sizing also requires
the fallback, even below the section budgets. For W128's gas shape with one account, a START

6 MiB mission budget that admission enforces.
