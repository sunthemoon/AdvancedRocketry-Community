The write scope is `satellite/mission/GroundSurveys.java`, the related `RecordSizer`

The measurement comes first. The standalone benchmark uses the real codec sizer,
50 measured calls per case after warm-up, Xmx4G, and the machine-wide heavy-JVM lock.
Each measured call is a complete domain operation. Fixture construction is outside
the timed interval. It reports START, account creation through final analysis, and
an ordinary survey change on a restored near-byte-bound root. Additional
reservation-provable fixtures distinguish the optimized cases from the fallback.

The change will reuse the existing reservation proof. Its candidate bound adds
the new job and instance reservations for START and `RecordSizer.ACCOUNT_BYTES`
only for a newly created account. Existing records remain covered by their
lifecycle reservations. A failed proof still calls the unchanged encoding and
round-trip preflight. Validation, epoch overflow checks, publication and refusal
codes stay in their original order. Boundary tests will cover an exact fit, one
byte over, and restored over-budget roots, including refusal without mutation.

If the post-change measurements miss docs/17, implementation stops at this
behavior-preserving optimization. Numbers and options go to Root; this worker
does not choose a new admission policy or per-tick quota. Root arranges the fresh
read-only review before merge.
