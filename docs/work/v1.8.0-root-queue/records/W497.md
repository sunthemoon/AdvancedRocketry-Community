### F01 (medium, P01): the black hole generator's hull is lifted off its structure

- Where: `S/client/model/ObjModelFixture.java:92`, offset y changed from 0.5 to 3.5.
- The model is a head 3 blocks tall on a stem 1 block wide and 3 blocks long. At C6 the head was at y -1..2,
  x -0.75..1.75, z 0..2.75, which is the pattern's box (-1..2, -1..2, 0..3) within a quarter block. Only the
  stem hung below, at y -4..-1.
- W493's rule "the hull's lowest point sits on the pattern's base" lifts everything by 3. The stem (x 0..1,
  z 1.125..2.125) is now inside the pattern's solid centre column, where it cannot be seen, and the head is at
  y 2..5, entirely above the 3 by 3 by 3 structure. The formed machine is a bare cube of blocks with the hull
  standing on top, 6 blocks tall.
- The accretion disc is drawn at y 2.1 with radii 1.2 to 2.6 (`S/client/AccretionDiscRenderer.java:44`). At C6
  it floated 0.1 block above the head's top. Now it cuts through the bottom of the lifted head.
- W487 did not propose a vertical move for this hull. It only noted the stem below the pattern.
- Fix: put offset y back to 0.5 and keep the new z of 0.625, which centres the head. Then
  `K4ModelProjection` (`S/machine/classic/runtime/K4ModelProjection.java:36`) needs y -4..2 again, and two
  tests need the generator's case changed: the `shift` table in `K4ObjModelTest.aligned` and the base assertion
  in `drawnHullIsCenteredOnPatternAndStartsAtItsBaseInEveryFacing`. Whether the stem below the structure is
  acceptable is then the open question for the frames, as before.

### F02 (medium, P01): the elevator anchor's tether seat is half a block south of the tether

### F10 (note, P02): what polygon offset does at a distance

- `S/client/model/HullRenderTypes.java` equals vanilla `entity_cutout` (same shader, texture, transparency,
  lightmap, overlay, format, mode, buffer size, crumbling and sorting flags) plus `POLYGON_OFFSET_LAYERING`. I
  compared it with the vanilla definition from memory of 1.20.1, and the unit test pins the format, the mode and
  the layering name.
- Polygon offset pulls a face forward by a fixed number of depth steps, and a depth step grows with the square
  of the distance. A hull face that lies slightly behind a block face therefore starts to win from some distance
  on. By my estimate (24-bit depth, near plane 0.05) that is about 0.01 block at 30 blocks and 0.05 block at 64
  blocks. The observatory's hull, offset by 0.005 block, is the closest case.
- This is how the vanilla layering behaves everywhere and I do not see a better small fix. W487's GPU check
  already asks for a view from about 30 blocks; add one from about 60 for the observatory.
- As W487 wrote, this does not help if two surfaces inside one mesh fight.

### My view on scale

Keep scale 1 for all five.

- All five patterns are solid: none has an air cell. A hull scaled to fit inside its pattern's box is drawn
  inside opaque blocks. Only faces that share a plane with an outer block face would show, and they would show
  through P02's offset as a flat skin.
- The sizes do not fit one factor. The railgun is 9 by 9 by 11 over 3 by 3 by 6, the drill 11 by 9 by 3.25 over
  3 by 3 by 3, and the anchor 9 by 10 by 1.5 over 5 by 5 by 2. A uniform factor that fits the footprint leaves
  the railgun's barrel at 3.7 of 6 blocks and flattens the drill to 0.9 block. A factor per axis distorts the
  models.
- Parts of these hulls have to meet things drawn at full size: the tether seat and the beacon-beam tether, and
  the barrel and the pattern's iron column.

So the real choice per hull is between a full-scale hull placed as well as it can be, and no hull, which means
the block models that the mirrored assembler already uses. That choice needs W491's frames. What geometry can
say is this:

- The drill and the railgun are placed as well as scale 1 allows. Their overhang of 3 to 4 blocks over open
  ground, without collision, is what the frames have to judge.
- The black hole generator should go back to its C6 height (F01).
- The elevator anchor should be centred on its tether seat (F02), and its height is a choice between two
  imperfect looks (F03).
- The rolling machine should keep the west move only (F04).
