## Findings

**F1 (production, liveness): a named-transfer loader fill is never reconciled once its L1 is durable.** The fill is
admitted, reserves its floor key and reaches a durable L1. Then `RocketTransferRecoveryService` fences the rocket's
own COMMITTED landed record (`ARCE_TRANSFER_FENCED ... reason=ESCROW_PENDING_OR_TOMBSTONE`), because
`RocketTransferFences.actionAllowed` treats any unresolved escrow record naming the logical rocket as a lock. Loader
reconciliation needs that record settled (`RocketTransferService.escrowReconciliationBoundary`), so
`EscrowReconciliationView.capture` defers forever. `smoke-native-02` showed it in the same session: the fill never
reached C2 and the old stop exceeded its bound. `w107c-native-02` showed it across both restarts of both named cuts:
the record stays PREPARED with reason NONE, `boundary=false`, `mutation_allowed=false`, no floor is installed and the
reservation stays. Nothing is lost or released, but nothing persists a reason either, so the hold is invisible to the
reason table and not eligible for accept-current, which W11A section 5 limits to manual conflicts and stalls. W11b2's
named-fill fixtures likewise kept their journal fenced until O3J ([W11B2-F7F8.md](W11B2-F7F8.md), its warning audit),
but they were settled by operators, not reconciled. The NONE-guard fill (`R1_FLOORS_256`) is unaffected. This
contradicts W11A section 7's R1 row ("outstanding work retires") for named transfers. Not changed here (task rule);
it needs Root's decision.

**F2 (diagnostic): a relocated loader reports `MISSING_ENDPOINT`, not `RELOCATED_ENDPOINT`.** Ordinary
reconciliation looks only at the recorded position, so a loader whose block and root moved within the chunk is
diagnosed as missing there; the moved device keeps its marker and stays locked. The R3 row's requirement (locked
repair where detectable) holds; W11A section 4 names RELOCATED_ENDPOINT for detected relocation.

**F3 (recorded behaviour, R3 ledger cases): nothing persists a reason for orphan markers.** With the ledger missing,
replaced or behind, no record names the endpoints, so recovery cannot persist LEDGER_REPLACED or LEDGER_BEHIND; the
endpoints stay locked by their orphan markers, and pruning refuses silently. This matches W11A section 4 ("persist
REPAIR/reason when writable, otherwise retain runtime lock"). Its documented limit stands: with BEHIND, the restored
ledger's watermark is 0, so a different device's next fill would be issued sequence 1 again; not exercised here.

**E4 residual.** `EscrowLedgerWriterStaleCallbackTest` replays generation 1's IO task after generation 2 is captured:
`tick` refuses "Stale or foreign writer callback", acknowledges nothing, and the successor commits normally. This is
an R-032-class in-process invariant, not an S2 PASS.

## Choices where R43 or the ADR left room

- E1: the vanilla store is still submitted unchanged; only the barrier's retained future fails, after that write
  completes (so the stopped disk holds the source as written). The gate also requires the start scenario and the
  release-test server gate, and the fault fires once per store, for overworld chunk 4,4 only.
- E2: 1,572,864 deterministic random bytes in a vanilla chest's Forge data at 76,100,76, placed before the baseline
  save. The record decodes to 1,592,351 bytes, inside the 4 MiB bound; vanilla compresses it to about 1.57 MB.
- R1: the seeded floors are schema-2 neutral floors (no transfer). A named floor whose transfer is absent from the
  64-record journal is collected at once, so neutral floors are the only way a native world holds 255. Floor *i*
  retires earlier sequence *i* + 1, so the seeded watermark is 255 or 256 and the fixture's fills follow it; the oracle
  rebases those sequences to reuse the matrix checks. Each named rocket gets a COMMITTED landed record whose arrival is
  its own snapshot, settled by production's transfer recovery before any fill.
- R1 after F1: the named cuts stop at K2 (L1 replaced, before its callback) instead of after the callback, which is
  unreachable in-session. COALESCED queues the second same-key fill while the first is queued instead of after its
  credit. The expected restart outcome was not changed.
- R3: every cut stops at K6 (both endpoints applied and stored). REPLACED is the run's own generation-1 copy with a
  new ledger ID; RELOCATED moves the block and root by palette and position edits. The generation-1 copy is captured
  by the probe on the writer's own thread, because a harness poll could block a Windows atomic replace.
- R3 oracle: it requires a persisted manual reason, not a particular name, because W11A section 7's R3 row requires
  "locked repair where detectable". Reasons are logged and compared with the design above.
