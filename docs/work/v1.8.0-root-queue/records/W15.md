## Design choices

Reachability is existential, not a yield or resource-consumption simulation. Each
chance candidate counts as potentially producible, including iridium; this does
not assert that all five candidates can appear in one capped batch. The etcher
lens remains an AND requirement and is neither manufactured by the recipe nor
consumed by the monotone evaluator. Vanilla block tags are OR selectors, so their
unobtainable alternatives cannot serve as survival witnesses; direct forbidden
inputs remain subject to the existing strict assertion. These choices preserve
ADR-064 section 9 and ADR-066 sections 6.2 and 6.3.

`PackagedRecipeGraph` is 538 lines. It was checked for mixed responsibilities and
remains a test-side packaged-resource importer; no runtime framework was added.
