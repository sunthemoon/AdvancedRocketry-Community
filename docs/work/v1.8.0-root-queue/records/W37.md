### Sleep

- The rule applies to a non-Overworld mapped surface or Space. In code, that is the Space Level, any
  Level in this mod's namespace, or any Level the catalog maps to a body. The Overworld, the Nether,
  the End and other mods' unmapped Levels keep vanilla rules.
- `PlayerSleepInBedEvent` is refused when any bed cell (head and foot) is:
  - unloaded;
  - PENDING;
  - not breathable, by the existing `AtmosphereManager`.

  The refusal is `NOT_POSSIBLE_HERE`, with an action-bar reason.
- In Space (owner decision 05), a bed inside a committed station region also needs live VISIT
  permission (owner, member or operator). A registry that is not operational refuses. A supplied
  sealed room outside every station qualifies.
- D1 outcome 3: when the check admits an off-world bed, that operation's own native spawn setter is
  cancelled, so the saved respawn point (or its absence) is kept. Other setters are untouched,
  including a forced `/spawnpoint`-style one. This takes effect once the types are natural; today
  vanilla returns before it reaches the setter.

### Respawn

- `PlayerList.respawn` removes the old player from the player list, then discards it from its Level
  (`EntityLeaveLevelEvent`, reason DISCARDED). Only after that does it read the saved point. That
  removal is the hook; I found no Forge event before respawn, and ADR-073 forbids a new Mixin
  without its own ADR.
- An off-world saved point is judged by `SleepRespawnRules.decideRespawn`:
  - With allow off: Overworld.
  - With allow on, every one of these must hold, even with force:
    - the Level exists and the catalog maps exactly one body to it;
    - every chunk within 3 blocks is already loaded;
    - the block is a vanilla bed where beds work, or an anchor that vanilla would use;
    - vanilla's stand-up search finds an arrival;
    - the standing body has no solid collision, liquid or burning block;
    - in Space, a station there permits a visit.
  - Force skips only the final air check (feet and eye cells breathable).
- A refused point is cleared from the discarded player, so vanilla runs its existing Overworld
  respawn. The respawn event then restores the point on the new player and sends a chat reason.
  Nothing loads a chunk; the facts are asked lazily and stop at the first refusal.
- Both transient maps (admitted bed operations and pending refusals) are instance state. They are
  cleared at every server tick end and on server stop.

## Questions for Root

1. **Natural dimension types (blocks actual sleep).**
   - D1 outcome 1 changes only `natural` from false to true for the six types.
   - The packaged `moon`, `space`, `mars` and `venus` types come from the historic v1.4 output.
     `tau_ceti_f` and `tau_ceti_g` come from `V180ExoplanetWorldgen` (v1.8).
   - A new v1.8 writer for the first four would duplicate the v1.4 entries. `processResources` and
     `sourcesJar` would then fail unless `build.gradle` excludes the v1.4 copies, as it already does
     for v0.3 (`currentSupersededV030Types`). This matches D1 outcome 2's "one current writer and
     one packaged entry per type".
   - I may not edit `build.gradle`, and flipping only the two Tau Ceti types would be a partial D1
     outcome, so I changed no type.
   - D1 also requires its thirteen qualification rows (prior-world readback, compass, clock and
     portal behaviour, restart, real clients) before or with that change.
   - GameTest 4 already covers the natural case.
2. **Sleeping does not save an off-world point, even with allow on.** D1 outcome 3 and owner decision
   03 keep the saved point on every ordinary off-world bed operation. ADR-066 does not say otherwise,
   so I did not treat this as a disagreement. The effect is that `allowPlanetRespawn` applies only to
   points saved some other way: commands, other mods or older data. If the owner wants sleeping to
   save a planet point when allow is on, that is a new decision.

## Choices where the ADR was open

- **The saved point is kept after a refusal**, and the chat message says so. This follows D1
  outcome 4: existing records are retained. Vanilla would drop an unusable bed point. Keeping it means
  a temporarily unloaded or disabled point can work later.
- **Force without allow does nothing.** The ADR states force only as "if allow is true ... unless
  force is true".
- **Real bed or anchor.** Only a vanilla `BedBlock` where beds work, or a `RespawnAnchorBlock` that
  vanilla would use (charged unless forced, where anchors work), counts. A forced plain coordinate
  falls back. So does a modded bed that does not extend `BedBlock`.
- **Hazards.** These count: a solid collision of the standing body (`noCollision`), any fluid in the
  body cells, and a burning block in the body or the floor. Vanilla's search already avoids most
  of them.
- **Loaded.** Every chunk within 3 blocks of the saved block must be loaded, because the vanilla
  stand-up search reads that far. A respawn point far from every player therefore falls back, even
  with force, as the ADR requires.
- **Scope.** The rule covers the Space Level, this mod's namespace (so a removed body's Level is
  still judged as a missing body) and any Level the catalog maps. A pack that maps a body onto the
  Nether would bring the Nether under the rule.
- **Sleep cells.** Both the head and the foot must be breathable. A PENDING cell refuses, so a room
  still being scanned cannot be slept in. Sleep is judged only at admission. Losing air later means
  vacuum damage, which wakes the sleeper.
- **No new ADR or risk entry.** Nothing refuses a save or quarantines data.

## Known risks

- **The respawn hook relies on vanilla's order inside `PlayerList.respawn`** (discard the old player,
  then read its point). If another mod rewrites `respawn`, the hook may not fire, and the native
  respawn would use the off-world point.
- **Another mod can block the clear.** If it cancels `PlayerSetSpawnEvent` for the clear, the
  service logs `ARCE_PLANET_RESPAWN_NOT_CLEARED` (WARN) and the native respawn continues.
- **Behaviour change for existing worlds.** Before W37, a forced `/spawnpoint` or a bed point on
  another planet or in Space was honoured, because the types have `bed_works=true`. Now, with the
  default options, such players respawn in the Overworld.
- **Large fixture class.** `SleepRespawnGameTests` is over 500 lines. Each test builds and clears
  its own fixtures, including two committed stations deleted at the end.
