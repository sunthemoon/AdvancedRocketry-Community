### Fixture and matrix

The diagnostic JAR adds test-source classes to an exported committed tree. All pre-existing production JAR entries
must be byte-identical. The real installed sealed coordinator, transaction writer, native terrain/entity proofs,
carrier materializer and restart recovery run unchanged. No live source world is used.

The fixture is a saved two-member rocket with seven tagged diamonds in a chest snapshot and an iron block.
Small roots have no ballast. Near-limit roots have 255 inert assembly records in an absent dimension, leaving one
slot for the real sealed transaction U. Qualify their uncompressed bytes (at least 99% and at most 64 MiB with U),
record identities and codec round-trip. Padding is deterministic, split within existing payload limits. Ballast is
never a live rocket or resource. Recovery must retain all ballast IDs without loading its absent dimension.

Cases: small/near quiet; near bounded traffic (one ballast progress mutation and checkpoint request per 20 ticks,
at most 20 mutations, followed by quiet observation); C1 and C3 one-shot UNKNOWN; C1 and C3 proof-expiry controls;
and near interrupted drain. C3 controls first reach election on the small root, then add the near-limit ballast
before the election checkpoint; this is a phase-isolation control, not an end-to-end near-limit completion sample.
UNKNOWN is injected after atomic replacement plus failed readback, once. Expiry controls hold that phase's IO
committer for 120 server ticks, then release it; they do not change the 100-tick proof or 200-tick request/retry bounds.
Natural outcomes (completion, rejection, rollback, retained hold) are observations, not preselected successes.

### Observation and shutdown

Observe each request/recovery for at most 1,200 server ticks, with a separate 240-second wall-clock observation
ceiling. A ceiling produces `NOT_COMPLETED`, not a product timeout or successful completion. The runner requests
normal stop, releases only its own diagnostic latches, and waits for process termination before copying/reopening
the world. It must not use an observation ceiling to kill a live writer. A further 180-second cleanup observation
ceiling records a live-process blocker and preserves the world and lock; it grants no permission to delete/reopen.

The interrupted-drain control stops while C1 IO is held. In the server's stopping callback it latches shutdown,
interrupts the owner thread, calls the real journal close, and releases the diagnostic latch from a helper after
one second. Measure close/drain/join as a combined interval and executor shutdown-to-termination independently;
verify interruption survives close and IO has ended,
then explicitly clear the injected interrupt solely to let vanilla finish its remaining shutdown. This is not a
watchdog/power-loss claim or a production shutdown timeout. Ordinary cases record the same close interval without
an injected interrupt. No gameplay effect is allowed in either callback. Two separate recovery starts use the same
stopped world without editing its NBT. Each is observed and stopped normally, even if retained work persists.
