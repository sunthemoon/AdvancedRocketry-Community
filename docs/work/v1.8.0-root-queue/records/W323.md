## Source investigation and reproduction

Both historical failures were inspected. W321's log at line 17308 reports one stone button at
`(134.58568821980097, -60.0, 1598.3745954623034)`, age 79. W322's final log at line 17330 reports
one at `(134.79161595424662, -60.0, 1598.5510601845854)`, age 80. Both assertions ran at test tick 8,
after the `escrow_governed_return_adapter` batch. The sibling's three-item controls are created,
checked and discarded synchronously, so they cannot account for an item that old.

The final full-run observer traced the matching earlier-batch mechanism. Water created button
entity `1f14aa22-fc41-401d-9913-5e9b7e9cd46a` at server tick 80760, at
`(131.36463548659623, -59.80243152244773, 1600.672923056683)`. Its creation stack contains
`WaterFluid.beforeDestroyingBlock`, `FlowingFluid.spreadTo` and `Block.dropResources`.
The neighboring cells include water and the intact command-block support at `(131, -60, 1601)`.
The framework control at `(131, -60, 1600)` belongs to
`returnAfterF4ProvesSharedSourceAndDestinationSlot`, in `escrow_governed_return_adapter`.
The adapter started at tick 80831 and passed its tick-8 check at 80839. A surviving item from
that creation would therefore be 79 ticks old, matching the historical failures. This traces
neighboring allocations across batches, not a demonstrated reset of the entire grid.

The historical logs contain no UUIDs, so their exact entities cannot be recovered. The final
run's layout differed, and the traced native predecessor item did not enter the checked area;
the original unseeded failure was not reproduced in that run. The earlier-batch framework
button is the supported source diagnosis, rather than a proven retrospective UUID match.

A deterministic regression places a button item in the observation area before the operation.
With the original fixture it reproduced the assertion failure: 2/3 tests passed and the seeded
test failed with one age-8 stone button. With the fix, all three fresh-world repetitions passed.

  asserts an empty start-control observation area before control retirement or water flow. Its

  that its pre-operation control is removed. Its existing negative control now verifies all

  Controls have zero velocity and no gravity, remain in their own test's regions and are
  discarded synchronously in `finally`.

stack. No item-type filtering was added. Existing observation bounds, the tick-8 assertion,
20-tick timeouts and all production budgets remain unchanged. No adapter behavior, ADR decision,
asset, provenance entry, persistence schema, network protocol or stable content ID changed.

The registration and component tests use the shared preparation and water helper, so they receive
the same empty-area preconditions without separate file edits. The main-source
