## Completed scope and design decisions

Both bipropellant motors and their block items now share ordinary `forge:obj`
models with placed blocks and assembled rockets, following the
[committed-first design](W255-design.md) and P1 contract. Their two OBJ/MTL/PNG
triples were inventoried before import through the
[LF plan](../../../../tools/import/v180-w255-plan.json) and
[coverage table](W255-asset-coverage.csv). The
[provenance record](../../../provenance/v1.8.0-w255-bipropellant-motor-models.json)
remains `PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW`: six targets, 912 budget vertices, 27,318 bytes.
OBJ/PNG bytes are unchanged; the importer records both `map_Kd` namespace and
block-atlas folder rewrites. R281 chooses `_classic` for consistency with P1;
P2 itself has no sprite-name collision.

Both upstream `facing=down` variants have an identity transform. Preserve the
source bounds `[-0.0005, 0.005, -0.0005]` to `[1.0005, 1, 1.0005]`, including
the tiny rim overhang. Items inherit their block geometry and P1's explicit
camera transforms. `flip_v=false`, `automatic_culling=false` and the assembled
rocket renderer remain P1's contract. There is no runtime fallback or new
renderer, block entity, state, protocol or persistence field. IDs, recipes,
fuel/oxidizer roles and server resource accounting are unchanged.

The server-use audit covered scan/transactions/metrics, sealed terrain and
placement, atmosphere tags/boundaries/collision/detection, landing/arrival,
passenger clearance, elevator/laser geometry, plant support and sky-dependent
environment/solar/research queries. All retain their previous results. The
local mapped Forge bytecode confirms that `noOcclusion()` only clears
`canOcclude`; collision, solidity and support remain full cube. Both motors
therefore reuse P1's non-occluding registration; the helper was renamed from
`monoMotor` to `chemicalMotor`. Intended vanilla light consequences are the
same as P1: light transmits, affecting spawn-light levels, grass and beacon
beams. Sky attenuation remains one, so `canSeeSky` eligibility is unchanged.
The detailed audit is retained as `server-audit.md`, SHA-256
`ad3080447368095cb9098b5e50096b0637869d651bf18bd31482aeb7920df9e8`,
under the evidence directory below. `ModBlocks` remains a single-purpose
registry at its existing 507-line size; no additional responsibility was added.
