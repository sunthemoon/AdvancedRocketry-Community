# W115: structural decoding for durable fluid payloads

## Plan (committed before any code)

Base `91b9d665` (W110 and W110F merged), clean worktree `D:/GitHub/arce-v180-w115`. Authority:
[TASK-W115](../TASK-W115-FLUID-STRUCTURAL-DECODING.md) and Root decision R39 as recorded in R-035. This is a
persistence slice; an independent read-only review follows before merge.

**The defect.** Startup (`ManagedSavedDataPayloadValidator.java:33`, `ResourceEscrowSavedData.java:19`) decodes the
ledger through `EscrowCodec.operation` (`:54`) into `EscrowOperation`'s constructor. Its fill branch
(`EscrowOperation.java:43-50`) and its cargo branch, through `EscrowCargoPayload.decode` (`EscrowCargoPayload.java:51-55`,
constructor `:31-44`), look the Fluid up and require the native round trip, so a removed or renamed Fluid refuses the
world as `INVALID_SCHEMA`.

**Design.** No bound, field set, hash, saved format, schema version or dictionary changes.

1. New public `EscrowFluidCodec`, beside `EscrowItemStackCodec`. `validateStructure` checks the bound (8,192 bytes,
   depth 12, 384 nodes), the exact field set, the canonical ID syntax, an int `Amount` of 1 to 1,000 and a compound
   `Tag`; it never touches a registry or builds a `FluidStack`. `validateNative` adds the registry lookup, non-empty
   and the exact canonical round trip, and returns the `FluidStack`. Both throw `IllegalArgumentException` as today.
2. `EscrowOperation`: the fill branch uses `validateStructure`; the cargo branch reads its cursor through a new
   structural `EscrowCargoPayload.validateStructure`. New `Kind.fluid()` and `nativeFluid()`, the one native check
   for fill and fluid cargo operations.
3. `EscrowCargoPayload`'s constructor and `decode` stay native, so an `EscrowCargoPayload` object, which is what
   eligibility and `RocketCargoMutation` act on, exists only after the native check. W110F kept
   `EscrowItemStackCodec.decode` native the same way; existing assertions stay unchanged.

Cargo C2, O2 and readback do not exist (the driver answers `RECONCILIATION_UNIMPLEMENTED`,
`EscrowReconciliationDriver.java:50-52`). C-E must call `op.nativeFluid()`, or the native `EscrowCargoPayload.decode`,
before `RocketCargoMutation.apply` and before changing the device tank, at admission, O2 and readback, and map a
refusal to REPAIR for that operation only.

**Scope of a hold (AGENTS 3.3).** Held: one operation, phase `REPAIR` with reason `OTHER_STATE`, the existing manual
state that the scheduler (`EscrowReconciliationScheduler.java:97-99`) already sets for C2 refusals. Its endpoints stay
locked, and under the existing rules operations chained behind it on the same endpoints wait; other components, the
ledger, its writer and the world continue. Duration: until audited settlement or offline repair; restoring the mod
before the first C2 attempt avoids the hold, after it the hold is sticky. Logs: one new WARN
`ARCE_ESCROW_FLUID_UNAVAILABLE` per refused C2 attempt, at most once per operation per session until the REPAIR is
persisted, plus the existing scheduler WARN. Before the check the driver only captures read-only and prunes markers
of already retired operations (C8, no resources change).

**Tests.** A new class, run first against the commit before structural decoding to reproduce the refusal: an
unregistered Fluid and a Fluid renamed through a Forge registry alias, in a fill and both cargo kinds, PREPARED and
SETTLING, through the real `WorldDataMigrationService` pre-start, decode and re-encode, SavedData load and coordinator;
the file bytes are unchanged and no backup appears; the native check refuses the same record. A consumer test drives
the real scheduler and `applyNext`: the held fill becomes REPAIR without publication, the other fill still publishes
and keeps `NONE`, and the persisted ledger differs only in that record. Admission gates refuse without a sequence,
reservation or queue entry; settlement freezing, receipts and the inspector work on the held record.
