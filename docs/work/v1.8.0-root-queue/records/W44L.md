## Design decisions and boundaries

- All new cuts/actions require the existing release-test hooks, a recognized H cut,
  server-thread ownership where applicable, and the empty-server gate. Defaults
  remain no-ops or the supplied ordinary committer. Production persistence order,
  holds, retry intervals, budgets and eligibility rules are unchanged.
- The writer latch is single-use, bounded by 1,200 ticks and 60 seconds, and released
  before shutdown draining. FM9 checks two actual DESCENT entities and records;
  FP5 checks a production-codec zero-record ELECTED source and matching receipt.
  Neither is misrepresented as a second full player launch.
- The saved-DESCENT fixture saves on S before election, never from W. FM7 removes
  its fault online after inspecting both restart refusals and adds a stable start.
  FM2a retains its original terminal UUID through recovery.
- FM12a uses the normal checked landed-reservation release. FM12b uses normal
  refuel/launch and replaces the old reservation with PREPARED; recovery correctly
  takes KEEP_SOURCE, retaining the same fully fueled Moon entity. It is not claimed
  as a completed return to Earth. The original first event/receipt survives both.
- Station fixtures create two actual platforms through normal creation, at
  4,000,000 FE each. Headless warps use the existing quote/commit pipeline.
  FW9 performs a second ordinary warp; FW11 deletes the winner via the normal
  mutator after dispatch and before acknowledgement.
- Paired fixtures overlay only stopped isolated world copies with production-codec
  roots. The near-bound fixture uses legal station/member/invite records, not
  ignored padding or an enlarged budget; its extra records do not claim platforms.
  The inspector's default 4,096-tag bound stays unchanged; only this legal large
  fixture requests a separately bounded larger read limit.
- FP6 faults exactly one unrelated transfer generation under the unchanged
  DELIVERED outbox, observes actual UNKNOWN/SOURCE_UNCONFIRMED rather than rollback,
  and retains its third COMMITTED record after normal bounded retry.
  Load faults occur after pre-start validation, keep the target raw bytes intact
  through three faulted starts, then recover without editing the file.
- The paired oracle preserves exact reservations and binds a Moon event's transfer,
  checksum and terminal UUID to its COMMITTED record. Only the ordinary root clock
  may advance monotonically; event orbital ticks and all other authority stay exact.
  FP4 waits for the normal clear-motion diagnostic before its one ordinary warp.
  No production guard is bypassed.
