## Design decisions and remaining scope

The recommendation is a bounded same-ledger account list, not enlarged receipts or another SavedData file.
New fluid operations/receipts reference exact account digests. Root-2 migration preserves old embedded encodings
and UNKNOWN history rather than inventing baselines. Native numeric checks supplement, not replace, existing
signature/identity and own-store proofs. Operator-approved signed discrepancies are not proof of zero loss;
missing contents remain unknown. Item count accounting is a separate possible requirement, not implemented by
fluid accounting. No accepted ADR was changed; these choices remain recommendations pending Root's decisions
and the different-engine read-only review required by R434.

There are no production, test, script, registration, ADR, risk-register, status or QUEUE changes. No tests were
added or modified. Implementation, real-codec measurements of the proposed format, migration/native cuts and
independent review remain undone. All production switches remain off. R-044's failed downgrade results and
X01-X03's open status are unchanged. The design estimates 9-13.5 focused engineer-days plus 1-2 days of complete
serial candidate verification/review; final performance/client work and X01-X03 are excluded.
