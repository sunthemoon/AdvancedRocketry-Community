The five failures had three fixture dependencies:

1. **Three logical-drill tests: an awake Space level and completed chunk setup.**
   A region ticket and synchronous `getChunkAt` do not finish asynchronous entity
   loading. More importantly, the mapped Forge runtime's `ServerLevel.tick` stops
   block-entity ticking after 300 ticks without players or forced chunks. An ordinary
   region ticket does not satisfy `ForgeChunkManager.hasForcedChunks`. An earlier
   station/player batch had hidden that requirement. The fixture now retains an owned
   forced chunk and waits for entity-ticking readiness and formation before starting
   each scenario. Its listener releases the region ticket and only its own added
   force on success or failure; normal close also releases them. Existing operation,
   energy, output, warp and break/repair checks remain unchanged.
2. **Physical laser: completion of its connected owner's own chunk loads.**
   The initial whole-Level chunk count could be sampled before asynchronous player
   loads finished, attributing those loads to the drill. The test now observes
   `getChunkNow` across the player's FULL view/simulation area before the measurement.
   GameTest's `PlayerList` settings are zero, but `ChunkMap` clamps view distance to
   at least two; the wait includes that clamp and the two-chunk FULL outer ring.
   It loads nothing itself. The whole-Level chunk-count inequality and exact ticket
   map comparisons remain unchanged; failure text now includes before/after counts.
3. **Elevator expiry: a foreign flight ticket still holding the arrival.**
   A diagnostic run of the existing prefix identified transfer
   `4b1f0817-7a5e-4999-8752-6d06e915f03c` from `tau_ceti_path`, still holding Space
   chunk `[0, 0]` with `arce_rocket_flight` level 31 after landing. Its 400-tick
   expiry outlasted the elevator's 100-tick countdown, permitting a real paid ride;
   the energy failure was not a projection debit. Before requesting the ride, the
   test now waits for all non-fixture tickets capable of retaining the arrival to
   expire. Read-only ticket inspection is bounded to the FULL ticket radius; it
   never removes another test's hold. The existing 130-tick unload interval is paced
   at normal 20 TPS using `GameTestTickPacer`, within the unchanged 1,200-tick timeout.
   Packet counts, unchanged energy, no teleport, deadlines and the audited
   `ARRIVAL_UNLOADED` result remain asserted. Temporary diagnostic logging is removed.

The new inspection/listener reflection follows existing GameTest patterns. The
562-line physical-laser class was checked for mixed responsibilities; these additions
are fixture readiness, not a new production responsibility. No ADR choice was needed.
