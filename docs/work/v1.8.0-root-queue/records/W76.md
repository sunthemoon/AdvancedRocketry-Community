## Why these failures are possible

**Moon.** The FORCED ticket already retains the destination chunk; unlike [W73](W73.md), this is not
a missing-retention problem. However, the 100 readiness waits were unpaced. As established by the
[2026-10-10 investigation](../../v1.8.0-gametest-pacing/HANDOFF-01.md), GameTest ticks do not sleep,
whereas chunk preparation progresses asynchronously. The hosted log's wait can therefore expire
before the ticket becomes entity-ticking. The existing pacer gives waiting ticks normal server-tick
time and pumps chunk tasks. The exact `++waited > 100` check and 200-tick test timeout remain intact.

**Spawn egg.** Synchronous block availability does not guarantee accessible entity sections.
Inspection of the pinned Forge 47.4.10 mapped classes showed that entity insertion can succeed into
a hidden section, while `EntitySectionStorage` skips that section during spatial queries.
The egg's return value alone does not establish query visibility. The fix waits before either
spawn, then retains both immediate exact-count queries, egg consumption, command result, absence
of natural-position checks, vacuum damage and cleanup in the same callback. It does not retry a
failed spawn or defer its observation. The original 40-tick timeout still bounds the whole test.

A separate native cold-column diagnostic confirmed the mechanism under CPU load:
`used=CONSUME empty=true join_events=1 query=0 entities_loaded=false added=[true]` immediately after
the egg, followed by `tick=2 query=1 same_entity=true` after entity readiness. This is a controlled
reproduction of the visibility gap, not a claim that the original hosted invocation was reproduced.
