**The stop check: nothing in the TASK's stop list is needed.**
- *Saved structures this slice writes*, all with existing fields only: (1) the receipt root `BAY_RECEIPTS`: the phase
  REGISTERED with `src_after`, `dst_after`, `registry_epoch` and `updated_tick`, the existing `preexisting` flag at D1,
  and whole-receipt removal at D7; (2) the satellite registry: the existing `launch` (data, with its initial mission) and
  `launchIdle` records; (3) the transfer journal: T's existing PREPARED record with its two snapshots and checksum
  replaced; (4) the source entity's existing rocket payload: the snapshot whose bay roots hold the existing `consumed_op`
  field instead of `package`; (5) at a restart, a source entity rebuilt with existing fields only (UUID, snapshot,
  flight data, owner, logical ID, position). No field, schema, file, tag, ticket type or network channel is added.
- *Bounds, budgets, quotas and timeouts*, all existing: 8 bays per rocket, 32 steps per tick, 256 claims and the
  4,096-entry rejection map (W27d1); 256 outstanding registry checkpoints and the registry writer's 10-tick and 200-tick
  cadence (ADR-074 item 4); W27A's three-failure REGISTRY_UNPROVED rule; ADR-065 4.1.1's runtime budget (16 MiB, 16,384
  visits, one reconstruction per tick, `RocketCargoWorkBudget`); the barrier's ordinary sub-quota and W98's 200-tick
  retry after a failed proof; at most 16 checked columns per end (`RocketDiscoveryColumns.refreshSet`, the existing
  `FLIGHT_TICKET`); `MAX_TRANSFER_ENTITY_MATCHES` (64). No wait has a time limit; each retries at its existing pacing.
- *`RocketTransferService` gains no line.* The TRANSIT wait is the section 8.1 claim, and READY_SOURCE(T') hands T' and
  S' to the existing `tickSource` and `spawnDestination`. `RocketTransferSavedData` (800 lines) is first reduced by an
  extraction and then gains one package-private hook, so it stays at or below 800.

**D2 under the permit.** After the exact INTENT acknowledgement, a queued step revalidates and registers inside
`SatelliteMissionSavedData.registerWithPermit(permit, ...)`, a scope like W27d1's `validateWithPermit`: the session's
permit for (op, X), still INTENT in RAM, is honoured by `launch` and `launchIdle` of X only, never by `decommission`,
and ends when the call returns or throws. The call is `SatelliteLaunchService.validate` then `register`, the shared
replay-first order. SUCCESS on the FRESH branch for DATA is checked from the launch result at once (F2): the returned
mission is `initialMissionId(X)` and its satellite, owner, definition and target equal the request. SUCCESS or an
IDEMPOTENT replay with an equal identity takes a registry watch of X at its sequence m; a refusal becomes REJECTED with
its code (B9), written as in W27d1. W27d1's interim REJECTED (DEPLOYMENT_UNAVAILABLE) for a success and its D0 refusal of
an existing X are removed (R49 Q4); D1 samples `preexisting` from the registry.

**D3 (ADR-074 item 11).** When every member of the claim has passed D2, one `requestCheckpoint(server, watches)` covers
all of its watches. The step waits for the ticket's answer, the first published completion with s >= m. COMMITTED that
proves every watch goes to D4, with the completion's carried epoch as each receipt's `registry_epoch`. FAILED, UNKNOWN or
a discarded ticket counts one failure and requests again after 200 ticks (the writer's own backoff also paces it); from
the third failure the claim reason is REGISTRY_UNPROVED. INTENT and the RAM registration are kept: nothing rejects after
a RAM registration.

**D4: group publication and the fence lift.** Once every member is proved, REJECTED and acknowledged, or refused at
runtime, the claim freezes one group for exact (L, T): the proved members only, in bay order, each consumed by its own
`op`. Two payload-only preparations, one over T's source snapshot and one over its destination snapshot, replace each
consumed bay root with `{owner, consumed_op}` through the runtime's ADR-065 4.1.1 budget (one reconstruction per tick).
They give S', T' and the four hashes. A new receipt-store group call queues every REGISTERED receipt of the group before
one write request, so they publish in one generation. Its exact acknowledgement lifts the decommission fences (the store
already releases an acknowledged REGISTERED). A definite failure restores INTENT and the step publishes again; UNKNOWN
waits for the writer's exact-candidate retry. A group with no proved member ends as W27d1's claims do.

**D5: payload-only consumption.** In one server-thread step, under scoped permits: S' on the live source through a new
one-use `RocketEntity` permit (snapshot only; the escrow-only checks still apply and refuse into REPAIR), and T' through
a new journal mutation in a new class, `rocket/flight/persistence/RocketTransferConsumption`. It is allowed only for
PREPARED T named by the group's REGISTERED receipts, keeps the transfer ID, phase, owner, source UUID, flight data,
fuel, reservations and snapshot IDs, changes only the two snapshots' payloads and the checksum (a new
`RocketTransferRecord` method), refuses a governed record, and is written by the existing off-thread journal writer.

**D6: the survivor-slot proof.** When the journal's acknowledged version for L is T' (`journal.actionable(L)` with T' in
RAM), the coordinator takes `journal.logicalLease(L, T, 0)` and asks W98's port (`RocketRetirementWorld`, the barrier's
ordinary sub-quota) for one capture of the source's entity slot. The validator is W98's `survivorSlot` against a frozen
altitude projection: L's exact source UUID, owner, S' (ID and hash), TRANSIT for T, T's fuel, the exact altitude
position, not a passenger. A failure repeats after 200 ticks, a busy budget at the next barrier tick.

**D7: the guarded removal.** A second group call removes every REGISTERED receipt of the group in one generation, and the
store passes the request's guard to the writer. The coordinator issues it only when the store has no other queued or
captured change, so the guarded generation holds this group alone. The guard reads only atomic state: the barrier
proof's `valid()`, the journal lease, and the group's invalidation flag, which a save, join, leave or movement of the
source sets (B16). GUARD_REFUSED or a definite failure repeats D6; UNKNOWN waits for the exact retry. The exact
acknowledgement ends the claim with READY_SOURCE(T', source), and the transfer spawns its destination from T'.

**Restart rows, one copy.** R0 is W27d1's ServerStartingEvent claim. INTENT with X absent stays REJECTED (INTERRUPTED);
INTENT with an equal X now promotes: the loaded registry's acknowledgement proves the watch, or D3 requests a checkpoint,
then D4. REGISTERED (and a promoted group) resumes: R1 renews flight tickets on T's checked columns of both ends (at most
16 each) under the claim, one group step per tick, and waits until the source end's columns are entity-loaded and
ticking. R2 checks T (PREPARED, owner, L, snapshot IDs, before or after hashes from the receipts). The exact source UUID
present and neutral is kept (a lagging COUNTDOWN or ASCENT image gets TRANSIT from T at the altitude position, with no
launch effect); absent, a bay-resume constructor rebuilds it with T's source UUID, the journal's source snapshot,
T's TRANSIT flight data restamped to the recovery tick, zero velocity, at altitude. Never `rebuildSource`. R3 completes
a mixed `(S',T)` or `(S,T')` by publishing only the missing half after recomputing it and matching the recorded after
hashes (else REPAIR, CONSUMPTION_MISMATCH), then D6 and D7. Any other recognized neutral copy at either end holds the
claim at runtime as DUPLICATE_PENDING with no record change (W27d3 resolves it). A protected or unexpected copy, a
non-neutral source, an escrow conflict, or a T outside the receipts' hashes holds as runtime REPAIR with no write.

**Placement.** Domain (`rocket/bay/deployment`, pure Java): the frozen group and its stages, D3's failure rule and the
resume decision table. Adapters (`rocket/server`): registration (D2, D3), publication (D4), consumption (D5 with the
entity permit), the source proof (D6 and D7) and the resume (R1 to R3), each below 500 lines; `BayTransferRecovery`
delegates to them. Model: a multi-replacement constructor of `RocketSnapshotPreparation`. Escrow: the two group calls
in `BayReceiptStore` and the session handle. Registry: `registerWithPermit`.

**Choices (the most conservative reading where the design is open).** The group retires in one generation, so no partial
retirement exists in this slice. At a restart, bays without a receipt are not admitted by a restart D0; they keep their
packages unconsumed for the next flight (no durable state, so nothing is stranded). Online passengers are left in place
until READY_SOURCE, which section 8.1 allows; the ordinary passenger transfer then moves them. Runtime REPAIR holds
write nothing; the next start classifies again.

**Not in this slice.** Copies and the absence census (W27d3), released flight (W27d4), admission (W27e), ADRs, the risk
register (Root registers entry D first), status files and the ledger.

### Choices where the design was open

- (choice) **Two halves in separate commits.** The half-1 commit `3874646a3` carries a coordinator whose D5 to D7 stages
  are an idle hold; its file was derived from the final one by `scratch/half1.py` (evidence). As W127 says, the halves
  cannot merge apart.
- (choice) **D3 per claim.** One `requestCheckpoint` covers every REGISTERING member of a claim once no member waits for its
  INTENT write. A discarded ticket (writer refusal, 256 outstanding, shutdown) counts as a failure like FAILED or UNKNOWN.
  After a failure the claim asks again no sooner than 200 ticks later; the claim reason is REGISTRY_UNPROVED from the third
  failure and clears at the proof. Nothing rejects after a RAM registration.
- (choice) **F2 and identity mismatches hold, not write.** A fresh launch result that fails F2, or a published commit that
  covers the watch's sequence but not its identity, holds the claim as runtime REPAIR (REGISTRY_SIGNATURE_CONFLICT); the
  next start's INTENT row then decides durably (W27d1's REPAIR write).
- (choice) **D4 waits for every REJECTED acknowledgement** before it freezes, so a group is frozen only with known
  outcomes. D2's refusals are written as W27d1 writes them (one REJECTED per member); D4 publishes the REGISTERED receipts
  of the group in one generation.
- (choice) **S' and T' in session** are computed once at D4's freeze and kept in RAM for D5; after a restart, D5 takes T'
  from the journal when it already holds it, and otherwise recomputes it and requires the recorded after hashes.
- (choice) **The D7 guard** is the barrier proof's `valid()`, the journal lease taken when T' was acknowledged, and the
  group's invalidation counter (bumped by `endpointChanged`, the section 8.1 join and leave hook). Movement is checked in
  RAM just before the removal (the survivor's projection must still equal the proved image). The removal is issued only to
  a quiet store, so the guarded generation never carries another change; under constant store traffic it waits.
- (choice) **W98's port** (`RocketRetirementWorld`) is used for its slot and barrier calls only, built without a transfer
  dispatch; the altitude projection is the bay's own (`BaySourceProof.projection`), and W98's `survivorSlot` validates it.
  A failed proof repeats after W98's 200 ticks; a superseding save repeats at once.
- (choice) **R1** renews the existing `FLIGHT_TICKET` on each end's checked column set (`refreshMargins`, at most 16 per
  end) for a healthy restart group from RESUME to RETIRE; an end that is not admissible, or a missing Level, waits with
  END_UNAVAILABLE and adds no ticket. One R1/R2 group step runs per tick.
- (choice) **R2 copies.** Any other loaded image of L at either recorded end (snapshot ID and a before or after hash of
  that end), neutral, holds the claim as DUPLICATE_PENDING with no record change; a protected one, more than 64 matches,
  or an unexpected exact source holds as runtime REPAIR. D6 runs the same check before every capture. The bay-resume
  constructor uses the journal's current source snapshot (S or S') and T's TRANSIT flight data restamped to the recovery
  tick.
- (choice) **Restart D0 and passengers.** Bays without a receipt are not admitted at a restart (design note), and online
  passengers are not remounted by a permit: they stay in place until READY_SOURCE, which section 8.1 allows, and the
  ordinary passenger transfer moves them at arrival. Offline seats stay in T.
- (choice) **Solar in A1.** No GameTest can grant the shipped solar satellite's 120 lifetime research, so the flight test
  registers the solar X beforehand and the bay replays it (IDEMPOTENT, `preexisting=1`); the data X registers fresh with
  its initial mission. Fresh non-data registration is covered by the native B4 (survey) case.
- (choice) **Native B9 and B18_REJECTED.** W27d2 registers the fixture's data bay, so its D2 no longer writes REJECTED;
  the gated property `bayRefuseRegistration` makes D2 refuse with SERVER_ERROR on a release-test server, which keeps those
  cases. It registers nothing and is ignored elsewhere.

  seam that pauses before the guard.
- **B5 and B7** (copies, offline login, cold chunks) belong to W27d3. B10's release half belongs to W27d4.
