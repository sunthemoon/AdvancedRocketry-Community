## Design, files and tests

- `src/test/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/recipegraph/OperationalTechGraph.java`
  adds code-cited operational prerequisites to the existing packaged graph. It
  includes typed ground/satellite asteroid outcomes, paid analysis, independent
  ground and satellite research witnesses, all satellite kinds and lifetime
  thresholds, missions, tree criteria, endgame systems, solar and beacons.
- `src/test/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/recipegraph/OperationalGraphWitness.java`
  emits every node, producer and earliest-round witness, all unavailable-node
  blocking paths, every structural cycle's closed path and the unmodelled guards.
  It distinguishes harmless reachable cycles from the unavailable residual.
- `src/test/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/recipegraph/OperationalTechGraphTest.java`
  adds 8 tests: deterministic report/archive checks and producer-order invariance;
  required reachability; every producer's AND slots and source citation;
  controller-acquisition isolation; independent research routes; explicit scope;
  seeded/unseeded/alternative cycles; and blocked AND/OR diagnostics. These are
  JSON/source tests and do not initialize Minecraft registries or start a world.
- [The archived report](../../../releases/v1.8.0/RECIPE-GRAPH-REPORT.md) embeds the
  entire raw witness, its reproduction command and source commit.

The result is 820 nodes, 811 producers, 219 inherited survival roots, 810 reachable
nodes and 196 reachable required operational nodes. The 10 unavailable nodes are
exactly the previously authorized compatibility dusts/tags. There are 224
structurally cyclic nodes, all reachable; the unavailable residual has zero
cyclic nodes. No required unreachable node or hard lock was found in the declared
model, so there was no product-code fix to make.

Research thresholds come from the packaged definitions, with bounded finite
repetition of paid Earth missions/jobs. Component statistics are checked against
the actual code limits. Classic advancement display parents are not unlock
prerequisites. Seven groups of dynamic or otherwise unmodelled preconditions
remain explicit rather than being promoted to roots. This is conditional
dependency reachability, not all-device runtime operability or player acceptance.
