## C6 authority and conservative choices

The ordinary route uses `advancedrocketrycommunity:station_deployment_kit`
from a real player on a catalog surface Level, not Space, owning zero
stations. The server resolves that body's orbit and consumes one kit outside
Creative. Admin create does not establish those ordinary-route preconditions;
the driver independently checks distinct eligible owners. To test ordinary
creation, use a fresh FI06 copy with the eventual O5 UUID parameters, not an
account already owning a K08 operator-created station.

The Overworld-owned `data/advancedrocketrycommunity_stations.dat` holds IDs,
owners, orbit, region, pad, membership/invitations and `warp_energy`. C6 reads
back root schema 5 and station record schema 3. A warp core is a stateless
Forge Energy receiver: its block entity stores schema 1 and its probe returns
zero, not the station balance. Runtime pending FE folds every 200 ticks or at
normal stop. The charged variants use an adjacent ordinary solar generator
for 12 seconds, then remove the generator and temporary tickets. Their
positive saved balances survive restart but are **not warp-ready**. Effective
readbacks are enabled=true, in-system 2,000,000 FE, interstellar 8,000,000 FE.
The campaign must supply enough normal FE and wait for the fold.

Invite/accept/decline/remove require a player source. Invite uses an online
player **name**, not a bare UUID; offline removal uses literal
`remove <station> uuid <member-UUID>`. Warp request/confirm require the
player's own non-silent local source, loaded Space region, aimed core within
five blocks and server-side authority/target/energy checks. `/execute` is not
a replacement for local consent. Quotes and confirmed 200-tick countdowns
are lifecycle-owned memory, not SavedData: restarting cannot preserve a
pending-warp fixture. No suitable C6 registered setup hook was found.

The manifests bind actual IDs to owner invitation, member decline/accept,
removal, member/non-member warp refusal, owner quote/confirm/cancel and
permission-2 deletion with literal `confirm`. Console creation, inspect,
warp diagnostics and a separate disposable station's confirmed admin delete
were executed. The witness never replaces a delivered or inherited station.
The two-account client addendum supersedes only the original prospective
client scripts: B may be A's member, then non-member after removal; no four
simultaneous accounts are required. It includes core approach/look positions,
Earth/Moon and Tau Ceti E targets, explicit reinvitation after decline, online
name binding, and GUI-scale 2/3 cancel steps. These are instructions, not
observed player results. Rebuild under a fresh output directory once O5
accounts are known; never rewrite sealed owners or saved membership.

Both overlay sources contain unchanged K06 ZIPs, with baseline active and
both overlays disabled. Baseline adoption reports 12 valid bodies. No
countdown exists, so no before-due overlay activation was executed. The
unchanged expectations remain `WARP_TARGET_UNAVAILABLE`, and conditionally
`WARP_QUOTE_CHANGED` if the effective class cost differs and sufficient folded
FE avoids a masking guard. Neither is claimed as an observed result.
