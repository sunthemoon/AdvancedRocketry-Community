# W120: cargo D-A, the rocket item loader and unloader devices, with D-D's resources

## Implementation plan (committed before code)

Worktree `D:/GitHub/arce-v180-w120`, branch `codex/v1.8.0-w120`, base `53e9fa1a`. Authority is
[TASK-W120](../TASK-W120-CARGO-D-A-ITEM-DEVICES.md), the [cargo design](W11CD-DESIGN.md) sections 5.1, 5.3, 5.4 and
5.5, section 7's protection scope and the D-A row, with [W11B3](W11B3-DESIGN.md) sections 3 and 4 where 5.1 applies
them. The leaf mirrors C-A ([W109](W109.md)) and adopts D-P's device root form ([W118](W118.md)). Admission stays
unwired: nothing starts an escrow operation. No saved format of a published world, ADR, risk register, status file,
ledger, build script or workflow changes.

**Names.** Blocks, items and block entities `rocket_item_loader` (LOAD) and `rocket_item_unloader` (UNLOAD), the
names that `EscrowAdapterIds.itemCargoDevice` already expects; the menu `rocket_item_cargo`.

**Classes** (package `rocket/cargo/device`).
- `RocketItemCargoRoot`: the root `arce_rocket_item_cargo`, schema 1, at most 32 KiB, depth 20 and 2,048 nodes, with
  the closed field set `schema_version`, `owner`, `device`, `role`, `binding`, `buffer` and `resource_escrow`. The
  `buffer` field is `EscrowItemBuffer`'s encoded form, four slots, an empty slot written as `{}`, each stack within
  section 5.3's bounds. An unknown, future, invalid, mismatched or unregistered-item root is kept raw and protects
  the device, as C-A's does.
- `RocketItemCargoBlock`, `RocketItemCargoBlockEntity` and `RocketItemCargoItem`: the role fixed by the block; the
  placer owns a placed device; the item carries nothing and refuses a `BlockEntityTag`; the buffer spills as
  ordinary item drops once, and a protected device spills and drops nothing.
- `RocketItemCargoPort`: `ITEM_HANDLER` on every side; the loader only inserts and the unloader only extracts.
- `RocketItemCargoMenu` with its server container and slots: four vanilla slots and the player inventory, owner
  only within 8 blocks, both ways; status, binding, role and cursor (x, y, z and slot) as scalar data. The client
  screen `client/RocketItemCargoScreen` is drawn in code.
- `RocketItemCargoLinker` (the linker roles) and `RocketItemCargoProtection` (break, explosion and chunk-save
  hooks), mirroring C-A's.
- Small shared changes: `RocketCargoBindingChecks` gains owner-and-binding overloads that the fluid methods call;
  `RocketFluidCargoIndex` holds either device kind, so one placement UUID names one loaded device;
  `RocketCargoDeviceHold` takes the endpoint unit (`CONTAINER` for items, `TANK` unchanged for fluids).

**Resources (D-D's part).** The pre-authoring declaration
[v1.8.0-c17a-rocket-item-cargo-preauthoring.md](../../../provenance/v1.8.0-c17a-rocket-item-cargo-preauthoring.md)
comes first. Then new providers `V180RocketItemCargoArt` and `V180RocketItemCargoData`, so C-A's provider, its
pinned counts and its provenance stay unchanged.

**Tests.** A0: `RocketItemCargoRootTest` (every bound just inside, at and just outside, and the closed field set) and
`V180RocketItemCargoDataTest`. A1: `RocketItemCargoGameTests`, `RocketItemCargoAuthorityGameTests` and
`RocketItemCargoProtectionGameTests`, repeating C-A's checks for items, the spill-once drop and an oversized-NBT
stack left unchanged, with exact WARN expectations in the log manifest.

**Not in this leaf.** Admission and escrow operations, W118-R2 and R7 (D-E), D-D's native S1 checks, S2 cuts, ADRs,
the risk register, status files, the ledger, build scripts and workflows. No Mixin and no network packet.
