## What was done

ADR-066 section 3.2's torch rules, as the design note describes.

- **Blocks.** `unlit_torch` and `unlit_wall_torch` keep the standing shape or the wall
  `facing`. They emit light 0, show no flame, and have no item or recipe. They share the
  block entity type `unlit_torch`. Its schema-1 root `arce_unlit_torch` holds exactly the pair,
  block and item IDs, and the block entity overrides `onlyOpCanSetNbt()`.
  - A resolvable torch drops its saved source item once, through the loot table's dynamic
    entry under `survives_explosion`.
  - An undecodable root is kept as raw data and written back unchanged. A root whose pair,
    blocks or item no longer resolve is kept too. Both refuse player breaking, explosions,
    relighting and support loss, and the blocks' pushing reaction is always `BLOCK`.
  - An outgoing root above 2 KiB, depth 8 or 64 nodes refuses the chunk save through
    `GuardedChunkSaves`.
- **Tags and catalog.** `combustion_torches` holds the vanilla standing and wall torches, and
  `combustion_fire_starters` holds flint and steel, fire charge, blaze rod and blaze powder.
  `torch_pairs` reloads with bounded reads, registry shape checks and whole-set rejection. The
  vanilla pair `minecraft:torch` is built in. Thermite torches are never governed.
- **Conversion.** Placements and neighbour notifications queue tagged torches whose cells read
  noncombustible or pending. After the atmosphere tick, volumes that lost supply are swept.
  Each Level then spends 256 checks per tick, of at most 8,192 queued positions. An
  unsupported tagged torch is refused regardless of air. A supported tagged torch is refused
  in a noncombustible or pending cell while the queue is full. In-flight and deferred positions
  remain counted during callbacks, so neighbour notifications cannot exceed the capacity.
  The overload WARN is logged once per episode, and `CombustionTorches.metrics` exposes
  the counts.
- **Fire starters.** A tagged item is suppressed beside a noncombustible or pending cell. On an
  unlit torch the server relights it only in combustion air, with a resolvable root,
  `mayUseItemAt`, a surviving state and an uncancelled preflight event.
- **Configuration.** `OXYGEN.dropExtinguishedTorches` (COMMON, default false) removes the torch
  and drops one pair item instead of converting it.

### Continuation changes and verification

for breathable or unmanaged air before checking catalog support. That allowed unsupported
tagged blocks to be placed. The queue removed in-flight and deferred positions from its set
while invoking callbacks. A callback could fill those apparent free slots, and appending
deferred entries could then exceed 8,192. Both defects are fixed in `470ec0a8`.

full queue. It verifies capacity, deduplication and FIFO progress on the following tick.

## Choices where the ADR was open

- **Combustion air.** The atmosphere model has no oxygen fraction, so a cell is combustible
  only when the instrument reading (`AtmosphereAnalyzerRuntime.readAt`) is `BREATHABLE`.
  `UNAVAILABLE` (an unloaded cell, or a Level with no mapped body such as the vanilla Nether
  or End) is unmanaged: supported torches are not converted and ordinary fire-starter use is
  not suppressed there. Catalog admission still rejects unsupported tagged blocks in every
  dimension. Otherwise every unmapped dimension would put out torches and block nether portals.
- **Cells.** A torch's eye cell is its own position. A fire starter uses the loaded cell next
  to the clicked face; relighting uses the unlit torch's own cell.
- **Settled atmosphere.** A block change dirties the supplied room around it, and its cells read
  vacuum until the rescan reaches them. A noncombustible reading therefore extinguishes only
  when the Level has no dirty backlog and no scan task other than those waiting on unloaded
  chunks. Otherwise the entry goes back to the tail. A deferral has no expiry; the queue cap
  bounds it.
- **Volume events.** "A subscribed atmosphere invalidation" is implemented as a diff of the
  Level's supplied volumes after each atmosphere tick. A volume that lost supply is swept over
  its at most 4,096 cells. At most 128 sweeps wait per Level, and an extra one is dropped and
  counted.
- **Budget.** The 256 checks per tick alternate one-for-one between queued positions and sweep
  cells. A sweep reads only while the queue has room.
- **Catalog.** "At most 64 pairs" includes the built-in pair, so data packs add at most 63.
  - A pair's ID is its file ID.
  - Registry checks require real `TorchBlock` and `WallTorchBlock` subclasses, a standing block
    with no properties, a wall block with only `facing`, no block entity, and one
    `StandingAndWallBlockItem` that both blocks name.
  - Any defect rejects the whole set. Unlike the black-hole data, a failed first load keeps the
    built-in pair and logs one ERROR instead of failing world loading.
- **Unsupported tagged torches** are always refused on placement, as ADR-066 requires.
  Already-existing unsupported tagged blocks are not converted because their data and drop
  identities cannot be inferred safely.
- **Torch with no root** (from `/setblock`): it breaks with no drop and cannot be relit. It has
  no owned tree to protect, so it is not quarantined.
- **Environmental-change adapter.** Every change posts one cancellable preflight
  `BlockEvent.EntityPlaceEvent` that names the new state and implements
  `DeferredRoomBoundary`, following the force-field preflight. A relight names the player, so
  claims and station protection apply to that player.
  - An environmental change names no entity: no FakePlayer, no operator bypass.
  - A FakePlayer subject would make the station `BUILD` check refuse every extinguish inside
    station regions.
  - A cancelled change keeps the block, its data and items.
- **Relight cost.** Damageable fire starters, including flint and steel, lose one durability,
  and a fire charge is consumed. Nondamageable other tagged items, including blaze rods and
  blaze powder, and creative players spend nothing.
- **Suppression** cancels the whole right-click, as the legacy handler did. While a player holds
  a fire starter in vacuum, block use is refused too (for example a lever, or TNT ignition).
- **Unlit blocks** have no item. Their pushing reaction is always `BLOCK`, because a piston has
  no block-entity-aware hook to spare only quarantined torches.
- **Messages.** Action-bar messages explain each refusal: `no_combustion`, `unsupported`, `busy`,
  `quarantined` and `unknown`, in English and Chinese.

## Known risks

- **Three GameTest failures outside W33.** They occur in both inherited runs and reproduce
  in the continuation on `470ec0a8`. The placement-dependent explanation below is the
  previous session's hypothesis, not an independently established root cause.
  - `stalebindingtoanunloadedownercellstaysprotected` and
    `emptyassociationbehindanunloadedfacadechunkiskept` (W25, batch
    `scrubber_stale_binding_frontier`) fail with "No loaded frontier available". Their batch is
    unchanged and runs at the same index (123), but GameTest lays tests out in one grid across
    all batches. With the new W33 tests, 276 tests are placed before it instead of 271. In
    W11b2's run at 20:50 on a branch that contains `b7db9c79`, 271 tests were placed before it
    and both passed. Their search for a loaded chunk next to an absent, ticketless chunk depends
    on where they land. W33 creates no overworld chunk tickets. I did not change those tests;
    they need their own fix.
  - `jointterrainslotproofretainstheotherendpointlease` (`escrow_core`) is W29's open
    intermittent failure. It failed in W11b2's run too.
- **Per-update cost.** Every server `NeighborNotifyEvent` now reads up to seven loaded block
  states and checks a tag. An instrument reading is made only for cells that hold a governed
  torch.
- **Busy Levels defer.** A Level whose atmosphere never settles keeps deferring extinguishing.
  With enough queued torches, the 8,192 cap is reached and new vacuum placements are refused,
  which is the specified fail-closed behaviour. A block change inside a supplied room starts a
  bounded sweep of that room, up to 4,096 reads at 128 or 256 per tick.
- **Unmanaged cells.** Torches stay lit, and fire starters work, wherever the instrument reading is
  unavailable. This covers dimensions with no mapped body and any Space position without a
  station body context.
- **Suppression scope.** In vacuum, a held fire starter also blocks using the clicked block.
- **Quarantine.** R-034: removing a pack that supplies torch pairs quarantines every unlit torch
  of those pairs until the pack returns. A fluid flowing into a quarantined torch is not
  refused. The oversized-root save refusal carries R-027's whole-chunk scope, Level saturation
  and per-retry ERROR logging.
- **Evidence limits.** There was no dedicated-server restart, real-client V1 or V2 check,
  1.12.2 art screen or independent review. Logs stay in `build/` and are not committed.
