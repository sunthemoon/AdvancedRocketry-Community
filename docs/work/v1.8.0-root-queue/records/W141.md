## 关键设计决定

- The stale-copy scenario checks after removing its own temporary Moon ticket, before the chunk-unload and entity-index
  checks can short-circuit. Its intentional setup ticket is therefore not reported as an earlier test's leak.
- The discard and margin scenarios check before their existing cold-pad waits. A warm pad without a permanent hold
  still waits exactly as before. No timeout, tick limit, existing assertion, readiness predicate, or helper implementation changed.
- `EscrowGovernedWarmupGameTests` already checks both initial predicted-pad waits. Its later re-cooling step follows
  its own warm-up expiry, rather than an inherited precondition. `EscrowFillLaunchGameTests` deliberately holds the
  destination and waits for it to become warm, not cold, so adding this precondition there would be wrong.
- The refusal class's named-pad scenario prepares its own new station with FULL-only tickets and waits for loaded,
  non-entity-ticking columns. It is not the shared predicted Moon-pad loop and was left unchanged.
