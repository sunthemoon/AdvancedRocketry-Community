## Choices where the ADR was open

- **Who earns formed_multiblock.** §6.2 names the event ("first actual formed classic
  multiblock") but not the recipient, and formation is asynchronous with no acting
  player. I chose every connected, living, non-spectator real player in the same Level
  within 16 blocks of the controller when a **new binding generation** reaches FORMED.
  Resuming the same generation (a revalidation or a chunk reload) is not a formation.
  "Classic multiblock" means the five machines on the shared lifecycle coordinator:
  rolling machine, precision assembler, lathe, arc furnace and cutting machine. The
  endgame multiblocks do not count.
- **Who earns moon_landing.** Players riding the landed rocket who hold a seat in its
  manifest, at the tick the committed descent publishes LANDED in the Moon Level.
  Offline passengers do not earn it.
- **Who earns station_warp.** Players in the Space Level inside the station region who
  hold live VISIT access (owner, member or operator), right after the checked warp
  commits. A request, confirmation, countdown or failed commit earns nothing.
- **seat_on_tnt.** A Forge `RightClickBlock` listener at the lowest priority, server side,
  for a real player holding a rocket seat on vanilla TNT, after vanilla's packet checks
  (reach, teleport acknowledgement, spawn protection). It never cancels the event or
  consumes anything; vanilla still places the seat on the TNT.
- **moon_surface_visit** uses vanilla `changed_dimension` into the Moon Level, because a
  vanilla trigger exists for the arrival (TASK: vanilla where possible).
- **Two catalogs.** The six inventory tutorials stay unchanged in
  `ClassicInventoryAdvancements` (their tests still assert exactly six). The eleven new
  entries live in `ClassicMilestoneAdvancements` with their own provider; the tree test
  checks all seventeen together.
- **Reachability scope.** §6.3 asks for a route report for every registered survival item,
  research edges, JEI presentation and a fresh-world playthrough. W12 applies the §6.3
  rule to the seventeen advancements only (TASK item 4): a test-side graph over the
  merged `build/resources/main` feeds the existing C16d forward evaluator. Roots are the
  Overworld, vanilla items, water and lava, and vanilla tags; other mods are ignored.
  Assumptions written in the graph: FE comes from a combustion or solar generator; a
  Moon or Space flight needs an assembler, an engine, a propulsion tank, a seat, a
  guidance computer, a fuel loader and fuel cells; machine recipes need their formed
  machine (every cell of the packaged pattern) and FE; the press needs obsidian and a
  redstone source. A recipe type without a producer model is skipped and listed in the
  report (skipping can only hide a route, never invent one); an unknown ingredient form
  or pattern matcher inside a modelled type fails the test.

## Blocked: the world-first milestones (D3)

ADR-066 §6.2 selects source outboxes for the D3 world-first receipts: a
`classic_first_warp` outbox committed in the same checked station-registry write as the
warp, and a `classic_first_moon_landing` outbox in the rocket transfer journal with a
checked landing-completion authority, delivered to a schema-1
`advancedrocketrycommunity_classic_progression` receipt store with acknowledgement and
login grants. ADR-066 says the outboxes "join C17's station registry 4 -> 5 migration
once" and "extend C17's journal 2 -> 3 migration once". ADR-065 section 5.2.1 (lines 594 to 596) adds: "Owner-selected C18 D3
first-event source outboxes must join this same root-5/journal-3 field/migration freeze;
the product selection neither accepts its shared technical contract nor bumps the roots
independently."

In this tree the station registry is still schema 4 (W07 is designing schema 5 now) and
the journal is still schema 2 (journal 3 is not scheduled). So W12 cannot add the
outboxes without the independent root bump both ADRs forbid, and a receipt store
without its source would be the "second authority" §6.2 rules out. I therefore shipped
`one_small_step` and `first_station_warp` with `minecraft:impossible` criteria, and the
GameTests assert that a real Moon landing and a real warp do not award them. Nothing
about them is persisted. The TASK's GameTest "granted to the first player only and
survives a save and load" is **not done** for this reason.

Proposal for Root: fold the `classic_first_warp` outbox field into W07's schema-5 freeze
(W07F is open), schedule the journal-3 migration with the landing-completion authority,
then a follow-up slice adds the progression receipt store, the delivery and
acknowledgement, login grants, the two `classic_event` events and the S2 restart and
cut fixtures. That slice needs the one-page design note and the independent read-only
review of AGENTS.md section 13.

## Known risks

- The 16-block formation radius rewards bystanders as well as the builder.
- A crash after the landing tick but before the player's advancement file is saved can
  lose a per-player award (vanilla behaviour for every advancement). Restart recovery
  repeats the descent and fires the event again only if the rocket's chunk was saved in
  DESCENT.
- `PackagedRecipeGraph` is 519 lines (one responsibility: reading packaged data into the
  graph). It reads `build/resources/main`, so it depends on `processResources` running
  before the unit tests, as Gradle does.
- The seat GameTest drives `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.handleUseItemOn` and acknowledges
  the join teleport from the embedded channel's outbound packets; a vanilla change to
  that handshake would break the test, not production.
