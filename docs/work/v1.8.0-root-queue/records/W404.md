## Fix and tests

`releasedDeviceFluidMatches(Scenario, LoaderTank)` explicitly requires `fluid() == null` when
`isEmpty()` is true. A non-empty tank must name `minecraft:water`, or exactly `minecraft:lava`
for SUBSTITUTE. The latter is stricter than merely accepting any non-water fluid. The surrounding
device-unlocked, exact total `5000 + deviceDelta()`, amount, epoch, and marker checks remain intact.
READBACK never reaches this released check: it returns through its held-settlement branch.

The new bootstrap-isolated parameterized test has 54 invocations: all 11 scenarios, both directions,
and empty/non-empty tanks (44), plus the five prepared-release cases in both directions (10).
It checks native encode/decode, null identity and an empty envelope for zero, exact positive identity,
and rejection of the other fixture fluid and flowing water. The prepared cases retain their starting
amounts and total 5,000 mB. No assertion, test, timeout, or budget is removed or relaxed. No ADR choice
is needed; this only corrects a gated test expectation. The probe remains a single-purpose fixture
adapter at 515 lines; no new product responsibility or framework was added.
