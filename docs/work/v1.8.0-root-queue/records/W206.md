Before the measured first request, arithmetic-only candidate columns are recorded. No
selector or heightmap is called to predict them. An exhaustive read-only ticket snapshot
and loaded-chunk snapshot qualify the destination: no other ticket may supply readiness.

the observer. The generated control is prepared in its own process and then stopped;
the measured restart must again qualify cold/no-support status. The new-terrain control
must have no destination terrain region records before its first request. No support,
START, forced or player ticket is added to a measured end, including after F8.

The independent review, `D:/GitHub/ARCE-Task-Evidence/root-queue/W206-review/REVIEW.md` (APPROVE-WITH-CHANGES,

- **W206-R1 (Medium).** The restart row of the plan is only partly delivered. The recovered JVM shows no live warm-up,
  warm-up ticket or transfer entry for 100 ticks, but it makes no fresh request, so the plan's separately validated
  fresh request after the restart is still open. It is not "beyond the plan". It moves to the remedy task that follows
  W206, which re-measures on the same harness. Nothing here shows a product restart defect.

  `D:/GitHub/ARCE-Task-Evidence/root-queue/W206/SUPPLEMENTAL-CONSUMER-TIMING-SHA256SUMS.txt` (SHA-256
  `9e92e705…6472f36`) with the 12 hashes; all 12 match the review's independent `audit-results.json`. The original
  aggregate and summaries are unchanged.
- **W206-R3 (Low).** The measured END interval stops before the tick row is built, serialized and written, and
  `finish()` and the final exports come after the sample. So the snapshot, checkpoint-drain, assertion and column-union

The review's attribution, for the remedy task: synchronous pad selection (`RocketLandingPadSelector` loading chunks
and choosing by heightmap before the asynchronous warm-up) takes 480 to 900 ms of each new-terrain request and 511 ms of
