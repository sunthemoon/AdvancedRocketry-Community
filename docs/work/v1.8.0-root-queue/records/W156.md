### History and what the floor protects

- `0ec91523e24f7956c53241aa5ad04ce5c2d619bc` introduced
  [SafeCelestialTravel](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/celestial/service/SafeCelestialTravel.java)
  under [ADR-001](../../../decisions/ADR-001-FIXED-DIMENSIONS.md).
  `FIXED_FEET_POSITION` is `(8, 80, 8)`: developer travel builds a 5-by-5 floor at
  Y=79, clears three blocks above it, then places the player at Y=80. The original
  Moon generator had one bedrock layer and three end-stone layers, not ground at Y=79.
- `96e92d995b9b8007f0076a4a8e951069b219d080` introduced the rocket selector's
  `max(surface, FIXED_FEET_POSITION.getY())` under the Earth-Moon transfer design,
  [ADR-006](../../../decisions/ADR-006-ROCKET-CROSS-DIMENSION-TRANSFER.md), introduced
  in `a25fac92a0a929f4eb4d53d40b0ace019a4b56c7`. ADR-006 specifies server-owned,
  bounded pads but does not itself mandate Y=80. The selector does not call the
  developer platform builder.
- `d120d4152689022473512329a44cd29a29317b2f` added the accepted
  [ADR-033](../../../decisions/ADR-033-PLANETARY-WORLDS-AND-SURFACE-ADMISSION.md).
  Its physical-admission section, lines 75-81, expressly retains Moon minimum
  landing Y=80, exempts it from the new ground-support requirement, and preserves
  captured recovery identities.
- [ADR-063 section 5](../../../decisions/ADR-063-MATERIALS-ORES-AND-PLANETARY-SURFACES.md),
  lines 255-263 and 314-325, explicitly preserves that behavior over the new terrain,
  acknowledges the unsupported rocket and fall, and requires a separate landing-rule
  ADR to move Moon arrivals onto the heightmap while keeping existing pads and
  recovery identities working. Section 9 also requires Y=80-or-higher arrival tests.

Thus the documented rationale is fixed-pad/developer-platform and existing-arrival
compatibility, not proof of ground support. The developer command actually creates
support; rocket selection does not. Nor is Y=80 a safe void guard: the selector
starts at `minBuildHeight + 1`, raises Moon arrivals to 80, and exempts Moon from
`supported()`. Removing just the Moon clamp would still accept an all-empty footprint
above the build minimum. Empty-column handling needs an explicit rule in the revised
landing policy; raising the rocket into unsupported air is not a solution.
