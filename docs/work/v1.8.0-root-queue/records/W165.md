## ADR choices where the TASK left options open

1. **Footprint without ground:** refuse, not search. An unsupported candidate falls through to the next of the eight
   fixed candidates; with none left, the existing `LANDING_PAD_UNAVAILABLE` and its audit-only detail apply, and the
   journal (including any existing T) and fuel stay unchanged. Searching inside a candidate would create coordinates
   outside ADR-006's fixed list and need more inspection.
2. **Recheck of recorded destinations:** a threshold at the literal y 80. Transfer records hold no landing-rule marker,
   and the TASK expects no saved-data change. Every destination recorded under the old rule has its lowest block at
   y 80 or above, so rechecks there keep the legacy Moon check (no support), and rechecks below y 80 get the full
   ADR-033 check. Selection always checks support. ADR-075 alternatives D, E and F explain the rejected options.
3. **Inspection:** the rule adds no constant and no chunk load. It does run ADR-033's existing support phase on the
   Moon: at most 2,048 reads per candidate at selection (at most 8 × 2,048 per selection), and at most 2,048 reads per
   evaluation of a recheck below y 80, no more than that evaluation's existing air inspection. The governed path can
   evaluate the recheck several times in one visit, for at most 8 visits per tick; the number and placement of the
   evaluations do not change (W165-R4). All reads lie in chunks the candidate already loaded. Mars and Venus have had
   this envelope since ADR-033. I did not stop for this, because the TASK's stop condition concerns more inspection
   than the existing limits allow. The review confirmed this reading (W165-R4).
4. **Test transitions:** the two `MoonArrivalGameTests` cases are renamed, because their names state the y 80 rule.
   Their new assertions are stronger (exact height from the footprint heightmap, support, no fluid).
5. **Passenger dismount:** unchanged, as the TASK requires. ADR-075 records the fixed, non-collision-aware dismount
   offset, and its suffocation and drop consequences, as a residual shared with Mars and Venus for the owner to
   accept.
