The task's retained failure excerpt from hosted run 44861941486 shows source/probe/transmitting
columns already at 15/13/13 while the iron control still reads 6 beneath an opaque block whose
own light is 0. The old readiness predicate did not wait for the control's pending light removal.
A source/probe becoming bright is not a completion signal for asynchronous light decreases.
The old below-cell had five stone neighbours, but its upper source/probe corridor was open.
The saw control also used z=3 with a wall at z=4, outside the empty template's cleared z[-3,3].
Those facts expose timing and neighbouring-structure interference; the excerpt alone does not
identify which neighbour supplied the historical light. SKY light cannot explain a BLOCK-layer
reading, although the new fixture also verifies that no sky light enters the measured cell.

The repository-wide search found five transmission tests with opaque controls: mono motors,
bipropellant motors, all six nuclear facings, three fuel tanks and the saw. The first four share
the motor fixture. Station-light/thermite emission and solar/exposure sky-light tests have no
opaque transmission control and are deliberately untouched.

- `SealedLightColumn` replaces the two duplicate partial fixtures with complete stone shells.
  Seven 4x3x5 columns fit inside the existing empty template clearance, sharing only stone.
  Snapshots restore shared cells in reverse placement order, including on assertion failure.
- `LightPropagationBarrier` queues per-chunk `POST_UPDATE` markers after all fixture changes.
  Their futures complete after the light worker's preceding block checks, propagation and
  publication. The main thread polls without blocking. It does not relight chunks, alter light
  tickets, force-load chunks, or infer completion solely from `hasLightWork()`.
  Forge 1.20.1 has no public fence API, so this test-only helper identifies the unique private
  four-argument queue method by signature and the enum constant by name, independent of SRG
  method names. A signature change fails explicitly rather than bypassing the wait.
- Exact source=15, probe=13, transmitting-beneath=13 and opaque-beneath=0 assertions remain.
  All original occlusion, attenuation, collision, support, atmosphere, saw state and skylight
  properties remain asserted. Full shell integrity and measured SKY=0 are additional checks.
  The original 100-tick timeout and tick-99 deadline remain unchanged.
- `LightTransmissionNeighbours` provides opt-in luminous companion GameTests, one in each
  affected batch and in its own vanilla-placed structure, enabled with JVM property
  `advancedrocketrycommunity.lightTransmissionNeighbours=true`. Normal inventory is unchanged.
  The companions use only a centre glowstone block, verify light 15 and an open-air probe at 14,
  and retain the source until the disposable GameTest world is deleted by the launcher.
- `MonoMotorOcclusionFixtureTest` retains its three checks and adds two bounded geometry checks
  covering every shell cell, shared-wall compatibility and the control/probe geometry.

No ADR decision, save/network/schema contract or upstream provenance changed.
