### Item 1: LR1

`RocketJournalLeases` now keeps every record instance that leaves RAM, together with the mutation sequence of that
change. A record leaves RAM through a removal, a `replace`, a `put` that supersedes an earlier version, or a schema-1
migration. When the owner thread publishes an acknowledgement at or after that sequence, it drops the instance.
Acknowledgements only move forward, so the instance is absent from that generation and from every later one. The
published view carries the instances it still keeps as an immutable list next to the current records. The writer
still reads only the view, off the owner thread.

A lease that names T or a serial is refused if any of the following holds. L's exact expected record is excluded
from each check.

- A current record names T or the serial (unchanged).
- The acknowledged generation's own transfer index (`Acknowledgement.records()`) contains T.
- A kept instance names T or the serial. The serial rule is the same as before: a schema-3 record without a native
  envelope names every serial.
- More than 1,024 instances have been kept since the last acknowledgement. The oldest then fold into a floor that
  names every T and every serial until an acknowledgement at or after the floor, as the existing change history
  does. A lease that names neither T nor a serial is not affected.

Records of other rockets that name neither T nor the serial still never revoke L's lease, acknowledged or not. The
lease is still acquired and checked where it was before: at capture and immediately before replacement.
`RocketTransferSavedData` passes the instance that each change takes out of RAM to `RocketJournalLeases.changed`;
nothing else in it changed. `EscrowFlightJournal` has a comment change only.

### Item 2: LR2

`EscrowGovernedTerminal.JournalHold` is new and package-private. It combines three checks for one journal instance:
L's checked lease `logicalLease(L, T, serial)`, `actionAuthorityAvailable(L)`, and L's exact record.

- **Entry:** a fresh hold must hold. This replaces `actionAuthorityAvailable()` and the exact-record check.
- **Capture:** the F6 work keeps the hold that was current when the work was created. If that hold no longer holds,
  the work is closed and captured again, like a change of record, survivor, outcome or observation.
- **Barrier callback:** the work's own hold replaces `actionAuthorityAvailable()` and the exact-record check.
  - A healthy proof is kept while another rocket's change is pending.
  - The proof is discarded, with the existing `SOURCE_OR_JOURNAL_CHANGED` retry, by: L's own or unacknowledged
    change; another record naming T or the serial (item 1); a failed write; a stopped journal; or a different journal.
- **Checkpoint diagnostic:** the work's hold, checked against `RocketTransferSavedData.get(server)`, replaces the same
  two checks.

The following are unchanged: the native-proof, passenger, bay-boundary, envelope and transient-motion prerequisites;
F7's own proof and lease; the retry schedule; and the retry reasons.

## Choices where the task or R17 left room

1. **What "the last acknowledged generation" covers.** The kept instances include every acknowledged record that RAM
   no longer holds, and possibly more. A record that was put and removed after the acknowledgement was never written,
   but it also refuses until the next acknowledgement. This can only refuse. It lasts only until the next write,
   which the healthy writer completes within ticks.
2. **The refusal is not latched.** As Root's reading says, a lease that another rocket's conflict revoked holds again
   once that removal is acknowledged. A change of L itself still revokes the lease for good.
3. **History bound.** The kept instances have the same 1,024-entry bound as the change history. Beyond it they fold
   into a floor that names every T and every serial. The floor can only refuse a lease, and only after more than
   1,024 unacknowledged removals.
4. **F6 recapture.** If a work's kept hold has ended while a fresh hold holds (for example, after the change history
   folded), the work is closed and captured again without backoff, as for the other recapture conditions at that
   point. The barrier callback keeps its existing retry with backoff.
5. **Journal checks that R17 and the review do not list.** Two terminal tick checks still wait for a fully
   acknowledged journal: the clean-up of work for departed records, and the retention of envelope holds and retries.
   They only delay clean-up and never discard a healthy proof.
