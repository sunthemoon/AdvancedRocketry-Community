### F2: the visitor form

- A new live-only notice kind, `warp_visitor`
  (`station.advancedrocketrycommunity.notice.warp_visitor`), takes three arguments: the
  station name, the code (`WARP_STARTED` or `WARP_COMMITTED` only) and the target body. It
  is never stored: the codec already refuses every kind that is not an outcome, and
  `StationNoticeSummary` refuses it too.
- `StationNotice.forVisitors()` turns a start or commit into that form. A countdown
  (station, target, seconds) and an abort (station, code, target) carry no cost and no
  balance, so they are sent unchanged.
- Each recipient is now sent one of two forms. The live owner and members, the actor
  (see F3) and every player inside the region who holds VISIT access get the full
  notice. VISIT access is the same check that `/arce station warp status` uses: the
  owner, a member or an operator. Every other player inside the region gets the visitor
  form.
- English: `[%1$s] %2$s Target: %3$s`. Chinese: `[%1$s] %2$s 目标：%3$s`. `runData` wrote
  only these two lines into the v1.8 lang files, and they are committed with the
  generator.
- **Tests.**
  - `StationNoticeTest.theVisitorFormDropsTheCostAndTheBalance` (new) covers:
    - the arguments of both visitor forms;
    - that a countdown and an abort are unchanged;
    - that the kind is never stored and allows only its two codes;
    - its code-token key.
  - The pinned key lists in `StationNoticeTest` and `V180StationNoticeLanguageTest` gain
    the new key; no existing entry was removed.
  - In `StationNoticeGameTests.warpNoticesReachMembersAndVisitorsInsideTheRegionOnly`:
    - the owner, the member and a new operator inside the region who is not a member
      must receive exactly the full token list;
    - the visitor must receive exactly
      `[warp_visitor(station, warp_started code, moon), five countdown seconds, warp_visitor(station, warp_committed code, moon)]`;
    - none of the visitor's messages may contain the cost, the balance or a cost-class
      key.

### F3: the actor's current authority

- `StationNoticeService` no longer adds the actor unconditionally. An online actor is
  added with the full form only if they pass the same VISIT check as every other
  recipient, at each send.
- An actor who fails the check is treated like anyone else. Inside the live region they
  get the visitor form; elsewhere they get nothing.
- **De-op test** (`StationWarpAbortGameTests.aDeoppedOperatorsCountdownAborts`).
  - **What changed.** The old assertion required the de-opped former operator to
    receive the `WARP_ACTOR_CHANGED` abort. That is exactly the delivery that review F3
    names, and Root decided to remove it. The test now enforces the stricter rule:
    - while still an operator, the actor receives exactly the full start notice and the
      first countdown second;
    - the actor is then de-opped and moved to the gap outside the region, so that only
      their own authority could still reach them;
    - from then until the commit, they must receive nothing at all.
  - **Why this is not a relaxation.** The test now asserts more, not less:
    - the countdown ends and the station neither moves nor pays (both as before);
    - the abort is kept as the station's summary at revision 1, with no
      acknowledgement, because nobody was told live;
    - the audit line `ARCE_STATION_WARP action=commit code=WARP_ACTOR_CHANGED` for this
      station and actor appears exactly once (captured with the existing
      `AuditLogCapture`).
  - **Why the move out of the region.** If the former actor stayed inside the region,
    they would correctly receive the countdown and the abort as a visitor. That is the
    visitor rule, not the actor rule.

### F7: notice failures in `commit`

- `StationWarpService.commit` now builds and delivers the outcome notice inside a
  `try`. A `RuntimeException` is caught and logged as one WARN line:
  `ARCE_STATION_NOTICE_FAILED station=<id> code=<code> error=<exception class simple name>`.
  - The line is bounded: it has no message text and no stack trace.
  - The audit line `ARCE_STATION_WARP action=commit ...` follows as before.
  - The result, the cooldown and the write budget are unchanged.
- To make the failure injectable, `StationWarpService` gains a constructor that takes
  the `StationNoticeService`; the existing constructors pass a new one, as before.
  `StationNoticeService` is no longer `final`, so a GameTest can override
  `deliverOutcome`.
- **Test.** `StationNoticeGameTests.aFailingOutcomeNoticeNeitherEscapesNorSkipsTheAudit`
  (new, batch `station_notice_failure`) runs a private warp service on a private catalog
  with a notice service whose `deliverOutcome` throws. It asserts that:
  - the countdown ends and the station is at the Moon with 500,000 FE left;
  - the checked write is on disk;
  - exactly one `ARCE_STATION_NOTICE_FAILED ... code=WARP_COMMITTED error=IllegalStateException`
    line was logged for the station;
  - exactly one commit audit line was logged for it;
  - no summary was kept.

  An exception that escaped the tick would fail the test.
- **Log manifest** (`scripts/gametest_expected_log.json`, now 75 entries):
  - the new exact WARN expectation `station-notice-failed`, counted once in
    `station_notice_failure`;
  - one more `station-warp-energy-dropped` WARN in that batch, because its fixture
    deletes a station that still holds 500,000 FE.

  No ERROR expectation was added, so the pinned ERROR total (80) is unchanged.

## Choices where the decision was open

- **Visitor form scope.** Root's decision lists the station, the code and the target. I
  applied it to the start and commit notices, the only ones with a cost or a balance.
  - The countdown keeps its seconds. It has no code to show, and ADR-065 section 5.3
    keeps the countdown on the action bar.
  - An abort already has exactly the station, the code and the target.
  - The start's origin body and seconds are dropped from the visitor form along with
    the cost class, the cost and the balance.
- **Operators inside the region** who did not act get the full form, as Root decided.
  Operators elsewhere who did not act are still not told, as in W22.
- **No live station.** When the station has been deleted or the registry is unavailable,
  only the operator part of the VISIT check can still be evaluated. So only an actor who
  is an operator now is told; an owner whose station is gone is not. No existing test
  covered this case.
- **The F7 WARN** names the exception's simple class name only, like
  `ARCE_FORCE_FIELD_LISTENER_REFUSED`, so that its length is bounded.

- **Positional visitors.** As in W22, a player passing through the region is a visitor
  only while their block position is inside it.
- **The actor outside the region.** An actor who loses their authority and is still
  inside the region receives the countdown and the abort in the visitor form. Neither
  carries a cost or a balance.
- **Size guidance.** `StationWarpService` grows from 656 to 671 lines; it was already over
  500 and stays under 800. The addition is the constructor and the guarded delivery of the
  same commit.
