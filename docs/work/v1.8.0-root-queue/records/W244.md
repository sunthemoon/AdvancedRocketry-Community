### Design note (written before the code)

**The legacy capsule.** At upstream `c5cd5af6`, `RenderElevatorCapsule.doRender` translates to the capsule entity's
render position plus one block up, `(x, y + 1, z)`, and rotates by the entity's yaw about +Y. It binds
`spaceElevatorCapsule.png` and draws the `Capsule` group of `spaceElevator.obj`. It draws `Door` only while the
capsule moves. `TileSpaceElevator` spawns the entity on the landing pad at the controller's y + 1 and sets its yaw by the
controller's front: EAST 180, SOUTH 90, NORTH 270, and 0 otherwise. The legacy OBJ loader flips V (`1 - v`), as the K4
fixture's `flipV` does.

**What `Capsule` is.** It is 104 triangles over 60 positions. They form a closed octagonal shell, 3 blocks across and
4 blocks high, with an outer and an inner wall, a floor, a ceiling and both caps. On the outside of its local +z side
are a rail and two small boxes. Its local −x side has a doorway 1.09 blocks wide and 3.5 blocks high, which the `Door`
group closes. Every face samples only opaque texels of the capsule texture (`capsule-faces.py`, `capsule-faces.log`).
The exceptions are six faces with unmapped default UVs: the back of the rail and the backs of the two boxes. Each lies
against an opaque face of the opposite orientation, so it cannot be seen.

**The modern draw.**

- **What is drawn.** While at least one ride is pending at an endpoint, the client draws one stationary capsule there.
  It is exactly the `Capsule` group of the already-imported K4 `models/spaceelevator.obj`, drawn with
  `textures/models/spaceelevatorcapsule.png` through the group's own UVs, V flipped as in the legacy loader. Triangles
  are emitted as quads that repeat their last corner, as Forge's OBJ baker does. The render type stays
  `entityCutoutNoCull`. The countdown tint of the cage it replaces stays: the endpoint's ride closest to departure sets
  it.
- **Where it is drawn.** The legacy transform is used unchanged: the entity's position, translated by `(0, 1, 0)`, then
  rotated by the yaw about +Y.
  - The entity position's modern counterpart is the endpoint's arrival point, `ElevatorEndpointBlockEntity.arrival`:
    the platform centre at the endpoint's y + 1, where riders board and arrive. Legacy riders boarded at the same height
    above the controller.
  - The yaw is 270, the legacy value for a north-facing controller. The modern endpoints have no facing, and the K4
    anchor hull is always drawn as the legacy north-facing elevator. The doorway therefore faces north.
  - No width reflection is applied. The capsule was a separate entity, not part of the mirrored pattern, and reflecting
    it would mirror its texture.
  - A unit test pins every drawn corner against an independent transcription of this transform.
- **Riders stay visible.** Legacy drew `Door` only while the capsule moved. A pending ride is stationary, so `Door` is
  never drawn and the doorway stays open, and riders are seen through it. The shell does not hide the rider entity; the
  vanilla renderer draws riders as before. From inside, a rider sees the inner walls and out through the doorway. As in
  legacy, the floor lies 0.25 blocks above the rider's feet. A rider at a corner of the modern 3 x 3 platform stands
  partly outside the octagon.
- **One capsule per endpoint.** The pure selection state gains one capsule per endpoint, derived from its existing
  nearest-first, one-endpoint-per-rider effects. The geometry is therefore bounded by the unchanged limit of 64
  endpoints: at most 64 x 416 = 26,624 vertices a frame. The old cage was at most 112 vertices per rider.
- **Budgets.** The group's vertices already count in K4's catalog budget, inside `elevator_anchor`'s 8,776, and the
  catalog total stays 35,268. The capsule reader reads the OBJ once more per reload, bounded by the existing
  16 MiB set limit. It runs the unchanged strict `ObjModelCheck.checkObj` on the whole file and accepts the group only
  within 1 to 256 faces and the model box (−2.5, −1, −2.5) to (2.5, 3, 2.5).
- **Checks once per reload (W244-R4).** A client reload listener reads the OBJ and the texture once per resource
  reload, off the render thread, and publishes the mesh or nothing. The texture must pass `ObjModelCheck.checkPng`:
  signature, header, at most 1,024 pixels a side, and a full decode. Rendering reads only the published field.
- **Fallback.** A missing or unreadable model or texture, or a missing or invalid `Capsule` group, leaves nothing
  published, and the existing per-rider line cage is drawn as before.
- **No new state.** There is no new field, entity, packet, ride action or persisted state. W244's strip geometry
  (`CapsuleCageGeometry`) and its unit test are replaced by the mesh and its tests.

**W244-R2.** A commit whose identical-candidate rename was recorded but which neither graph pass lists is a merge
compared against a later parent. Such a commit is now listed in the history after both passes, with its
`rename_candidates`, and its message is checked for triggers like every listed commit. Existing records do not change:
before R259 no candidate could be recorded, and W244's only candidate commit is an ordinary one.
