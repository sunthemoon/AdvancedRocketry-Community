## Changed files and design choices

- `rocket/model`: `RocketSnapshotHasher`, `RocketSnapshotPreparation` and
  `RocketStructureSnapshot` share the existing canonical writers with two
  streaming digest states. The completion constructor is package-private and
  accepts only internal computed hashes. The payload-only result must retain
  the old structure digest. No whole canonical-input buffer is retained.
- `rocket/persistence/RocketSnapshotNbtCodec`: the immutable-snapshot witness
  checks decoded fields, payloads, bounds, hash and canonical encoding without
  synchronously rehashing the expanded palette. Ordinary decoding still hashes.
- `rocket/cargo`: `RocketCargoPreparation`, `RocketCargoPreparations` and
  `RocketCargoWorkBudget` implement staged work, lifecycle ownership and
  reservations. One admitted continuation owns the tick's reconstruction slot;
  small requests cannot displace it. The waiting-device fair queue remains C-E's.
- `resourceescrow`: `EscrowCargoPreparedCapture`, `EscrowCargoPublication`,
  `EscrowCargoCapture`, `EscrowCargoEligibility`, `EscrowCoordinator`,
  `EscrowRuntime` and `RocketEscrowGate` add the smallest capture, lifecycle and
  publication hooks. `RocketRuntime` and `RocketEntity` bridge locks and atomic
  publication to Forge objects.
- Tests add `RocketCargoPreparationTest`, `EscrowCargoPreparedCaptureTest`,
  `EscrowCargoPermitUseTest` and `EscrowCargoPreparationGameTests`, and extend
  `RocketStructureDigestTest`. Documentation is limited to this record and plan.

ADR-065's existing limits remain one reconstruction, 16 MiB and 16,384 block
visits per tick, without accumulated credit. Hash output bytes are counted
exactly. Native copies, encoding, validation and digest passes reserve
conservative ceilings before execution and charge at actual input lengths;
the capture's final O1 step reserves 8 MiB, while final admission and native
publication envelope steps reserve whole ticks.
BEFORE and AFTER publication envelopes are verified in separate ticks, rather
than combining their walks. The original supplier-constructor test verifies
reserved execution; it does not exercise the production `nativeCapture` factory.
Changed inputs require a fresh complete capture before sequence allocation,
reservation or queueing. C-E must supply the exact device/configuration
unchanged predicate. W112F removes caller-supplied budgets from these entries.

No saved schema, hash definition, bound, proof, lease, replay exclusion or
production cargo-admission gate changed. No upstream asset/code was imported,
and no ADR, risk register, status file, ledger or build script was edited.
