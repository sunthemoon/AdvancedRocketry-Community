## F7 mapped-layout evidence and choice

The command used Java 17's `$env:JAVA_HOME/bin/javap.exe -c -p` against
`net.minecraft.client.gui.components.BossHealthOverlay` in the installed mapped jar:

```text
C:/Users/Administrator/.gradle/caches/forge_gradle/minecraft_user_repo/net/minecraftforge/forge/1.20.1-47.4.10_mapped_official_1.20.1/forge-1.20.1-47.4.10_mapped_official_1.20.1.jar
```

The jar's SHA-256 is
`95eecc5985233d83a6571299f89f02de034267646da171f7b36a5be2d394d71e`.
In `render`, bytecode offsets 17–19 start the bar at y=12; offsets 58–64 center
it at width/2-91; offsets 79–95 supply the default increment 10+9=19 to Forge's
`BossEventProgress`; offsets 149–153 place the name at y-9; offsets 175–190 use
the event's final increment and stop after the next y reaches height/3. The
`drawBar` overload passes width 182 and blits height 5. These are mapped 1.20.1
observations, not an assumed layout from another version.

The chosen option is **below the drawn boss bars**, not an additional W16 panel.
The client resets occupancy in `RenderGuiEvent.Pre` and observes uncancelled
`BossEventProgress` events at lowest priority. It reserves through the greatest
actual bar y+5, so Forge-customized increments and vanilla's visible-bar cutoff
are respected without reading the private boss-event map. The strip's highest
pixel stays three pixels below that exclusive bottom edge. With no bars it keeps
the original top of 3. If the strip and label do not fit, they are omitted rather
than clipped or moved over a bar. V1 visual evidence is explicitly out of scope.
