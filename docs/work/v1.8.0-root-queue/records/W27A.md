## Choices where the ADR left room

The note lists eight choices for the reviewer (section 11). The main ones:
- The package is consumed at altitude while the transfer waits in TRANSIT, and the wait lasts until the
  receipt retires. The alternative, consumption after landing, would leave unconsumed copies in the
  destination and the old source entity.
- Receipts get their own managed file, not a section of the 1 MiB ledger root, because the ledger writer
  encodes on the server thread.
- The registry proof is an exact acknowledgement added to the existing synchronous checked replace, under
  ADR-050 section 2's launch barrier policy. If the 16 MiB benchmark fails, the implementer stops; an
  off-thread registry writer needs ADR-050's follow-up ADR.
- Transfer recovery must tolerate a consumed snapshot hash through the receipt, because it matches rockets
  by content hash.
- The transaction journal needs a checked acknowledgement as a deployment precondition.
- At restart, INTENT with an absent satellite becomes REJECTED (INTERRUPTED) instead of registering again.
