## Landing policy

[ADR-033](../../../decisions/ADR-033-PLANETARY-WORLDS-AND-SURFACE-ADMISSION.md) deliberately retained Earth's
spawn-relative eight candidates, heightmap height, and exemption from the new surfaces' sturdy-support rule.
[ADR-006](../../../decisions/ADR-006-ROCKET-CROSS-DIMENSION-TRANSFER.md) requires bounded server-owned coordinates,
not a search for arbitrary nearby terrain. Neither requires accepting fluid. Simply enabling support on Earth would
also reject dry non-sturdy spawn terrain and would change more than this bug needs.

The implementation separates fluid safety from sturdy support. A surface candidate must have no fluid at the
heightmap top of any occupied horizontal column, including columns occupied only by upper rocket blocks and lower
columns beneath overhangs. This is not a downward scan: a solid roof/platform over subsurface fluid is still dry
ground. The unnamed Space pad checks both each column's heightmap top and the landing plane without acquiring
sturdy-support semantics.
The eight offsets, ordering, chunk policy, terrain readiness, inspection limits, and existing support decisions stay.

The surface loop adds at most 2,048 fluid-state reads to its existing, at-most-2,048 heightmap column queries, with no
additional chunk positions. The unnamed station phase deduplicates columns, counts two fluid reads per column before
world access, and refuses before exceeding the existing 2,048-inspection limit. Its heightmap and landing-plane reads
use only the footprint chunks already admitted and loaded by the selector. Named pads already reject fluid in their
bounded support check and are not changed.

Recorded-destination rechecks and reconstruction retain their existing compatibility rules, including
[ADR-075](../../../decisions/ADR-075-MOON-LANDING-ON-THE-HEIGHTMAP.md)'s y 80 Moon threshold. This task concerns new
selection and does not relocate recorded destinations or already-landed rockets.

When all eight candidates are occupied or wet, selection returns the existing audit-only
`all fixed pads are occupied or unsafe`; its caller returns `LANDING_PAD_UNAVAILABLE`, with no new journal entry or
fuel debit. The unnamed Space pad has no alternative candidate and uses `station pad is occupied or unsafe`.

### Exact clarification proposed for Root, not an ADR edit

> For new landing selections in every dimension, fluid safety is independent of the sturdy-support exemption.
> Reject a surface candidate when the block immediately below its MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES heightmap value in any
> occupied horizontal column has a nonempty fluid state, including waterlogged blocks. Reject fluid directly below
> the landing plane or at an occupied column's heightmap top of an unnamed station pad as well. Keep Earth's
> spawn-relative fixed candidates, heightmap height, and exemption from sturdy support; keep Space's existing support
> exemption. Do not search additional
> coordinates or heights, build platforms, or load chunks outside the existing candidate footprints. Exhaustion uses
> the existing unsafe-pad refusal.
> Recorded-destination rechecks, recovery origins, and ADR-075's legacy Moon threshold are unchanged.
