## What the bay does

- **Root.** Schema 1 under the key `arce_satellite_bay`: `schema_version` 1, `owner` (int[4]), then
  at most one of `package` (the raw native item tag) or `consumed_op` (int[4]). The root's own bound
  is 4,352 bytes, depth 18 and 272 nodes. The package has the native item bound (4,096 bytes, depth
  16, 256 nodes, as `FuelPayloadBounds.item`), an `id` string and `Count` exactly 1. The codec
  refuses and never normalizes. Unknown fields, both entries or wrong types make the root INVALID; a
  newer `schema_version` makes it FUTURE. Either way the bay keeps the tag unchanged and is guarded.
  A bay that has no root (for example after `/setblock`) is an empty bay without an owner.
- **Package identity.** `SatelliteItemData.readTag` reads the identity from the raw `tag`, so an
  unregistered item still has one. An unregistered item ID is PACKAGE_UNAVAILABLE, a FUTURE identity
  in a package item is UNSUPPORTED_DATA, and anything else that is not a valid package by
  `SatelliteTerminalInventory.validPackage`, or would not come back byte for byte from
  `ItemStack.of`, is INVALID_PACKAGE. All three stay raw.
- **Locks.** Insertion needs a live, owned, empty and unclaimed bay and a package whose identity
  owner is the bay owner. Extraction needs a live, unclaimed bay with an available package. A
  position is claimed while a record of the rocket transaction journal has a region that contains it
  (`RocketTransactionSavedData.claims`, at most 256 records; a blocked journal claims every
  position). Captured bays have no block entity, so they have no slot.
- **Menu.** Opens from `use` only, for the owner or an operator (permission 2), never a spectator or
  a fake player, within 8 blocks of the bay's centre, and never for a guarded bay; a guarded bay
  shows its guard message instead. `getMenuProvider` returns null, so a spectator's container
  shortcut cannot reach it. The slot's `mayPlace` is the handler's `isItemValid`, `mayPickup` a
  simulated extraction, and `set` and `remove` go through the handler; dragging into it is off.
  Two `ContainerData` slots carry the phase (NONE) and the fixed reason DEPLOYMENT_UNAVAILABLE.
- **Removal.** A valid, available, unclaimed bay drops its package once as the ordinary item plus a
  plain bay item (no `BlockEntityTag`). The loot table has a dynamic entry
  `advancedrocketrycommunity:rocket_satellite_bay_package`, and the removal callback drops the
  package too; one `extracted` flag on the block entity lets only the first of them emit it. A
  `consumed_op` bay drops only the bay. Guarded and claimed bays cancel `BreakEvent`, return false
  from `onDestroyedByPlayer` and `canEntityDestroy`, leave the explosion's block list, ignore
  `onBlockExploded`, have zero destroy progress and return no loot. A raw removal of a guarded root
  (`/setblock`, `/fill`, third-party code) is detected and logged only.
- **Placement and NBT.** `setPlacedBy` gives a new bay its placing player's UUID; it never loads a
  carried root and never overwrites an existing one. `onlyOpCanSetNbt()` is true, so only an
  operator's item NBT reaches the bay. A live load (`/data`, an operator's item NBT, a structure)
  of a bay that holds a guarded root keeps that root and logs one line.
- **Rocket adapter.** `satellite_bay_v1` is frozen beside `vanilla_container_v1` in both
  `RocketAdapterRegistry.freeze()` and `RocketBlockEntityAdapters.defaults()`. External adapters can
  claim neither its ID nor the bay's block entity type. Capture copies the root exactly (an empty
  compound for a bay without a root) and throws for a quarantined root, so such a rocket does not
  scan. Restore loads the copy into the fresh bay; the existing recapture check then compares it.
  Each bay is one of the 128 block-entity payloads.
- **Save refusal (R-038).** A held root that native serialization can keep is written unchanged by
  `saveAdditional`. One it cannot keep is written by reference, its chunk is recorded through
  `GuardedChunkSaves.recordObservedDenial` at load and `onLoad`, and `SatelliteBayProtection`
  refuses any outgoing chunk that carries such a bay root through `GuardedChunkSaves.refuse`.
- **Audit lines (WARN).** `ARCE_SATELLITE_BAY_GUARDED` once per load that ends guarded,
  `ARCE_SATELLITE_BAY_LIVE_REPLACEMENT_REFUSED` once per refused live load, and
  `ARCE_SATELLITE_BAY_GUARDED_ROOT_REMOVED` once per raw removal of a guarded root. No total cap.

## Choices where the design was open

1. **Package.** The bay lives in `rocket/bay`, not `satellite/`. Neither package imported the other
   before; the bay needs the transaction journal and the rocket adapter needs the bay, so a
   `satellite` placement would have made a two-way dependency (AGENTS.md section 3.4). Now `rocket`
   depends on `satellite` only.
2. **"Native serialization cannot keep it."** I read R-038 literally: only a root the native writer
   would change, or the native chunk reader could not read back, refuses a save. That is a lossy
   write (`BoundedNbt`'s checks: typed empty list, string over 65,535 bytes, non-canonical NaN,
   unknown tag class) or a node deeper than 510 below the root (the root sits at chunk depth 3; the
   reader throws past 512). A larger root that is still lossless is written unchanged. The walk needs
   a hard bound, so a root above vanilla's 2 MiB NBT accounting quota or 2,097,152 nodes is treated as
   unkeepable too. That is conservative: it refuses a save, it never drops data.
3. **Claimed bays are protected like guarded ones** (no break, out of explosions, no drops). The
   task drops only an *unclaimed* bay's package and does not say what a claimed bay does. A bay inside
   an interrupted transaction's region is also in that transaction's snapshot, so dropping it could
   duplicate the package when recovery restores the rocket. The claim also fails closed when the
   journal is blocked. R-038 does not list claims; they refuse no save.
4. **Capture accepts raw packages.** A schema-1 root whose package is unavailable (FUTURE identity,
   unknown item, invalid) is captured byte for byte; only a root that fails the codec refuses
   capture. Refusing raw packages would have left such a rocket impossible to assemble, since the bay
   itself cannot be broken. W27d's D0 refuses deployment for them.
5. **A consumed bay accepts nothing.** The root allows only one of `package` and `consumed_op`, and
   the marker is W27d's tombstone, so insertion would have to erase it. Breaking the bay and placing a
   new one gives an empty bay.
6. **A bay without an owner** (for example from `/setblock`) opens only for operators and accepts no
   package. It gains an owner only through placement by a player or an operator's NBT.
7. **The menu refuses guarded bays** and shows the guard message on use; the two status slots stay
   the fixed phase and reason, as the task asks.
8. **The rocket tag.** The immutable v0.5 `rocket_movable.json` cannot list the bay without a
   `build.gradle` exclusion, so I added a v1.8 tag `advancedrocketrycommunity:rocket_satellite_bays`
   and accepted it beside `rocket_movable` and `rocket_propulsion` in the scan and transaction worlds,
   as the propulsion slice did for its tag.
9. **Recipe and art.** The recipe is community-designed (`p p` / `pcp` / `pbp`: aluminum plates, a
   chest, a basic circuit). The textures are drawn in code by `V180SatelliteBayData.grid`; the
   upstream `satelliteBay.png` is REVIEW in the asset plan and was not used.
10. **Audit lines.** R-038 asks for one line per state transition and one per backoff transition.
    W27b has no backoff state (that belongs to the W27c1 writer), so it writes none; its transitions
    are the three WARN lines above. Normal insertion and extraction are not logged, so hoppers cannot
    flood the log.

## R-038 wording

What I built matches R-038: guarded roots and unavailable identities are protected (no player
break, out of the explosion list, no drops, no extraction or deployment); only a root native
serialization cannot keep refuses its terrain chunk, then the whole ServerLevel after 256 denied
coordinates, until the Level closes; a serializable quarantined root is saved unchanged; the
guarded state returns at every load; a valid bay drops its package and an empty bay once;
`/setblock`, `/fill` and third-party raw replacement are detection only. Two notes for Root, neither
a contradiction: R-038 does not mention the transaction-claim protection (choice 3), and the
"keepable" bound includes the 2 MiB walk limit (choice 2).

## Known risks

- Breaking a bay inside an interrupted transaction's region is refused until recovery removes the
  journal record (choice 3). A blocked journal locks every bay.
- `GuardedChunkSaves` saturation is Level-wide: 257 unkeepable bay roots in one Level stop all of its
  terrain saves until it closes. S1 shows that this is R-038's stated scope.
- `SatelliteBayPackage.decode` runs `ItemStack.of` on every insertion attempt; hoppers try every 8
  ticks. The package is at most 4,096 bytes, so the cost is small, but it was not measured.
