## Files and test coverage

- [StationWarpRestartGameTests](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/gametest/StationWarpRestartGameTests.java)
  adds three F8 tests. Each restores the stateless core and reinstalls a decoded station registry as the live
  authority. The controller and warp service check charging, pending and committed cuts, the exact energy balance,
  orbit and countdown, one-shot confirmation, and the absence of a later replay.
- [ElevatorRestartGameTests](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/gametest/ElevatorRestartGameTests.java)
  adds three F12 tests. Cargo is tested in both directions with both endpoint block entities and the endpoint,
  pair and transit tables reopened together, before and after materialization. The assertions cover every input
  and receive slot, tagged payloads and unrelated inventory, identity, owner and exact energy. The ride test covers
  a discarded pending ride and a fresh ride followed by a committed-state restore, counting teleport effects and
  checking energy and arrival tickets.
- [SimpleDeviceRestartGameTests](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/gametest/SimpleDeviceRestartGameTests.java)
  adds three F3 tests using native chunk save, unload and disk reload in isolated fixture chunks. They check the
  press's persisted powered edge and exact item output, consistent door halves and power changes, and an inert
  retained seal handle with working fresh forwarding.
- [RestartTestSupport](../../../../src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/gametest/RestartTestSupport.java)
  provides test-only current-tag block-entity replacement and cleanup on success, failure or timeout.

## Design decisions and boundaries

No ADR decision was changed. [ADR-044 section 4](../../../decisions/ADR-044-STATION-WARP-LOGICAL-RELOCATION.md)
discards confirmations and countdowns at stop. Its stop hook folds charging credits before the registry snapshot.
The pending-state test therefore requires fresh controller consent instead of inventing a persistent countdown.
[ADR-059 section 8](../../../decisions/ADR-059-SPACE-ELEVATOR-LOGISTICS.md) has no passenger journal;
`ElevatorRides.clear` releases tickets and forgets pending rides. The ride test uses that lifecycle boundary, not a
nonexistent serialized ride registry. Pair and cargo state do have saved forms and are restored jointly.

Station/elevator tests are current-format snapshot recovery on a running GameTest server, not S1/S2 process restart
evidence. Only the three simple-device tests request a native chunk unload and disk read. Native campaigns, visual
checks, production fixes and W415's devices are outside this task. There were no upstream imports.
