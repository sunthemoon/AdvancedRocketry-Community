  uncommitted, readable, zero-claim projector placement. It releases the exact

  Native survival placements at 4,095 and 4,096 entries check world, inventory and

  Forge commits its item debit. Unowned projectors also reserve capacity but
  remain inert. Their existing schema-1 identity uses the all-zero owner UUID
  only as an internal reservation sentinel; authorized binding atomically
  replaces that empty reservation without another slot. Existing owned data is
  unchanged, and claimed, orphaned or conflicting reservations cannot be rebound.

  initializes its own Minecraft bootstrap instead of depending on suite order.
- F3, `70cdd2e9`: `spawnAfterBreak` defers world-destruction loot until physical
  removal succeeds. Refusal clears the pending drop. Ordinary loot queries do
  not arm a drop, and explosions retain their separate post-removal drop without
  duplication. Paired native `Level.destroyBlock(pos, true)` tests check exactly
  one dropped projector on success, zero on refusal, the remaining source blocks
  and a repeated destruction call.
- F4, `79c26e1f`: power is computed through a loaded-only `SignalGetter` snapshot
  of the stock query's 25 distinct positions. Reads use `getChunkNow`, never a
  loading Level accessor. An unavailable cell or a callback read outside that
  snapshot gives no decision and no effect. The scheduler reserves bounded work
  capacity, then refunds unused capacity while retaining the five-tick cooldown.
  Accounting includes distinct cells actually read and successful mutations,
  including cleanup and validation. Native tests use actual item placements,
  a loaded edge conductor with an absent second-hop chunk, and seven conductively
  surrounded projectors. An observer attached to actual loaded-chunk reads
  independently verifies 50 cells inspected and five visits deferred under the
  unchanged 64-cell cap. `229e923f` loads the complete power stencil in existing
  native fixtures; no assertion, timeout or budget was weakened.
