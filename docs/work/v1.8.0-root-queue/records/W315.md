## The fix

- **Moon iteration: no station precondition.** Station regions are Space Level coordinates. In production, only
  `SleepRespawnService.station` consults them, and it returns `NOT_APPLICABLE` for every Level other than the Space
  Level. The atmosphere authority does not consult station regions at all. So no station, wherever it is, can change
  the Moon refusal. The Moon bed keeps the structure's own column. Its existing assertion of the `UNBREATHABLE`
  action-bar explanation still proves that the refusal comes from the air and not from a station.
- **Space iteration: a bounded search.** `outsideEveryStation` now searches for a Space column outside every station
  region instead of asserting one fixed column. It keeps the old first candidate: the structure's column moved along
  x, keeping its offset inside the station cell, to the cell eight beyond the last layer the allocator's spiral can
  hand out. If that is taken, it moves one cell further out, at most eight cells (`STATION_FREE_CANDIDATES`).
  - A candidate is taken only when each of the 49 columns within radius 3 is outside every committed station and
    every reservation, which a later commit would turn into a station.
  - The work is capped at 8 x 49 lookups, plus a scan of the reservations, which are capped at 64.
  - The registry must be operational. A blocked registry answers every lookup with no station, while Space sleep
    would then refuse with `UNAVAILABLE` instead.
  - If no candidate is free, the test fails and names every refused column and the station or reservation over it.
- An explicit Space-only check stays where the old check was: `The Space vacuum fixture at ... lies inside a station`.
- Unchanged: the `NOT_POSSIBLE_HERE` refusal, the `!isSleeping()` check, the `UNBREATHABLE` action-bar explanation,
  the unset respawn point, the timeout and the cleanup.

The task left the search's form open. I chose to extend the existing far-cell scheme outward along x, because those
cells can only be taken by a restore or by another fixture's search, never by the allocator.

## Stations left by other tests

The five tests in the table above leave their stations. None of them has a `finally` of its own. Their cleanup is a
`GameTestInfo` listener or a fixture close that releases tickets and blocks but never deletes the station. Deleting
it would have to go through station deletion for a station that a flight has used, which is not a missing `finally`
line. As the task requires, I did not change them; I name them here for Root. Their leftovers will keep moving later
allocator stations outward, and any other test that compares its own column with station regions can meet them.
