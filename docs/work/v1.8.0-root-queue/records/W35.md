## Design decisions and changed areas

The audited MIT upstream `trackingcircuit.json` at commit `c5cd5af62fc07cd4e0d24f06a16033f181c47c04`
has SHA-256 `a17dd4c66b7cf05952a7db2ad4a9cbcd299e93460dd02aebf682325575b06ee8`.
One redstone dust, one ender eye and one Basic Circuit Plate make one Tracking
Circuit in 900 ticks at 50 FE/t. The audited baseline supplies only a machine
producer, despite the TASK's parenthetical reference to crafting. No workbench
recipe was invented. The new item and producer IDs are `tracking_circuit` and
`precision_tracking_circuit` in the existing mod namespace.

`precision_control_circuit` replaces two redstone dust with two Control Circuit
Boards. `precision_guidance_module` makes that replacement and replaces its
gold ingot with a Tracking Circuit. Their stable IDs, counts, outputs, times
and energy budgets are unchanged. Existing signature-change recovery retains
old work and resources; no new automatic migration or signature mapping was
added. Historical-signature fixtures still seed their historical inputs.
Only these two current precision stand-ins were rebalanced. Historical generated
resources and bootstrap crafting producers were not changed.

Changes are confined to the assigned registration/creative entries,
`datagen/V180TrackingCircuitData`, data bootstrap/languages, the current precision
recipe provider and material-provider comment, v1.8 generated resources,
precision fixtures and opt-in commands, `run_v120_precision_restart_smoke.py`,
the new circuit tests, existing graph/kernel-resource tests, CHANGELOG and the
two new provenance records. No status, ledger, ADR, build, configuration,
network or persistence implementation was edited.

The graph still requires all 242 registered items except exactly the five
authorized cross-mod dusts, and requires the one sealing enchantment. Removing
Tau Ceti surface access leaves exactly 16 registered items unreachable: the
five dusts and the eleven C15c items (four lightwood items, electric mushroom
and six colored crystal blocks). The exact whole-graph loss is 24 nodes,
including the corresponding dust tags, two Level-access nodes and leaf-block
node. All 15 implemented advancement goals remain reachable; the existing two
world-first pending goals are unchanged. Discovery/orbital access is not removed.
