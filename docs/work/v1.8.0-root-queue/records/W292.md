# W292: rider placement at assigned structure seats

## Scope

The write scope is rider-position calculation and its entity adapter, the flight console's mounted-player
distance checks, focused tests, and this record. Seat assignment, roster semantics, persistence, packets,
rendered rocket models, data generation, status files, the ledger, and QUEUE are unchanged.

The seat index continues to identify the snapshot's existing sorted passenger anchors. Rider X and Z use
the seat's relative block-center coordinates; Y is the anchor plus 0.15 blocks, preserving the old sitting
offset for the usual seat at relative Y=1. Anchors outside the structure bounds and absent anchors use
the original fixed-height fallback. The client derives ordered seat positions from the already synced
visual blocks and seat tags, once per immutable snapshot, in an entity-owned cache. Incomplete client
seat recognition falls back rather than shifting later seat indexes. No network field is added.

Mounted players remain eligible for their own console irrespective of distance from the rocket origin.
All other menu and request guards remain unchanged, and unmounted players still need the original
interaction distance. Boarding and remounts retain vanilla's rider-position hook. Ordinary transfer,
reconnect, governed escrow transfer, and elevator fixture placement still use their existing teleport
and mount adapters. Their preliminary positions are staging locations, not seat offsets. The bay exit
already captures the actual rider position before detach; its descent continuity and safe-exit search
therefore receive the new seat position without any change to their policy or fixtures.
