## Choices where the ADR or the TASK left something open

- **Sound definition namespace.** The historical v0.1 `sounds.json` in the mod
  namespace is an immutable DataGen output, and a second file at the same path
  would collide in the JAR. Following the v1.8 language-file convention, the ten
  definitions live in `assets/advancedrocketrycommunity_v180/sounds.json`. The
  registry IDs are `advancedrocketrycommunity:<id>` as ADR-066 requires; each
  event's sound location is `advancedrocketrycommunity_v180:<id>`, and the OGG
  files are in the mod namespace. A consequence: `/playsound` needs the
  `advancedrocketrycommunity_v180:` name. If Root prefers locations equal to the
  registry IDs, `build.gradle` must exclude the v0.1 file and the definitions
  move; that is a Root-owned build change.
- **NEW audio, not imports.** All ten legacy OGG files remain `REVIEW`. The sounds
  are synthesized from code (`StrictMath`, fixed-seed generator) and encoded by
  the project encoder, mono 22,050 Hz, 0.42 to 3.02 seconds. `lathe` and
  `rolling_machine` are two distinct files. Loops are periodic over their length.
- **Ranges.** Loops and the railgun bang use a fixed 32-block range; the shock and
  laser gun events 16 blocks. Loops start within 30 blocks, stop beyond 32
  (re-checked every tick by each loop), at most 32 loops per client, nearest
  first, with deterministic ties. Discovery runs every 10 ticks over the loaded
  chunks within 32 blocks, nearest chunks first, at most 2,048 block entities and
  64 rockets. One-shots have simultaneous caps (railgun 8, shock 1, laser gun 4)
  and a 40-tick cut-off. These follow the unfrozen audio CONTRACT-02 numbers.
- **Loop producers.** `air_hiss_loop` follows the vent's `LIT` block state,
  `gravity_controller` and `laser_drill` their `active` update tag,
  `combustion_rocket` the rocket's synchronized `ASCENT` or `DESCENT` state.
  `railgun_bang` plays only for a launch effect the client accepted (no arrival
  bang, open owner question A-OPEN-6); `electric_shock_small` plays on an
  accepted mushroom flash, so it follows `effects.electricMushroomFlashes`.
  `effects.advancedVisuals` does not silence audio (A-OPEN-8 not assumed).
- **HUD data.** The hydrogen and suit panels read the local player's worn suit as
  the server synchronizes it, through the same bounded codec and authorization
  gate that the merged W06 visor client uses. The HUD never predicts gas use.
  The rocket readout has no legacy position key, so it requests the top-left
  corner and is placed by the same no-overlap pass. Default positions stack the
  four configurable panels in the top-right column (y 6, 30, 62 and 94). The
  earlier CONTRACT-02 leaf excluded hydrogen and suit panels; this TASK adds
  them, and the environment and oxygen panels keep their text, colours, gauge
  and visibility fences unchanged.
- **Accents.** With `effects.advancedVisuals=false`, the gravity field sparkles,
  black hole generator portal swirl, laser target impact sparks, electric
  mushroom sparks and the railgun launch streak are not spawned. The railgun
  launch flash and arrival sparkle, the beams and every status indicator stay.
- **Component rendering data.** ADR-066 section 7.1 asks for "bounded immutable
  S2C module/gas summaries". No such dedicated packet exists, and the TASK allows
  a new network message only when section 7 requires one. This slice derives the
  summary (module kinds and tank gases only, at most eight attachments) from the
  equipment vanilla already synchronizes for tracked players, through the bounded
  codec with its 16 KiB, depth 16 and 256-node preflight, and keeps no player
  object. Root should confirm this reading; the alternative is a dedicated S2C
  summary with a protocol change and the section 13 design note and review.
- **`sky.overworldOverride`.** The Overworld currently has no ARCE sky rendering,
  so the key is defined and documented but both values show the vanilla sky.

## Not done

- **Model bake (`ClientProxy.modelBakeEvent`).** No OBJ/MTL file has been
  imported; the asset plan lists them as imports of C16b, C17a and C18b (rows 266
  to 271). There is nothing to bake, and building an unused loader would break
  AGENTS.md section 3.1.
- **Elevator capsule effect.** There is no S2C countdown carrier for pending rides
  (only the tether flag), and ADR-066 section 9 forbids inventing a renderer
  packet schema. It needs a Root-owned carrier first.
- **Unbound sound events.** `lathe`, `rolling_machine` and `machine_large` are
  registered with NEW audio but have no producer: no processing state reaches
  nearby clients (audio A-OPEN-5 and A-OPEN-7, Root-owned). `basic_laser_gun` has
  no item yet (C18b).
- **Black-hole sky** (station sky payload schema 2 and `celestial_snapshot`
  protocol 4) is not part of this TASK and is not started.
- Status files and the ledger rows are Root's.

## Known risks

- The encoder is deliberately simple: decoded SNR is 26 to 40 dB per file
  (measured), with pre-echo on sharp attacks. A listen may ask for retuning.
- The component mounts are placed from body and head model coordinates; their
  fit over the armour model is unverified until V1.
- Sound locations differ from registry IDs (see above).
- Reading other players' synchronized equipment for rendering is an ADR
  interpretation that Root should confirm.
