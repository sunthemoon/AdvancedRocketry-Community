# W74: the intermittent six-host natural-beds GameTest

Implementer: a headless Claude session (no subagents). Root: Claude. Task:
[TASK-W74](../TASK-W74-NATURAL-BEDS-FLAKE.md). Branch `codex/v1.8.0-w74-natural-beds-flake` (local only), based
on the shared branch at `937c5161`. Dates: 2026-10-15 and 16. Nothing was pushed or merged.

## Summary

- **Cause.** The six-room fixture reported ready one tick too early, and the real wait that followed was chunk
  work counted in ticks.
  - `placePreparedVent` ticks each new vent once by hand. The atmosphere service therefore tracked, scanned
    and supplied all six rooms before any vent's own block entity had ticked.
  - Vanilla ticks a block entity only in a block-ticking chunk whose entity sections have loaded
    (`LevelChunk.isTicking`). A newly forced chunk loads them only after its neighbouring chunks are ready,
    which here means generating fresh terrain asynchronously.
  - At tick 2 the service dropped every vent that had not ticked by itself, and the rooms went back to vacuum.
    The matrix had already run its first Moon step at tick 1. Its all-six gate then waited for the slowest
    Level's chunks before every later step.
  - That wait is worker-thread terrain generation in five noise Levels. The pacer turns it into ticks at 20 per
    second. On the hosted run it took about 40 ticks, which left too few of the 60 for the remaining 23 steps.
- **Reproduced.** With the GameTest JVM pinned to two logical CPUs, the original code failed exactly like the
  hosted run ("Didn't succeed or fail within 60 ticks").
- **Fix.** Four test-only commits, with no production change:
  1. readiness requires a live room chunk;
  2. each matrix step waits only for its own host;
  3. the fixture finishes the chunk promotion during setup, as its synchronous `getChunk` already does;
  4. readiness ignores the atmosphere state left by the manual vent ticks.
- **Result.** On two pinned CPUs the final code finished the matrix at tick 27 of 60, where the original timed
  out. At `86e08b60` the whole unit suite passed, all 1,094 required GameTests passed, and the log checker
  passed on the whole log.

## Cause in detail

`Fixture` builds all six rooms in tick 0: a forced chunk, a sealed iron room, a prepared vent and a bed.
`ready()` then needed every room supplied (Tau Ceti f: breathable ambient air). The diagnostic runs below
logged every waiting tick. In runs 1 to 3, tick 1 was "ready" and tick 2 was not:

- At tick 0, `force()` had loaded each room chunk synchronously, but no room chunk had its entity sections
  loaded yet (`ServerLevel.areEntitiesLoaded` false for all six).
- `placePreparedVent` calls `OxygenVentBlockEntity.serverTick` once. That observation counts for the
  atmosphere tick at the end of tick 0, so the rooms were scanned and supplied, and tick 1 saw six ready rooms.
- `LevelChunk.isTicking` (1.20.1) needs `FullChunkStatus.BLOCK_TICKING` and `areEntitiesLoaded`. The vents'
  own block entities therefore did not tick. At the end of tick 1, `pruneUnobservedVents` dropped them
  (`trackedVents=0`), and the rooms went back to vacuum.
- Each vent came back exactly one tick after its chunk's entity sections loaded (run 2: Tau Ceti f at tick 33,
  Moon and space at 35, Venus at 36, Mars and Tau Ceti g at 37).
- The entity sections load only after the forced chunk's promotion. That promotion needs the neighbouring chunks
  generated, which is seconds of worker time in five noise Levels on a slow host. The pacer gives each waiting
  tick 50 ms, so the wait costs ticks in proportion to the host's speed.
- Run 2 shows how the steps fit the budget. The first Moon step ran at tick 1 on the false readiness. The
  remaining 23 steps had to wait until tick 39 and reached host 5's second step at tick 59.
- In the hosted log the shape is the same. The first Moon sleep ("Sweet Dreams") is at 10:29:26.15 and the
  Moon's last step at 10:29:28.35, about 40 paced ticks later. The space, Mars and Venus hosts then take
  4 ticks each, and the 60 ticks ran out at Tau Ceti f.

Locally on all 12 CPUs (run 1) the same wait was 17 ticks, so the test passed with room to spare. That is
why it failed only on a busy or small host.

## Choices

- **The synchronous setup wait.** The task suggested waiting for each host's chunks through the pacer.
  Run 3 shows why a paced wait alone cannot fit:
  - the terrain work takes 27 to 32 paced ticks on two CPUs (and about 40 on the hosted run), and the
    24 steps need 24 more;
  - the per-host gate overlaps nothing when all six Levels finish together.

  The work is host speed, not product behaviour, which is the 2026-10-10 rationale for the pacer. So the
  fixture now finishes it the way it already loads each room chunk, synchronously in tick 0, with the same
  main-thread task pumping that vanilla's synchronous `getChunk` uses.
  - The wait is bounded at 30 s, half the GameTest server's 60 s watchdog, and fails with each room's status.
  - The remaining asynchronous step, the entity-section load, still costs paced ticks; readiness waits for it.
  - The tick budget, the tick-50 guard and every assertion are unchanged. The test's cost is not hidden:
    tick 0 now visibly takes longer (3.2 to 3.3 s on two CPUs in runs 5 and 6, against 1.2 to 1.7 s in
    runs 1 to 3).
- **The per-host gate is a structure change.** It changes no assertion; every bed call still needs its own
  room live and breathable. I kept it because in run 5 the entity sections still loaded about 10 ticks apart,
  and the gate saved those ticks. The other three tests keep the all-room gate.
- The readiness failure message changed from "The six bed fixtures did not become breathable" to "The bed
  fixtures did not become live and breathable". The status string now leads with `ticking=` and `live=`.

## Not done

- `FirstMoonLandingGameTests` line 95 waits at most 100 unpaced ticks for a fresh Moon chunk to become
  entity-ticking. It failed in all four two-CPU runs (2, 3, 5 and 6) and passed in the 12-CPU runs. It is the same
  2026-10-10 pattern, outside this task's scope, and not changed.
- Other GameTests that use `placePreparedVent` and then wait for a supplied room (for example in
  `SleepRespawnGameTests`) may see the same one-tick false readiness. I did not examine them.

## Known risks

- Tick 0 of the four natural-bed tests now blocks the server thread until the room chunks block-tick: 3.2 to
  3.3 s on two pinned CPUs. A far slower host could reach the 30 s bound, and the test then fails with each room's
  status rather than timing out.
- `Room.live()` copies vanilla 1.20.1's `LevelChunk.isTicking` condition. A Forge or Minecraft update that
  changes it would need the same change here.
- The entity-section load after setup is still asynchronous. Runs 5 and 6 needed up to 11 paced ticks on two CPUs,
  and the per-host gate absorbs most of that.
