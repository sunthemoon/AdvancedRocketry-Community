## Files and design decisions

The write scope is the solar package, its new client screen and JEI entry, its data generators and tests, its registry/language/tag entries, generated v1.8 resources, log expectations and this record. The added pickaxe/stone-tool tag entries are in `V180MaterialData`; no existing entries were changed.

The fixed 3-wide, 22-deep layout uses the v1.2 kernel's matchers, transforms, footprint index and fair dirty queue. Its 66 cells do not fit the kernel's 16-cell axis limit, so no shared schema limit was widened. A Level capability owns the runtime, with at most 16,384 controllers, 32 validations and 8,192 charged cells per tick. The panel/casing tag is data-driven. Revalidation is event-driven, not a per-tick footprint scan. Two facade identity checks per active tick detect revoked plugs.

The two genuine `power_output_plug` blocks contain no energy or saved binding. They share the controller's 1,000 FE/tick push/pull quota. Reentrant pulls cannot spend energy during push callbacks, and old formation facades cannot regain authority. When both structures are dirty, queue order determines the first valid claim; an already live claim refuses the other controller. There is no persisted placement priority.

The array reuses `SolarExposure.read`, `SolarGeneration.tick` and `SolarSave` rather than introducing another irradiance function or buffer calculation. Its buffer is 20,000 FE, and the maximum requested credit is 8,064 FE/tick before remaining room. The UP exposure face is invariant under the four horizontal rotations. Countdown generation continues; the synchronous warp relocation is observed on the next server tick, with no added ten-second penalty.

Existing `machines.solarGeneratorEnabled` and `energy.solarGeneratorMultiplier` govern both solar profiles. No configuration key, configuration-count change or network channel was added. The menu uses nine bounded, signed-short-safe scalars and a distinct schema-1 opening marker; it has no slots or mutation actions. Array-specific JEI information supplements the ordinary crafting recipes.

The independent `arce_solar_array` schema-1 root stores only FE. Bounded unsupported roots are retained verbatim and stop ordinary operation/removal. Non-admissible roots retain their original Tag reference and join the documented sticky whole-chunk guard, including observed denial before serialization. Ordinary supported removal destroys FE and drops no energy carrier. The earlier design/provenance/protection documentation precedes the implementation.
