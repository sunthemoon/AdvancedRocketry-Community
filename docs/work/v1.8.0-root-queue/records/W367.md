## Key design decisions

The corrected R74/R76 fixture has one 4,000 mB rocket and a 1,000 mB unloader. Its historical 5,000 mB
is inside the stale journal snapshot, not another physical tank. The completed unload has no remaining
escrow operation or named floor. A protected source epoch alone can cause FLOOR_CONFLICT.
The existing settlement cannot select this case; floor retirement also requires different authority
and absence conditions. A maintenance start does not bypass those checks.

The proposed repair is a narrow offline planner/copier. Only after whole-world validation and operator
digest confirmation does it remove the exact stale PREPARED transfer in a complete candidate copy.
Ledger, entity flight, cargo, propulsion, epochs and terrain remain unchanged offline. No resource,
floor, operation, account row or receipt is reconstructed. Original files and the whole backup remain
intact. The proposal requires two actual save/restart cycles, fresh proofs, independent native/file
checks and ordinary unload/launch verification before whole-world replacement.

Root 3 is not activated at the inspected head. Its future retired-transfer case also need not retain
an account row. The proposal preserves both ledger forms and records signed, operator-confirmed values
or historical UNKNOWN in an external repair audit, without fabricating a missing ledger baseline.
Related live authority is refused rather than generalized into another repair mode.

These are recommendations, not accepted ADR changes. Existing ADR-065 decisions, R-035 accounting
holds, admission switches, source code, test assertions, limits and historical evidence are unchanged.
