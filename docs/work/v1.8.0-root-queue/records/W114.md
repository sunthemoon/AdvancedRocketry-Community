### Diagnostic and side-effect correction

For cargo with retained markers that passes the earlier `exclusive` checks,
`OTHER_TRANSFER_PENDING` precedes the whole `fillRefusal` call, not just its
`ROCKET_LIMIT` result. It also masks a `STALE` or `ESCROW_FULL` result from that call.
The earlier stale-epoch, receipt/READY-head and same-rocket exclusions keep their
priority; fuel's refusal ordering is unchanged. This describes the existing R40
ordering, not another behavior change in W114F.

Here, "no side effects" means no sequence, reservation, queue, writer or endpoint
change. `prepareTransfer` may still record the transient fairness/turn hint for
`OTHER_TRANSFER_PENDING`; it does not imply literally zero transient bookkeeping.
