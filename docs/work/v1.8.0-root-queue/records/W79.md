## Findings that shape the design

1. **No Fluid cargo exists today.** The default payload hosts are chest/barrel and the satellite bay; any other
   block entity rejects assembly, so a rocket with a `pressurized_tank` cannot be assembled (at `646bf9ee`,
   `rocket/forge/ServerLevelRocketScanWorld.java:80`). W11c must add the tank payload host that ADR-065
   section 4.1 names.
2. **The escrow core is fuel-only.** The operation kinds are only `FLUID_PROPULSION_FILL` and
   `TRANSFER_FLOOR_RETIRE`; the binding, validation and floors assume the rocket is the destination; and
   `ROCKET_COMPOSITE_V1`, the projection that covers the snapshot content hash, is refused in the binding, floors
   and READY heads. Operator settlement accepts fill members only. Cargo therefore needs a core leaf (C-K)
   before any device.
3. **Transfer identity breaks on the first cargo operation.** A landed rocket is its COMMITTED record's
   authority only while its snapshot content hash equals the record's (`RocketTransferEntities.java:252-261`).
   The record stays until disassembly, so one unload at a destination would block further fuel and cargo and
   push recovery into FLOOR_CONFLICT. The note proposes an identity rule (leaf C-I) that also accepts an equal
   structure digest for an escrow-bound rocket with a nonzero epoch.
4. **Receipt-backed rockets and legacy scalar-fuel rockets** cannot take a cargo operation safely with the
   current head and signature rules; the note recommends refusing them (Q2, Q3).

## Choices made where the ADRs and W11b3 leave room

All are proposals for Root, listed in the note's section 7:
- the cargo frontier uses design 2's existing `ROCKET_COMPOSITE_V1` formula rather than a new projection;
- one live cargo operation per rocket, with no chains and no fuel/cargo or load/unload mixing (Q4);
- deterministic, server-side payload selection in snapshot order, with no new packet (Q5);
- no after snapshot is kept between capture and apply; apply and reconciliation recompute it;
- item devices spill their buffer instead of using a drop carrier (Q7); automation is directional (Q8);
- only vanilla chest, trapped chest and barrel payloads (27 slots) are item cargo; `LootTable` payloads and
  modded chest subclasses refuse (Q9);
- a new closed-dictionary receipt field `payload_kind` that fuel receipts omit (Q10);
- one admission switch per kind (Q11).
