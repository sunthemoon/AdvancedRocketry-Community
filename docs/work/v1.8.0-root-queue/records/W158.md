## Step 0 disposition: initially BLOCKED; superseded by R104

The frozen restart contract cannot yet be bound to the merged sidecar without
a design decision. This is a static source finding, not a reproduced native
crash or a claim that the already-disabled release loses player data.

### B1's recorded slots are unavailable after the permitted H1 removal

W80 section 3 requires B1 to prove every recorded entity slot: the authority
slot, T's source slot and T's destination slot, deduplicated to at most three.
Its restart paragraph requires B1 to be reacquired. J1 explicitly removes T
when H1's remaining passengers are online; C5 explicitly permits a restart
after that removal is installed and before S2. The unfinished sidecar must
then complete without T.

The merged `RocketRejoinRoot.Batch` (lines 103-121) persists the authority's
dimension, pad and region, resource hashes, and before/scrubbed versions.
`RocketRejoinCodec.batch` (lines 50-71) encodes exactly those fields. Neither
stores the source/destination slot census or the transfer snapshots. A
version contains only checksum, phase and destination entity UUID. Hashes and
UUIDs cannot supply the missing destination dimension and chunk coordinates.
For H1, the stationary authority identifies its source slot, not its old
destination slot.

`RocketTransferSavedData.remove` deletes T, and `encode` serializes only the
remaining entries plus the outbox. W155 does not supply a durable history:
`RocketJournalLeases` keeps session-local mutation sequences, prunes removed
record objects after acknowledgement, and serializes neither. The checkpoint
acknowledgement and native barrier watches are also session-local. Thus C5
loses the inputs needed to reacquire the prescribed B1 proof. The "gone"
outcome cannot assume that an unknown former slot was examined either.

Checking only the authority slot weakens B1; keeping the fence forever fails
the specified C5 continuation; retaining T until after S2 changes J1's order;
adding a durable slot census changes the frozen batch contract. None was
silently selected. Root needs to decide how the recorded slot census survives
J1, preferably as bounded data in the existing sidecar, and specify the
schema-1 compatibility treatment before implementation resumes. No second
saved store or journal-root change is proposed.

### Other merged-code differences to carry into that decision

- **H1 readiness has changed.** W80 describes `allPassengersOnline` on MT.
  `RocketTransferRecoveryService` now uses `RocketOrdinaryRetirement` and
  `RocketRetirementGuards.crewReady`. The pure predicate in
  `RocketRetirementProof` requires placement (connected, alive, in the
  survivor's Level, mounted on it or unmounted), or remembered delivery in
  this return. A later logout does not revoke remembered delivery. Merely
  being online is no longer the source-removal predicate. W80's J1
  all-online wording must be reconciled with this stronger existing guard;
  it must not bypass an online but unplaced remaining rider. MT still
  supplies the source seats. H2 still tests the live landed ME.
- **Native proof is available, but not a persisted proof.** `EscrowChunkStore`
  retains the entity-store future; `EscrowBarrier` forces that worker,
  performs stable readback, and validates generation watches. `beginJoint`
  combines endpoints in one slot, not across Levels; a coordinator must
  retain and revalidate the individual slot proofs through S2. The later
  `beginOrdinary` API shares the global budget with an ordinary sub-quota
  and supplies no governed frontier. Its capture-support and shared-slot
  exclusion checks must be preserved, not bypassed with invented endpoints.
- **The manifest is serialized.** `RocketEntity.addAdditionalSaveData`
  encodes the actual `flightData`; that codec includes the passenger
  manifest. B0 can validate that image rather than infer it from a Level
  save. No new injection is needed for the existing capture mechanism.
- **J1's narrow CAS is still new work.** Ordinary `put` rejects changed
  checksums. Existing `migrateCommitted` and `replace` are not passenger-only
  CAS operations. The new operation must preserve that invariant, outbox
  and governed records, and notify both L and T mutation histories.
- **W123/F1/W155 remain usable.** W123 supplies checked replacement and
  exact-candidate exception-time readback. F1 retains an L/T absence lease
  through actual-serial capture. W155 permanently invalidates retained
  leases on intervening L/T mutations, even unsampled ones. J1 must acquire
  fresh authority after its own mutation, never revive a pre-J1 lease.
- **Startup ordering needs a release binding.** W157 validates before world
  start and installs its cache at `ServerStartingEvent`; it adds no rocket
  entity-join filter. The release leaf must classify before the first
  rocket join, not only before ordinary `ServerStarted` recovery.
