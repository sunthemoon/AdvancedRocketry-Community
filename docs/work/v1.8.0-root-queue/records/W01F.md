The worktree started clean at `83fcf0ba` on `codex/v1.8.0-hatch-kernel`.
The complete assigned review was read and its SHA-256 matched the TASK.
The write scope is the W01 runtime, its tests, the R-021 classic-root extension,
the labelled ADR-064 section 11 implementation note, and this record. No other
machine, GuardedChunkSaves implementation, status file, ledger, build script,
upstream asset, or other worktree will change. No agent will be dispatched and
no commit will be pushed or merged.

Missing hatch roots become empty, unbound instances. Missing required controller
roots become fresh, unformed checkpoints, provided every present retained root
can be written unchanged. Present oversized, structurally unbounded or
nonrepresentable roots still use the existing sticky whole-chunk refusal; bounded
unsupported roots remain quarantined and are preserved verbatim. This does not
promise conservation of unrelated resources during the remaining refusal cases.
R-021 and ADR-064 will describe their duration, saturation, logs and offline repair.

Claims are retired only after loaded-only lookup establishes that the physical
controller position has no matching machine UUID. A matching unsupported owner
and an unloaded owner remain protected. Retirement clears binding/facade epochs,
not the plug's FE. Formation will reconcile generation skew only for an exact
loaded owner identity, without changing shared kernel binding policy or transferring
Item/Fluid ownership. Item-bank removal will spawn its drops at the controller.
Each finding has its own implementation/test commit. The final automated checks
are targeted classic JUnit, locked runData, locked GameTest and the log checker.
