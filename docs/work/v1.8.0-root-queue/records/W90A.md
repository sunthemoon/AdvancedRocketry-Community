**The checked column set (R21 item 2).** `rocket/transfer/RocketDiscoveryColumns` is server-free. From an origin x and z
and the snapshot bounds it computes the chunk columns from `(min - 1) >> 4` to `(max + 2) >> 4` with `addExact` and
`subtractExact`, and returns VALID, ARITHMETIC (any overflow) or OVER_BUDGET (more than `MAX_LANDING_CHUNKS`). It also
says whether the origin column lies inside. `admissible()` is VALID and containing the origin. The refresh set is the
rectangle of an admissible end, and the release set is the origin column always, plus that rectangle. Columns come in
one fixed order, x then z.

**Readiness.** `RocketTransferEntities.endAlreadyLoaded` keeps the predicate: VALID, and every rectangle column both
entity-loaded and entity-ticking, through the pinned `columnsAlreadyLoaded`. It answers as the pre-W90 formula did for
every snapshot; where the old `int` arithmetic would wrap, both answer false, and the new code never throws.

**Margin tickets (R21 item 1).** After a checked ACTIVE classification, and only when the record is not held,
`refreshGovernedFlightTickets` adds one `arce_rocket_flight` region ticket (distance 2, key T, the existing type and
400-tick lifespan) on each column of each recorded end's refresh set. That is at most 16 per end and 32 per transfer.
It adds nothing unless both Levels exist and both ends are admissible. The F2 callback's two origin tickets and the
legacy flights' origin tickets are unchanged; they are the same ticket as a margin ticket on the origin column.

**Release.** F8's `releaseFlightTickets` removes, in each end's own Level, that end's release set. A missing Level is
skipped and the other end is still released; removing an absent ticket does nothing.

**The `DISCOVERY_MARGIN` hold.** The dispatcher's ready supplier first checks both recorded ends. If either is not
admissible it raises `EscrowGovernedExecutorHolds.Reason.DISCOVERY_MARGIN` and answers not ready, so
`afterClassification` skips the executors and, seeing the hold, refreshes nothing. The existing WARN
`ARCE_GOVERNED_EXECUTOR_HOLD transfer=<T> reason=DISCOVERY_MARGIN` is written once per flight and reason per process.
The record keeps ARMED, its phase, its locks, its reservation and its slot until R-035's offline repair. The scope is
one transfer.

**Admission bounds (R21 item 3, R22 answer 4).** `prepareLaunch` computes the governed test (a ledger-bound escrow
section, the same test that chooses the governed F1 branch) after the destination Level check and before pad
selection.
- For a governed launch, `RocketGovernedAdmission` refuses a source that is not admissible with `LAUNCH_CHUNK_BUDGET`,
  and an admissible source that is not wholly loaded and entity-ticking with `LAUNCH_AREA_INACTIVE`. Both come before
  any terrain access, named-pad claim or F1 store.
- The surface selector checks each candidate's checked margin from its origin x and z after `candidate()` and before
  `loadChunks` and the surface read. The fixed pads are 64 blocks apart, so the first failure ends selection with
  `chunk_budget`.
- The station default pad checks the relocated snapshot after the region and station-region checks and before
  `loadChunks`. The named pad checks it after the block budget and before the bounds check, `getChunkNow` and block
  reads. Both answer `chunk_budget`. `recheck` keeps today's region count.
- The destination refusal travels as `LANDING_PAD_UNAVAILABLE` with detail `chunk_budget`. The source refusals are codes
  alone, shown on the action bar.
- Legacy launches keep today's region count and get no source check.

**Wait logs and inspect (R21 item 6).** `RocketGovernedWaits` holds bounded RAM state for each visited live record (at
most 64), dropped when the record leaves the journal and on restart.
- A visit that is checked ACTIVE and unheld is not ready when the readiness predicate fails (`LOADING`) or when it holds
  but the launch boundary refuses (`CENSUS`). The census uses the participants the executors pass: the live source in
  PREPARED, and the arrival from DESTINATION_SPAWNED on.
- After 400 ticks continuously not ready the dispatcher writes one INFO
  `ARCE_GOVERNED_ENDS_WAITING transfer= logical= phase= cause= source=k/n destination=k/n`, and when the ends are ready
  again one INFO `ARCE_GOVERNED_ENDS_READY transfer= waited=`.
- Executor holds, the HOLD and CLEANUP routes and bay claims end a wait silently; they keep their own logs. A gap of
  more than 64 ticks between visits (a blocked journal skips the dispatcher) restarts an unreported wait.
- `/arce rocket inspect <transfer>` appends `margin=source:k/n,destination:k/n wait=<NONE|LOADING|CENSUS|HOLD:reason>
  tickets=<n>`. `k/n` reads only the recorded rectangle with `areEntitiesLoaded` and `isPositionEntityTicking`, and
  `invalid` replaces it for an end that is not VALID. `tickets` is the number of distinct columns refreshed at the last
  visit, and 0 while held or not ACTIVE. The field adds no ticket and loads nothing.

**R22 amendment 5 (wording).** Loaded and entity-ticking together is the conservative sufficient readiness check;
neither predicate alone is enough. Two GameTests construct the vanilla states that show this (section 5).

## 4. Unit tests

- The column set at one chunk (the origin's), across one border (2), across both (4), and margin 63 to 66 (columns 3
  and 4); 1 x 16 and 4 x 4 (16 columns) against 1 x 17 and 5 x 4 (OVER_BUDGET); negative coordinates; the exact
  `max + 2` and `min - 1` boundaries; overflow at both ends (ARITHMETIC, never a throw); an outside-origin snapshot
  (VALID, not admissible, released by its origin only); 20,000 random regions (the readiness columns equal the refresh
  set, and the release set adds only the origin).
- The re-expressed predicate equals the pre-W90 formula on 4,000 random and 22 edge snapshots under four column
  patterns.
- Tickets with a recording sink that models ticket equality: 8 columns over two Levels and their release; overlapping
  ends sharing columns; a second transfer on shared columns keeps its tickets; repeated refreshes and releases are
  idempotent; an absent Level gets no calls, the other end is still released and nothing is refreshed; an invalid or
  outside-origin end gets no margin ticket and releases only its origin.
- `afterClassification`: an already-held record and a hold raised by the ready supplier both make zero refresh and
  zero executor calls over 1,000 visits each.
- Admission, with a recording Level allocated without world loading: the source codes and that readiness is not read
  for an inadmissible source; the governed surface selector stops at the first candidate with `chunk_budget` and no
  terrain read, while the legacy selector selects the same source; the station and named paths answer `chunk_budget`
  before any terrain read, and legacy still reaches its unchanged checks.
- Wait logs: one WAITING after 400 ticks and one READY, `CENSUS`, short waits silent, holds and storage gaps excluded,
  the 64-entry bound, tickets 0 when excluded, state dropped with the record.

## 7. Choices where the design was open

1. **Where the source readiness check runs.** Together with the source budget, after the destination Level check and
   before pad selection. R22 answer 4 asks for it before any warm-up capacity is spent; running it before selection
   also avoids terrain loads and a named-pad claim for a launch that F1 would refuse anyway.
2. **Refresh needs both ends.** A record whose either end is not admissible, or whose either Level is missing, gets no
   margin ticket at all, consistent with the hold, as the pre-W90 refresh needed both Levels.
3. **The inspect `tickets` count** is the number of distinct (Level, column) pairs refreshed, so shared columns of two
   ends in one Level count once.
4. **Wait-log rules.** READY is written only after a reported WAITING. A held visit skips the census. Holds, other
   routes and bay claims reset the wait without a line. A visit gap longer than 64 ticks restarts an unreported wait,
   so a storage pause is not reported as loading.
5. **Ungoverned records in inspect** show their margins with `wait=NONE tickets=0`, since only the governed dispatcher
   keeps that state; their two origin tickets are not counted.
6. **The border-crossing geometry.** Revision 2 suggests origins at x ≡ 15. The product's fixed Moon pads are at
   x ≡ 8 (mod 16), so a launch through the product entry cannot put a one-column rocket's origin there. A 14-block
   footprint gives 2 x 2 margins at both ends with origins at x ≡ 2, and the test keeps the real F1 and F2 census.
7. **The decoded records** go through an isolated `RocketTransferSavedData` save and load and are then put into the
   live journal, instead of a whole-journal reload, so that they can share an existing batch.
8. **Scope.** Three files outside the listed scope changed, each because an item requires it: the new hold reason and
   its read-only accessor in `EscrowGovernedExecutorHolds`, the inspection record that carries the inspect field, and
   new classes (`RocketDiscoveryColumns`, `RocketGovernedAdmission`, `RocketGovernedWaits`, the GameTest support).
9. **Class sizes (AGENTS.md section 3.4).** `RocketTransferService` is 800 lines (it was 798). `RocketTransferEntities`
   is 528 lines (it was 463); it keeps its one stated responsibility, bounded transfer entity lookup and chunk
   activation, so it is not split. `EscrowGovernedMarginGameTests` is 516 lines: three independent scenarios and their
   shared fixtures. None exceeds 800.
10. **R21 item 5.** No cap is added beyond item 1's structural bound. The measured reference case with border-crossing
    margins against docs/17 is not part of this leaf (section 8).

## 9. Known risks

- The margin tickets keep up to 32 columns per transfer entity-ticking, and up to 2,048 tickets at 64 transfers, in
  Levels without players. Their measured cost is still open (section 8).
- The wait diagnostics add one read-only boundary evaluation per unheld ready visit, at most 8 per tick.
- The flight GameTest reloads the shared checked journal. If a journal-writing test ever joins `machine_menu_channel`,
  the two would race.
- The pending-load GameTest drives the main-thread chunk executor for at most 2 seconds per attempt inside one tick,
  with at most five attempts.
- The hold GameTests leave their ARMED entries in the shared test ledger, as the existing startup fixtures do.
- Cleanup: each heavy run released my own lock. After the last run I removed `build/gametest/world` and my temporary
  scratch directory; the evidence directory keeps only logs and the checker output.
