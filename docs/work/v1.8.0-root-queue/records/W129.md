The proposal in one paragraph: each root gets one checked writer with its own IO thread. The server thread only
captures immutable record references (at most 20,480 for the registry and 256 for the journal) and later publishes
the exact outcome. At most one capture exists, and every attempt captures fresh. A mutation made while a capture is
in flight is stamped with that capture's epoch, the snapshot epoch that ADR-050 section 2 requires. E therefore
advances at any committed publication, and W119's results are unchanged whenever nothing mutates during a flight.
B14 gains WRITE_PENDING. Barriers become acknowledged effects, with a terminal lock while a launch or decommission is
pending. The stop path drains in `save(File)` on both the normal and the crash path.

## Choices where the contract was open

- (choice) **Snapshot epoch.** A mutation during a flight stamps the in-flight carried epoch. FAILED reserves its
  epoch only if the registry mutated during that flight. The original claim that every existing W119 expected value
  stays unchanged is superseded by Revision 2 below: only the no-mutation matrices do; the defensive proof test at
  lines 120-137 intentionally migrates to the snapshot-epoch rule (W129-R3).
- (choice) **"Success means durable"** holds for the terminal's data start, claim and cancel through a pending result.
  Launch and decommission lock their package and chip until the change is covered (question Q1).
- (choice) **The stop path** persists the final RAM state on both the normal and the crash path, as today's
  synchronous writers do, with at most three drain attempts.
- (choice) **The cadence and backoff** (10 and 200 ticks) are copied from the receipt writer (question Q5).
- (choice) **One shared writer core** for the two new roots; the three existing writers stay unchanged.

## Known risks

- The capture cost at the bounds is an estimate from the code, not a measurement. The ADR's measurement gate (item 13)
  decides.
- The concurrent heap of the registry, transaction and transfer journal writes at their bounds is unmeasured. The
  shared IO permit is named as the remedy but not adopted.
- The terminal lock is a new player-visible protection. On a failing disk it lasts until a commit or the end of the
  session.
- The design rests on S2 (process kill). Power loss on Windows, which has no directory force, stays outside it, as
  today.
- The ground-survey whole-root preflight (design section 2.4) still runs on the server thread at a full registry.
  This design does not change it.
- Under constant transaction-journal churn, D0 could wait for whole-journal currency without a limit. The journal is
  normally empty, and W123's rule is kept exactly.
