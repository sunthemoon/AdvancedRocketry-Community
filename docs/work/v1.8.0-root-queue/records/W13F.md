## Implementation and design choices

The fix is committed as `3a8d4ad92e0a5a1e8407981150c90183b322ce17`
(`fix(station): check named-pad rocket structure occupancy`). Initial selection
and pre-spawn recheck share the same snapshot-region overlap check. Overlap
refuses before a new claim or destination entity. A pre-spawn refusal releases
the existing claim and returns the undebited source through the existing code.

Discovery queries only accessible loaded entity sections, not terrain chunks or
the global entity list. The envelope is expanded by the existing maximum legal
axis span, clipped to station ownership and build/flight height. Assembly origins
can be edge blocks, so half-width expansion alone would be insufficient; the
conservative full origin reach is `MAX_BOUNDING_VOLUME - 1`. No chunk or ticket
is requested by admission, and the 16 landing chunks / 2,048 terrain inspections
remain unchanged. At most 64 rocket snapshots are inspected, with one additional
overflow sentinel; exceeding that count is an explicit translated refusal. A
nearby rocket with no readable snapshot also fails closed. These are local
implementation choices, not an ADR decision, save schema or protocol change.

Three GameTests reproduce the review geometry at station-local X=0..15,
Y=80..82, over the pad at (10,79,0). The parked rocket is scanned and actually
assembled, never flown; its small entity AABB does not overlap the arrival box.
Tests cover preparation refusal, the real pre-spawn executor, no extra claim or
entity, an accepted non-overlapping pad, exactly 64 non-overlapping occupants,
overflow refusal and a cold chunk remaining cold. Loading waits use
`GameTestTickPacer`. An owned test-only ticket makes the destination entity-ticking
before the executor check and is removed during fixture cleanup.
