## Remaining work, risks and Gates

The scoped acquisition implementation is finished, but not every v1.8.0
Required Gate is satisfied. Native GameTest/log validation is unverified, the
known packaging failure remains, and the two world-first criteria are outside
W19. Root still needs its ADR implementation note and integration review.

The graph proves existential acquisition, not quantities, elapsed time,
guaranteed random drops, hazard handling or balance. It assumes default-enabled
worldgen, repeatable research/FE, sufficient storage, loaded unprotected sources
and vanilla survival tools/enchanting/XP. Item enumeration follows direct
central `ModRegistries.register(modBus)` providers and their actual deferred
entries; a registration-boundary refactor must preserve or update that discovery.
Root should review this local commit, address its refmap fix and rerun the
remaining v1.8.0 native/integration validation under a permitted lock invocation.
