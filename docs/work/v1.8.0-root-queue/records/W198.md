reach a cut. Existing explicitly selected partial-terrain saves are experimental
cut inputs, not completion proofs. On restarts the observer only loads the
bounded census and observes production recovery; it does not resubmit disassembly.

and passenger manifest; compare U's complete snapshot and identities; compare

host exists in this inventory). No old S, exposed partial inventory, unaccounted
recipient, or usable D/terrain duplicate is permitted. The initial C0 distinction
is never used to tolerate S at an I6-completed cut.

recovery cut, then perform **two clean, unchanged restarts**. Thus each complete
row costs four starts, including `RECOVERY_LOADED` rows. Both clean observations
must have no retained U/carrier, exact elected resources/identity, and identical
authority. Fuel disposal is allowed only after block election; rollback keeps
the exact current fuel and debit history. Native inputs (T/U, terrain, entity,
POI and player data for the census), raw observations, commands, source/JAR
hashes and replay checks are retained, not inferred from runtime log labels.

Every row is executable under the integrated mapping; none is declared
impossible or folded away. SAVED/NEVER_SAVED variants have equivalent D1 *after*
successful I6 proof, but their distinct pre-request disk populations are the
reason both are still run. C0 has only SAVED in the supplied inventory.

unseal cuts remain independent rows.
