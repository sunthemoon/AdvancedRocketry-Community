- **Codecs.** Each has schema version 1 and the design's limits, checked before any recursive read:
  - `LoaderRoot`, the block-entity root under `arce_fluid_fuel_loader` (32 KiB, depth 20, 2,048 nodes):
    the owner (absent while unclaimed), the device UUID, the optional binding, the tank and the escrow
    section. `LoaderRoot.read` reports ABSENT, VALID, FUTURE, INVALID or UNREGISTERED_FLUID; only ABSENT
    and VALID are supported.
  - `LoaderTank`, the native tank: one Fluid, 0 to 16,000 mB, in Forge's own FluidStack envelope of at
    most 8 KiB. Decoding requires the registered canonical Fluid ID and an exact native round trip.
  - `LoaderBinding` (512 bytes, depth 8, 32 nodes): the logical rocket UUID, the Level ID (at most 128
    ASCII characters), the physical UUID and block position as a locator, and an optional station UUID.
  - `LinkerSelection` under `arce_linker` (1 KiB, depth 8, 32 nodes): one device UUID, its Level and
    its block position.
  - `LoaderCarrier` under `arce_fluid_fuel_loader_carrier` (12 KiB, depth 18, 1,024 nodes): the owner
    and the tank only. `LoaderCarrier.drop` keeps no identity, binding or marker and refuses an
    escrow-locked root.
  - `LoaderEscrowSection` under `resource_escrow` (256 bytes, depth 8, 128 nodes): the ledger, the
    resource epoch and at most one SOURCE marker, in the layout of `RocketEscrowSection` and the test
    endpoints. `state(signature)` gives the `EscrowPrefix.State` that escrow classifies.
- **Signature.** `LoaderTank.signature(device)` is the SHA-256 of `arce-escrow-fluid-tank-1:`, the device
  UUID, `:` and `RocketNativeResources.signature` of the tank's native envelope (W11A section 1). Every
  type it needs is on the shared branch, so it is built; `LoaderRoot.signature()` applies it to the
  root's device.
- **Bank selection.** `BankSelection.plan` keeps the banks that match the propulsion role, the current
  catalog, the rocket's captured allowlist and the tank's exact Fluid and native tag, and have positive
  room. One such bank gives READY with a plan; more than one gives `AMBIGUOUS_BANK`; none gives
  `BANK_FULL` when a compatible bank only lacks room, and `INCOMPATIBLE_FLUID` otherwise. An empty tank
  gives `EMPTY`. Abstract fuel and a bank with another Fluid or another tag are never compatible, so they
  are never overwritten.
- **Refill.** `BankSelection.step` is `min(1,000, local mB, bank room)` in integer mB. `RefillPacer`
  allows one attempt per device per 20 ticks with no catch-up burst.
- **Status.** `FluidFuelLoaderStatus` has the seventeen refusal members and READY, PREPARING and
  TRANSFERRING, in the design's order, with `refusal()` and the key
  `message.advancedrocketrycommunity.fluid_fuel_loader.<lowercase member>`. The text comes with the data
  leaf.

- The signature: pinned for a 1,000 mB water tank and for an empty tank against values computed
  independently in Python from the NC1 framing, and shown to change with the device, the amount, the tag
  and the Fluid.
- Bank selection: the unique bank; exactly 1, 749 and 1,000 mB (a bank at 1,251 of 2,000 mB and a

  overwrite of another tag, of no tag against an empty tag, or of abstract fuel; no side effects; integer

- The pacer: one attempt per 20 ticks over 1,000 ticks, no burst after a long gap, a restart that waits

## Choices where the design was open

- **Package.** All classes, the escrow section included, are in `rocket/fuel/loader`. The section uses the
  public `EscrowPrefix.Marker`, `EscrowPrefix.State` and `EscrowLimits.BE_MARKER_BYTES`. `EscrowTags` is
  package-private, so `LoaderNbt` repeats its strict reads; this avoids editing `resourceescrow`, which
  W11b2 changes heavily. `EscrowPrefix.Marker`, `EscrowPrefix.State` and `EscrowLimits` are unchanged on
  the W11b2 branch at `4b4fa530`.
- **"Tank root".** I read the TASK's "tank root" as the loader's block-entity root, whose limits the design
  states. The tank inside it is Forge's FluidStack envelope with no schema of its own; its depth and node
  limits (17 and 1,021) are the carrier's less the carrier's own framing, so every valid tank fits a
  carrier and a root. A test pins this.
- **Refusal order.** An integer schema above 1 is FUTURE before the size check, because a newer jar may
  allow other bounds. Then the envelope, then schema 1 exactly. A FUTURE binding or section makes its
  root FUTURE. A well-formed root naming an unregistered Fluid is UNREGISTERED_FLUID, so the device can
  revalidate when the Fluid's mod returns, as the design's R-035 text requires.
- **Strictness beyond the design.** The escrow section accepts only the SOURCE role, because the loader is
  only ever a fill source. Binding and selection positions must be inside vanilla's horizontal world limit
  and the Y range that `BlockPos` packs. A root's binding requires an owner. I added no other cross-field
  rule, such as "a marker requires a binding", so that no state the runtime can reach becomes INVALID.
- **Signature format.** The parts are joined as the existing fixture signature joins them
  (`arce-escrow-fixture-1:` + identity + `:` + value).
- **Bank compatibility.** It also requires the rocket's captured allowlist (`Settings.allows`), because
  `RocketPropulsionFuel` rejects a Fluid bank without it. Native tags compare exactly, so no tag and an
  empty tag differ, as Forge compares them. A compatible bank with zero capacity is `BANK_FULL`. The plan's
  rocket state is what `RocketFuelState.withFluidBank` would produce (revision plus one).
- **Pacing.** The pacer counts attempts, not only moves, which also bounds refusal work. It is not saved,
  because the design's root fields do not include it; instead a device that loads waits one full interval,
  so an attempt just before a stop and one just after the restart cannot fall within 20 ticks.

## Known risks

- An admitted operation carries the moved Fluid in a payload inside the 12,288-byte, depth-16, 512-node
  operation envelope (W11A section 1). The tank accepts native tags up to 8 KiB, depth 17 and 1,021 nodes,
  so a tank can hold a Fluid whose tag no operation can carry. B3-E must refuse such a fill with a status
  before capture, or B3-A's capability must refuse such intake. Root should choose.
- The pacer's restart rule assumes that game time does not go back across a restart. If it does, the
  pacer re-anchors and waits one more interval; it never bursts.
- The loader's escrow section lives outside `resourceescrow`. If W11b2 changes the marker layout before
  merging, this section must follow it.
- These classes are not yet called by anything, so no runtime path exercises them until B3-A and B3-E.
