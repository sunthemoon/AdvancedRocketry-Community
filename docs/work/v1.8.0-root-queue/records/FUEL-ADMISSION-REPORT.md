  scenarios; their historical failures remain failures.

**Root recommendation: PLACEHOLDER — PENDING ROOT REVIEW for fuel admission.**

The native-fluid block `advancedrocketrycommunity:fluid_fuel_loader` and its linker, local tank operations,
ownership/binding checks, retained-root/removal protection and R-044 native-save guard exist independently of new
escrow admission. Ordinary item-fuel loading and hand exchange remain separate, with their ordinary two-store

boundaries. Switch off is not a claim that all associated protections are inert in production.
