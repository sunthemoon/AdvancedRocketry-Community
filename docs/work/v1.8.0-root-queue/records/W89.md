## Cause and evidence

Running only `landing_pads,station_permission_matrix` on the starting source
reproduced the exact two failing cells and `cell_full_chunks=27`. Temporary
read-only diagnostics identified
`LandingPadGameTests.cancellationReleasesExactlyOneTransferClaim` as the owner
of the surviving `arce_rocket_flight` ticket at Space chunk `(66,64)`.

The recovery fixture occupied station cell `(0,0)` at tick 2 and completed at
tick 3. Meanwhile, the named-flight fixture took `(1,0)` and the cancellation
fixture took `(1,1)`. Deleting their station records frees those cells immediately:
the matrix's two permanent fixtures then take `(0,0)` and `(1,0)`, and both admin
create/delete actions reuse `(1,1)`. This is intended allocator behavior, now
also covered by a unit test; no allocation change is needed.

The cancellation fixture removed its journal record and its own pad tickets at
tick 4, but did not release the flight ticket. At matrix tick 399, that ticket's
400-tick timeout had not elapsed. It retained 25 full chunks, `x=64..68` and
`z=62..66`, inside the reused region. The matrix created only two additional
full chunks: its four platform chunks overlap two of those 25. Thus the measured
27 is **25 retained + 2 newly loaded**, not 27 chunks created by the command.
There were no forced chunks or players in that station region. The recovery
and named-flight fixtures also retained their own flight tickets after finishing.

The downloaded passing hosted run `39015130819`, source `809e5fdb`, shows the
recovery fixture completing before the second station allocation. Its six pad
allocations use `(0,0)` five times and then `(1,0)`, leaving `(1,1)` cold for the
matrix, which reports four full chunks. In the failing sequence the recovery
fixture waits for readiness and overlaps the other fixtures. The reordered run
therefore exposed a fixture lifetime dependency, not a station permission or
region-allocation defect. Neither the logout batch nor an escrow change is
needed to reproduce it in a fresh filtered world.

## Fix and tests

- `src/main/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/rocket/server/LandingPadGameTests.java`
  retains each fixture's transfer records independently of journal removal or
  reload. Normal cleanup and the existing pass/failure listener release the
  exact source and destination flight tickets using
  `RocketTransferEntities.releaseFlightTickets`. It does not clear other
  fixtures' tickets or forcibly unload chunks.
- `src/test/java/io/github/sunthemoon/advancedrocketrycommunity/station/model/StationRegistryModelTest.java`
  adds a regression proving that the three deleted pad cells, including `(1,1)`,
  are reused by subsequent matrix allocations.
- The temporary diagnostics were removed before the fix commit. The corrected
  diagnostic run observed zero full chunks and zero tickets in `(1,1)` before
  the operator action. Both admin cells then reported four full chunks.

No ADR decision, persistence format, network protocol, assertion, timeout,
batch name, or performance budget was changed or exempted.
