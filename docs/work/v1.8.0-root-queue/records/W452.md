## The schedule driver

`soak` runs the C4 schedule in sampled seconds: by default a 120-second warm-up that is discarded
after every start, 14,400 sampled seconds, a flushed save every 1,800, and one orderly restart at
7,200. Any other value needs `--qualification`. Vanilla autosave is left on; the driver never sends
`save-off`. Restart downtime is not sampled time. On this PC each segment is one call and one lock
hold; `--segment all` runs both in one process where no machine lock exists.

- **Samplers.** K15's process reader gives RSS and CPU each second. K15's `GC.heap_info` gives the
  heap every 30 seconds, and `-Xlog:gc*` gives the pauses. K15's forced-chunk query runs with each
  component sample, and any ticket outside the declared set is listed as unexpected. With the
  release-test property, K15's raw ticks are polled each second and merged per server lifetime.
- **Events.** Every save, the restart, each action and each due client step is written with its UTC
  time. Each component sample records the lit rooms, the station count and the ACTIVE, READY and
  claimed mission counts.
- **Accounting.** The closed world is read before the first start and after every stop: per room the
  full and empty canisters, oxygen units, vent and generator energy and coal blocks; per pad the fuel
  cells and the loader root; every rocket's fuel; station, mission and satellite identities; the
  forced tickets; and a hash over every mod block-entity root. The reconciliation requires the
  canister shells of each room to be conserved and allows oxygen, coal and fuel cells only to fall.
- **Actions.** At 1/8 of the sampled time the console assembles the 2,048-block structure with the
  ordinary command `arce rocket assemble`. At 1/4 it opens and restores the boundaries of rooms 0 and
  5 with `setblock` on the trapdoor's `open` state. The two flights, the disassembly and the mission
  refill are client steps, unless the release-test property is set.

**An open point for Root: raw ticks need the release-test property.** K15's raw per-tick command
exists only with `-Dadvancedrocketrycommunity.releaseTestHooks=true` (W403). A08 and A09 say "no test
properties". Without the property the driver records `forge tps` 100-tick means only, so A09's mean
is observable and its P95, P99 and spike length are not. Either A09 runs with that one property and
the checklist header is changed for it, or its percentiles stay NOT_COVERED. I did not choose.

## Choices where the TASK was open

- **Footprint.** I read "footprint of at most 4,096 blocks" as the whole bounding box of a room,
  shell included. The scanned volume is then at most 2,744 cells, well inside the unchanged limit of
  4,096. A room with a 4,096-cell interior would sit exactly on two shipped limits (4,096 cells per
  volume, 65,536 indexed cells for 16 rooms), so I chose the smaller reading.
- **Forced room chunks.** The rooms are held by 16 vanilla force-load tickets, saved in the world and
  declared in the manifest. Without them the rooms run only while a player stands near them.
- **Trapdoor boundary.** From reading C6's event adapter, a console `setblock` of an ordinary wall
  block raises no break or place event, so it would not invalidate a room; a door-like block does. I
  did not test the ordinary-block case.
- **Two qualification runs.** The task asks for one. The run without the property is the one that
  matches A08's header. The run with the property shows that the pads fly and the samplers work.
