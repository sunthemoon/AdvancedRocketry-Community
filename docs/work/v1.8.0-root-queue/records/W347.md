### Registry (`SatelliteRegistryTickGameTests.coalescedAndAutosaveRequestsArePublishedOnLaterTicks`)

Evidence: W342's first full run, `W342/gt-full/latest.log` (SHA-256
`d81c7415c79144f3fa97727b30378488f7422c9c0316d5ccae2cd76939c91e12`).

- Progress line 4,200 (tick 84,000) is at line 18309, 08:58:39.268. The batch started at line 18314, 08:58:39.354. The
  failure is at line 18316, 08:58:39.729. Progress line 4,201 (tick 84,020) is at line 18318, 08:58:39.937.
- So the batch started one to three ticks after the autosave of tick 84,000, and the test failed at about tick 84,013.
  The autosave tick is not inside the failing window.
- In W342's second full run (`W342/gt-full-2/latest.log`, SHA-256
  `d02322374c0bfde532e6ee91fd5ae478cab7d3b5f906d75c5faedb6547102f6b`) the batch started after 4,194 progress lines, about
  120 ticks before an autosave, and passed.

What reaches the window instead:

- The registry writer starts at most one capture per 10 ticks (`CheckedRootWriter.CADENCE_TICKS`). A request made while
  a capture is in flight, or within the cadence, stays as the writer's single pending flag.
- In tick A = 84,000, the autosave's `DataStorage.save` requests a registry write, which starts at once.
- In the END phase of the same tick, `SatelliteManager.coalescedFlush` runs, because A is a multiple of 20 and at least
  100 ticks have passed. If a state change is pending, it requests again, and `coalescedFlushes` is counted in tick A.
  That request becomes the pending flag.
- The test reads `coalescedBefore` one to three ticks later, which already includes that count, and makes its change.
- The pending request starts at about A + 11 and captures the test's change. Its commit covers `linked` and settles
  `flushPending`. The test then sees the cover with no coalesced flush counted after its own read, and fails with "The
  commit was not requested by the coalesced flush".

Scratch run S1 (below) reproduced this. Variant R1 left a change pending at A − 10 and ran the test's body at A + 1:
the commit covered the change at A + 15 with `coalesced_before=2` and `coalesced_at_cover=2`, so the original assertion
fails. R2 ran the body at A + 1 with nothing pending, and R3 ran it at A − 120. Both kept the assertion.

The logs cannot show whether a state change was pending at tick 84,000 in W342's run, because no writer or flush line
names it. A suggestion for Root, not implemented: open this test's window only when the registry writer is idle (no
capture in flight and no pending request) and the next autosave falls after the window.

## Choices where the task was open

- **Window length.** The fault phase, from setup to placement, has no bound of its own except the measured budget, so
  the gate uses the whole 230-tick budget. An autosave after the fault is removed is harmless, so this is
  conservative, at a cost of up to 231 unmeasured ticks in about 3.9 % of runs of each test.
- **Timeout.** The wait has to advance server ticks, so it cannot happen inside one tick as W344's `managedBlock` wait
  does. The GameTest timeout therefore grows by exactly the wait's bound. The measured budget and its assertion stay at
  230 and 240.
- **Regression seam.** The fixture takes an optional `IntSupplier` clock, used only by the regression, so the window
  check runs on the real fixture without waiting thousands of ticks.
