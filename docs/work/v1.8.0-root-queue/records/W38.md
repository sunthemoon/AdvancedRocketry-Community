## Behaviour as built

- **Where it runs.** The listener acts on the logical server's main thread only.
- **When it leaves the event alone.** Any of these:
  - the event is canceled;
  - `environment.classicGravityEnabled` is false;
  - the entity has a living entity anywhere below it in its vehicle chain;
  - the applied multiplier is 1;
  - the distance is not finite and non-negative;
  - the multiplier is outside 0 to 4.
- **The multiplier.** It is the owned modifier, read exactly as `PaddedBoots` read it. A
  missing modifier, a wrong operation or a non-finite amount reads as 1. The complete gravity
  attribute is never used.
- **The result.** The distance is `distance * multiplier` in double precision, saturated at
  `Float.MAX_VALUE`. The damage multiplier is never changed, and no damage is applied.

## Tests

Assertions use a probe that records each watched entity's `LivingFallEvent` at `HIGHEST`
priority (the distance vanilla passed in) and at `LOWEST` priority, including canceled events
(the distance vanilla then uses). Health is raised to 100, so the health lost is the whole
damage. Where the case allows, each test also checks that zero or two scalings would have
produced a different damage.

| Test | Route | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `nativeMobLandingOnTheMoonIsScaledOnceAgainstTheOverworld` | real `travel` landings of cows | Overworld unchanged; Moon scaled once; with the switch off, an owned factor is ignored |
| `livingMountAndNestedRidersInheritOneScaledLanding` | real pig landing, cow rider, nested cow | the mount scales once; both riders inherit that one value |
| `minecartAndBoatPassengersScaleOnceWithTheirOwnFactor` | real minecart landing; boat via `Boat.checkFallDamage`'s arguments | passengers of non-living vehicles scale once with their own factor |
| `horseFamilyLandingPostsNoForgeFallEventAndStaysNative` | real horse landing with a cow rider | no Forge event; horse and rider each take the native, unscaled damage once |
| `slowFallingAndFallImmunityKeepTheirNativeReach` | full native ticks; then vanilla arguments | a slow-falling landing never reaches the event; the owned factor, not the complete attribute, is used; a `fall_damage_immune` chicken stops propagation |
| `controllerFixtureLeavesEveryUnscaledEventUnchanged` | direct listener calls | canceled, malformed, off-thread, switched-off, living-vehicle, out-of-bounds, wrong-operation and missing-modifier cases; boundary factors 0 and 4; no direct damage |
| `playerLandingOnTheMoonIsScaledOnceAgainstTheOverworld` | `ServerPlayer.doCheckFallDamage` per move | player Overworld against Moon; legacy player gravity with the switch off is not scaled |
| `stationPlayerKeepsItsOwnFactorOnFootAndInANonLivingVehicle` | vanilla arguments in a station | the station factor 0.35 applies once, on foot and in a minecart |
| `fieldPlayerRidingALivingMountInheritsTheMountsLanding` | real pig landing with the field player riding | the rider inherits the pig's landing, not the field's 0.5; in a minecart the field factor applies once |
| `paddedBootsStillCancelLowGravityLandings` | `doCheckFallDamage`; a minecart | boots still cancel, also with `lowGravityBoots`, and also for a passenger |

Notes on the routes:

- **Real falls.** Mob landings are real: native `travel` or `tick` from 41 blocks onto a stone
  block placed in the test's own column, found by a bounded search from y 180 to 270.
- **Players.** A player's server-side landing exists only through `handleMovePlayer`, which
  calls `ServerPlayer.doCheckFallDamage`. The tests call that method one block at a time.
  They do not emulate packet validation.
- **Vanilla arguments.** Three cases call `causeFallDamage` with the arguments vanilla
  passes:
  - a real boat landing breaks the boat and spills items into the Level;
  - a station pad's geometry is not a fixed fixture;
  - a slow-falling or chicken landing cannot produce a damaging fall.
- **Players and spawn immunity.** Players are connected survival players that join the test
  level and wait 65 ticks for vanilla's spawn immunity. A `FakePlayer` never ticks, so it
  never loses that immunity.

## Choices where the ADR or TASK was open

- **The once-only rule and player precedence.** See "For Root first" and the design note.
- **Priority.** The listener runs at normal priority, the same as `PaddedBoots`. Order
  between the two does not matter. A canceled event deals no damage either way, and
  `lowGravityBoots` reads the modifier, not the distance.
- **Bounds.** ADR-066 says "finite/clamped to existing body limits". The TASK says saturate at
  `Float.MAX_VALUE`, and the TASK wins. A multiplier outside the celestial model's 0 to 4 is
  treated as malformed and leaves the event unchanged.
- **Factor 1.** An exact factor of 1 returns before writing, so the event is untouched rather
  than rewritten with the same value.

## Known risks

- **Conservative fallbacks.** In these cases the listener falls back to zero scaling, the
  native behaviour:
  - a passenger whose fall comes from a non-living vehicle that itself rides a living entity
    (only possible with `/ride` or mods);
  - another mod calling `causeFallDamage` directly on a mounted entity.
- **Another mod's mount.** A modded living mount that propagates its raw distance without
  posting the event would leave its riders unscaled.
- **Observation for leaf A, not caused by W38.** Under slow falling, a mob at rest on the
  Moon hovers instead of descending, because slow falling's 0.00165 blocks per tick in Moon
  gravity is below vanilla's 0.003 motion dead zone. A player would likely do the same on the
  client. This is visible gameplay that Root may want to record.
