# v1.8.0 legacy content audit

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: **read-only** audit of every player-visible legacy
content unit for [v1.8 Classic Content Completion](../versions/V1.8.0-CLASSIC-CONTENT-COMPLETION.md)
(C14). No upstream source, configuration text or asset is copied into this
repository; the machine-readable results record identifiers, English display
names, line numbers and hashes only.

```yaml
upstream: https://github.com/Advanced-Rocketry/AdvancedRocketry
upstream_commit: c5cd5af62fc07cd4e0d24f06a16033f181c47c04
license: MIT
archive: AdvancedRocketry-c5cd5af62fc07cd4e0d24f06a16033f181c47c04.zip
archive_sha256: 40eb5d436a021238df080cd39c9d7422851ffe495e3ac656d56ee5b21dcca01d
java_files_equal_to_manifest: 510/510
assets_equal_to_manifest: 898/898
inventory_inputs: 685 files, each hash-checked by the generator
imports_into_repository: 0
```

The archive was unpacked into a Temp directory outside the repository. On
Windows the default path exceeded 260 characters for eleven JEI files; a shorter
directory name fixed it, and all 1,408 files then matched `legacy-manifest`.

## 3. Findings by system

### 3.1 Materials and ores

- Advanced Rocketry registers only two materials itself (`TitaniumAluminide`,
  `TitaniumIridium`, `AdvancedRocketry.java` 897–898). Titanium, aluminum,
  steel, copper, tin, silicon, iridium, dilithium, rutile, carbon and thermite
  come from the LibVulpes `MaterialRegistry`; their ingots, plates, sticks,
  sheets, gears, coils, dusts, boules and blocks are LibVulpes items.
- Recipes refer to products through ore-dictionary names (`plateSteel` 20 times,
  `coilCopper` 36 times in Java, `stickIron`, `sheetTitanium`, …). The modern
  replacement is Forge common tags (ADR-061 §2).
- Ore generation (`world/ore/OreGenerator`, `CustomizableOreGen`) is driven by
  20 configuration keys (per-chunk and per-clump counts for copper, tin, rutile,
  aluminum, iridium and dilithium, with a separate Moon dilithium count).
- The small plate press (`BlockSmallPlatePress`) is itself a downward piston:
  powered by redstone, with obsidian two blocks below, it turns the metal block
  (or ore) directly below into plates (or dust). It is the early plate source
  before the rolling machine.
- Modern state: no metal material exists. Existing v0.x–v1.7 recipes use vanilla
  ingredients only, so v1.8 rebalances them (C16d) without rewriting held items.

### 3.2 Planetary surfaces and biomes

- The legacy Moon uses `moonTurf`/`moonTurf_dark` over stone in the "Regolith
  Highlands" and "Regolith Lowlands" biomes, with craters. The modern Moon is a
  flat end-stone Level; Mars and Venus (v1.4) use vanilla blocks.
- Legacy biomes appear on randomly generated planets of six named stars
  (`DimensionManager`); random generation is not revived (ADR-031). The C15c
  batch attaches the six exotic biomes to fixed, data-driven worlds instead.
- Flora: lightwood log, leaves, sapling and planks (alien forest), charcoal log
  (volcanic), electric mushroom (stormland, can spawn lightning client-side),
  six crystal colours (crystal chasms), geodes with configurable ores.
- `MapGenSpaceVillage` places vanilla villages on habitable planets;
  `MapGenSpaceStation` is never referenced; `BiomeGenPumpkin` and
  `BiomeGenWatermelon` are never registered.

### 3.3 Processing machines and fluids

- Legacy machines are LibVulpes multiblocks: electric arc furnace (blast brick
  shell), lathe, cutting machine (saw blade; also the sawmill), crystallizer
  (quartz crucible; refuses to run above a gravity threshold,
  `crystalliserMaximumGravity`), chemical reactor, precision laser etcher,
  centrifuge (enriched lava → metals), with item, fluid and energy hatches.
  The electrolyser, rolling machine and precision assembler already exist on
  the v1.2 kernel.
- 157 recipe JSON files plus machine recipes registered in code
  (`RecipeHandler`, `registerAllMachineRecipes`) give the values; they are
  reference data (ADR-061 §4, `REGENERATE`).
- The fluid pump searches a 64-block-radius volume breadth-first in one tick
  whenever its cache is empty, scans downward through air without a bound,
  and calls `getBlockState` on positions that may be
  unloaded; the modern pump needs a per-tick budget and loaded-chunk checks.

### 3.4 Rocketry

- Engine tiers: monopropellant, bipropellant (fuel plus oxidizer), advanced
  versions of both, nuclear thermal (working fluid heated by a fission core).
  Tanks match each fuel. Fuel and oxidizer lists are configuration keys.
- The loader block has seven variants: data bus, satellite bay, item unloader
  and loader, fluid unloader and loader, guidance computer access hatch.
- The rocket monitoring station shows rocket state and can launch a linked
  rocket by redstone.
- Destinations come from planet and station ID chips placed in the guidance
  computer; modern rockets choose destinations on the flight star map
  (ADR-035).
- When a legacy rocket lands on a liquid other than water (such as lava), it
  places iron landing floats under its footprint. Water is a safe landing
  without floats: the rocket settles below the water surface. The float block
  has no recipe. The modern landing-site rule (ADR-033) needs solid support
  and skips liquid surfaces.

### 3.5 Stations

- Station control blocks: warp controller (screen), planet selector and
  holographic planet selector (UI entities), gravity controller, altitude
  controller (orbit distance), orientation controller (rotation, which sets the
  light the station receives), landing pad ("docking pad"), docking port and
  station light.
- Modern stations are controlled by commands (ADR-046); the warp, gravity and
  sky contracts already exist (ADR-041, ADR-044, ADR-047).

### 3.6 Life support and equipment

- CO2 scrubber with carbon cartridges, gas charge pad (refills suit tanks),
  atmosphere detector (redstone output), airlock door, pipe seal, torches that
  go out without oxygen (`torchBlocks`, `dropExtinguishedTorches`), thermite
  torch, seal detector, atmosphere analyzer.
- Suit equipment: four pressure tank sizes, a jetpack, six upgrades (hover,
  flight speed, bionic legs, padded landing boots, anti-fog visor, earthbright
  visor) installed at the suit workstation, the space breathing enchantment.
- The basic laser gun reaches 50 blocks, damages entities by 1 point, and keeps
  a per-player position map (an instance `WeakHashMap` on the item
  singleton); the modern tool needs break events, the
  protection chain and no static state.
- The force field projector places field blocks up to 32 blocks ahead into any
  replaceable block without place events or protection checks.

### 3.7 Research and satellites

- Optical, composition and mass satellites produce three data kinds; the data
  bus and wireless transceiver move data between machines. Modern research is a
  per-player point balance (v0.8) and discovery is shared (ADR-037).
- The observatory and astrobody data processor identify asteroids; modern
  asteroids are logical instances created by survey missions (ADR-051).
- The spy telescope satellite class exists but is never registered.

### 3.8 Presentation assets

- 43 OBJ and 20 MTL models (machines, engines, tanks, jetpack, laser gun,
  hovercraft, space elevator, warp core, terraformer).
- 17 sounds: five sound events (`crystallizer`, `cuttingMachine`,
  `electricArcFurnace`, `electrolyser`, `precAss`) point to files that equal
  the silent placeholder `dummy.ogg` (SHA-256 `f33cbdac…`); `lathe` and
  `rollingMachine` are the same file. Only `ElectricShockSmall` is registered
  in the Forge registry (`AudioRegistry` 31–36); the other fourteen are
  unregistered `SoundEvent` objects, and `buttonBlipA` exists only in
  `sounds.json`. No OGG has a Vorbis comment naming its author.
- 12 low-orbit planet images (`textures/planets/*leo.jpg`, painted or
  procedural, in a format the derivation check cannot read) and
  `atmosphereleo.png`,
  added in 2017 and downsized in 2021, carry no source note.
- Nine language files; only `en_US` and `zh_CN` were reviewed (v0.1.0). The
  others are community translations and need their own provenance.

### 3.9 Asset license findings

- A file name says nothing about origin. `tools/audit/vanilla_derivation.py`
  compares all 898 legacy assets with the vanilla 1.12.2 and 1.20.1 client
  assets (ADR-061 §4.8); the results are in
  [`v1.8.0-vanilla-derivation.json`](v1.8.0-vanilla-derivation.json): 799
  `CLEAR`, 45 `HIT`, 42 `SUSPECT`, 12 `UNSUPPORTED` (the JPEG planet images).
- Confirmed derivations (`HIT`) include the Moon turf, dark Moon turf and
  ferric sand (recolours of the 1.12.2 grass top, rank ≥ 0.995), the charcoal
  and lightwood logs and leaves (oak, big oak and acacia), the four plate
  press faces (piston and furnace), the quartz crucible block and item
  (cauldron), the carbon brick (iron ingot), the earth phase sheet (95 %
  of the vanilla moon phase pixels), the station light and holographic lamp
  (the sun), the iron armor layers (identical), the space-suit leggings and
  chestplate layer, and the arc furnace, crystallizer, cutting machine,
  precision assembler, laser drill and container GUI sheets (vanilla furnace,
  crafting table, shulker box and smithing frames). Two model JSON files are
  byte-identical to vanilla models (`orientablebottom.json`,
  `unlittorch.json`).
- The plate press faces are also a third-party contribution: issue #1527's
  author writes that they ran the vanilla piston through filters; the
  textures entered the repository in the `Asset updates (#1976)` commit.
- Files that only share a vanilla name (`env/sun.png`, `planets/shadow.png`,
  `planets/lava.png`, `gui/eyecandy/earth.png`, `blocks/forcefield.png`,
  `blocks/beacon.png`, `models/beacon.png`, the enriched-lava fluid sheets) are
  `REVIEW`, not presumed either way; `textures/font.png` has no vanilla twin
  and no consumer and is excluded for that reason.
- AR resources reference 36 distinct LibVulpes resource locations (machine
  casing 79 times, structure machine 40, dilithium ore 11, product templates,
  hatches). LibVulpes branch `1.12` at `c2ca79dc18625c9e63a191a795f1f07d078f29f0`
  carries an MIT `LICENSE` (`Copyright (c) 2017`, SHA-256 `1f9978a4…55be3`),
  added on 2017-10-01. Its commit `984d6747` took the casing, hatch, battery
  and plug textures "from the unmerged Cl1ff PR", and AR's own
  `textures/blocks/machinegeneric.png` is byte-identical to that casing
  texture; AR's `models/item/models/motor.obj` equals the LibVulpes motor
  model. ADR-061 §4.1 therefore does not approve LibVulpes and puts both AR
  files under `REVIEW`.
- AR's 2020 16x texture set entered through pull requests #1809, #1811,
  #1889 and #1976, merged into the MIT repository. The v0.1.0 imports
  `machinevent.png`, `machinewarning.png` and `datastorageunit.png` were last
  changed by #1811; this chain is noted for the maintainer's G0 review.

### 3.10 LibVulpes gameplay dependencies

- AR multiblocks are built from LibVulpes blocks: the machine structure block
  (124 Java references, 40 recipes) and the advanced structure block (529 Java
  references, 3 recipes), with motor tiers (four), item and fluid hatches and
  power plugs as ports.
- AR recipes and satellites use the LibVulpes battery (two sizes), the linker
  (binds machines to rockets, pads and each other) and the holographic
  projector (shows a multiblock's missing blocks in the world).
- The modern project already replaces the structure block with the machine
  casing, the hatches and plugs with kernel ports, the projector with pattern
  diagnostics and the battery with satellite batteries; the advanced casing,
  the motors and a linker are planned (C16a, C17a); the creative input plug is
  rejected.

### 3.11 Environment rules

- `PlanetEventHandler` adds rules beyond the registry: torches placed where air
  does not allow combustion become unlit torches and listed torch blocks refuse
  placement; flint and steel and fire charges fail there; players cannot sleep
  in unbreathable air; mobs whose class is not immune to a planet's atmosphere
  do not spawn; fall distance is multiplied by the gravity at the position;
  players in the space dimension who are not near a station are moved to the
  farthest station spawn or back to the Overworld; a planet with its own ore
  table suppresses vanilla ore generation; a per-planet spawn list adds
  creatures.
- Players inside a sealed suit keep their air under water (`playerTick`
  173–175), and fire starters fail without combustion air: flint and steel,
  fire charges, blaze powder and blaze rods.
- The coremod (`asm/ClassTransformer.java`, rejected as a mechanism) has three
  live gameplay hooks: `GravityHandler.applyGravity` in entity updates (items,
  mobs, projectiles, minecarts, falling blocks and TNT follow the planet's
  gravity), `RocketInventoryHelper.allowAccess` (containers of an assembled
  rocket stay open from a seat) and `AtmosphereHandler.onBlockChange` (block
  changes invalidate sealed rooms). The modern gravity controller acts on
  players only; C18a plans living entities, and ADR-062 §4 records that
  non-living entities keep vanilla gravity.
- `AtmosphereHandler` applies atmosphere effects to every living entity unless
  the `entityAtmBypass` list exempts it. Modern life support covers players
  only; C18a plans the non-player effects with an entity tag.
- The modern project has none of the sleep, fall, spawn, torch or space-return
  rules yet; they are planned in C18a.

## 4. Legacy defects the batches must not reproduce

1. The pump's one-tick breadth-first search over a 64-block radius, which can
   touch unloaded chunks (§3.3).
2. The force field projector's placement without events or protection (§3.6).
3. The laser gun's per-player map on the item singleton and unchecked block
   targeting (§3.6).
4. Metadata-encoded variants and LibVulpes-owned identities (§3.1, ADR-061 §1).
5. Recipes keyed to item IDs instead of tags, which blocked other mods' metals
   unless `makeMaterialsForOtherMods` was set (§3.1).
6. Silent placeholder sounds registered as real events (§3.8).
